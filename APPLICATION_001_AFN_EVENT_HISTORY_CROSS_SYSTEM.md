## 1. Why M1 was selected

[Methodology 001](METHODOLOGY_001_AFN_EVENT_HISTORY.md) found an integration gap rather than a missing OEE metric or theory. Existing activity measures, persistence and shadow methods, phylogenies and exact lineages, and system-specific causal analyses provide the ingredients, but the reviewed literature does not combine them in one provenance-backed event workflow.

The evidence program supplied a demanding fixed calibration set:

- Stringmol: two accepted ordered AFN classes and several later same-class or unresolved candidates;
- Avida: exact lineage evidence, explicitly rewarded tasks, and a platform repertoire spread over incompatible experiments; and
- Geb: qualitative behavioral reconstruction, strong activity/complexity context, incomplete chronology, and a genuine later unresolved candidate.

M1 was selected to test whether the frozen workflow could represent these materially different substrates without changing the logical rules or inventing a universal phenotype metric.

## 2. Frozen methodology

No methodological element was changed during application. The required sequence remained:

\[
\text{candidate detection}
\rightarrow
\text{system-specific reconstruction}
\rightarrow
N\land F\land H\land A\land C
\rightarrow
\text{AFN event history}.
\]

The frozen meanings were:

- **N — novelty:** historically new relevant capability or relation;
- **F — function:** system-grounded causal role;
- **H — heredity:** transmission or reconstruction sufficient for selection;
- **A — adaptation:** evidence connecting the role to differential evolutionary success; and
- **C — class distinctness:** a causal role distinct from earlier accepted roles in the same continuous history.

The application retained H2 as the minimum acceptable heredity level and A2 as the minimum acceptable adaptation level. A3 strengthens a claim but is not universally required. Functional class remained a pairwise relation—`SAME`, `DISTINCT`, or `UNRESOLVED`—rather than an intrinsic node label. Counts remained descriptive, history-specific, and censored.

## 4. Cross-system logical invariants

The following remained invariant in all three applications:

1. a candidate did not become AFN without N, F, H, A, and C;
2. time was represented by intervals or composite phases when a point was not justified;
3. every accepted event required traceable provenance of at least `SUFFICIENT` quality;
4. function was reconstructed as a contextual causal role rather than inferred from genotype, program length, topology, appearance, or behavior name alone;
5. class identity was evaluated pairwise as `SAME`, `DISTINCT`, or `UNRESOLVED`;
6. multiple records of one causal transition were deduplicated;
7. independent re-origin strengthened convergence evidence but not within-history class count;
8. different experiments on one platform were not concatenated;
9. unresolved candidates remained explicit rather than being forced into positive or negative categories; and
10. every finite history was right-censored and licensed no indefinite-capacity conclusion.

These rules were sufficient for all three systems.

## 6. Event-node schema in practice

The frozen event node could be instantiated without adding a field. In the tables below:

- `H2` means clear lineage or reconstructive transmission;
- `H1` means only consistency with transmission;
- `A3` means strong causal/intervention evidence;
- `A2` means comparative or lineage support;
- `A1` means correlational support; and
- function-grounding tags use the vocabulary frozen in Methodology 001.

Each row records the required system, history, interval and boundary, pre/post contrast, novelty object, functional role and provenance, heredity, adaptation, class relation, event provenance, resolution/censoring, and final status. Detailed source claims remain in the cited evidence artifacts.

## 12. Geb unresolved-event handling

Geb demonstrates that `UNRESOLVED` is a substantive state:

- G2 has a reconstructed controller and plausible function, so discarding it as negative would lose evidence;
- G2 lacks A2 adaptive establishment and has an unresolved class edge, so accepting it would relax the protocol;
- G3 preserves later activity as a measurement-limited candidate without inferring a hidden function; and
- G4-labels is excluded from the auditable graph because publication/run/time provenance is insufficient.

Evolutionary activity, component-normalized accumulated activity, active-gene diversity, and scalability remain Layer-1/context evidence. They do not populate F, A, or C for G2 or G3. The protocol therefore handles uncertainty without a YES/NO collapse.

## 24. Strongest positive interpretation

One frozen logical workflow represented three materially different substrates without a universal phenotype metric:

- direct molecular execution and interaction relations in Stringmol;
- exact program lineage, external reward semantics, and colony organization in Avida; and
- controller-grounded behavior with incomplete chronology in Geb.

It required no logical exception, reproduced the external benchmark without joining independent experiments, kept activity/complexity out of event validation, exposed provenance quality, and preserved meaningful unresolved nodes. The method therefore improves auditability over an undifferentiated prose claim that a system “evolved novelty.”

## 25. Strongest skeptical interpretation

The common schema may be disciplined prose in tabular form. The crucial outcome still depends on human selection of the causal-role level: EQU is `SAME` if the role is logic-mediated resource acquisition and potentially `DISTINCT` if each truth-table computation is treated as a separate function. Geb's rebel is unresolved because published evidence is weak, but the class boundary is also interpretive. A sufficiently permissive analyst can describe roles broadly or narrowly while satisfying the ledger mechanically.

Moreover, agreement with prior conclusions is not an independent test because the fixed corpus and frozen class boundaries derive from the same research program. M1 therefore validates representational consistency and auditability, not inter-analyst reliability or unique classification.
