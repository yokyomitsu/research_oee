**Methodological result:**

Values such as \(C_A=10\) and \(C_B=7\) are ordinarily meaningless across unlike objects and metrics. ## 6. Triviality controls

The durable firewall is:

\[
\boxed{\text{size}\neq\text{valid complexity}}
\qquad
\boxed{\text{formal unboundedness}\neq\text{scientifically meaningful complexity OEE}.}
\]

- **Tierra** is the direct size control: genome length rises while the admitted phenotype-conditioned information measure does not show temporal growth.
- **Hintze** is the formal/proxy firewall: a long, nonsaturating compression trend and architectural extensibility do not rescue a complexity object that fails the metric-validity gate.
- **Inactive or bloated structure** receives no credit merely because it exists; the metric must justify active, fitness-informative, executed, organized, or otherwise system-relevant content.
- **Raw resource growth** changes available capacity. It counts toward I only when evolution attains higher established complexity under a declared comparable scaling protocol; it does not count as S.

**Interpretation.** Hintze is not merely a negative system. It is the adversarial control that prevents formal unboundedness, incompressibility, or accumulated size from defining the target by themselves.
