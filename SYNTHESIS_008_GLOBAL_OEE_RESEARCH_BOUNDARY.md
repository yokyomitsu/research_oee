## 9. What is established about artificial closure

### Established descriptive constraints

**Fact.** The durable constraints are:

1. Darwinian evolution alone is insufficient for OEE.
2. Evolutionary activity alone is insufficient.
3. Finite novelty, including finite repeated adaptive functional novelty, is insufficient for ongoing novelty.
4. A large formal state space or repertoire is insufficient.
5. Formal unboundedness, universality, or extensible description is insufficient without evolutionary accessibility and uptake.
6. Complexity increase, retention, or a ratchet is insufficient for indefinite complexity growth.
7. Resource scalability is distinct from within-regime temporal sustainedness and is insufficient alone.
8. One or several major transitions need not recur and do not establish a transition-renewal mechanism.
9. Broad-scope evolvability has no unconditional solution; transfer requires some shared structure, correspondence, present effect, or descendant association.
10. Fixed microphysics does not imply fixed effective organization.
11. Observer, representation, and measurement choices matter, but do not explain the entire empirical shortfall.
12. Non-observation under finite, censored histories does not establish permanent closure or impossibility.

### Candidate explanations

**Interpretation.** The repository reviewed many candidate explanatory families—ecological feedback, evolvability, resource scaling, major transitions, self-reference, autonomy, compositionality, and meta-evolution—but none survived as a sufficiently distinct, general, causally discriminable explanation of open-endedness versus closure.

### Causal mechanisms

**Fact.** No general causal mechanism for artificial closure, artificial OEE, or the difference between them has been established.

## 10. What is not established

The repository has not established any general causal relation explaining:

$$
\boxed{\text{open-endedness versus closure}.}
$$

In particular, it has not established that ecology, resource scaling, major transitions, self-reference, autopoiesis, compositionality, or meta-evolution causes OEE. It has also not established a general cause of artificial closure, a causal comparison with natural evolution, or an ontological impossibility of artificial OEE.

The following remain mandatory:

$$
\boxed{\text{not observed}\neq\text{not possible}},
\qquad
\boxed{\text{not established}\neq\text{does not occur}}.
$$

## 27. Natural-evolution comparison limit

**Fact.** Natural and cultural evolution have so far served mainly as motivation, calibration, and evidence that richer long-run evolutionary outcomes are at least plausible. They have not supplied a controlled counterfactual for artificial closure.

**Interpretation.** A causal comparison is currently unsupported because the systems, timescales, measurement opportunities, histories, and intervention structures are radically unmatched, and no controlled natural negative case exists. Broader natural-evolution study would be scientifically valuable in its own right but has **LOW immediate expected value** for the artificial-OEE mechanism gate until a specific matched causal question exists.

## 33. H2 versus empirical-boundary decision

**Decision: EMPIRICAL-BOUNDARY PAUSE WINS.**

H2 remains a legitimate unresolved hallmark, but not every unstudied hallmark warrants a program. Its potential positive cases are not known to preserve the prospectively frozen descriptive contract needed to make model escape nontrivial, its overlap risk is high, and a finite positive would not repair the current causal contrast deficit. By contrast, the empirical boundary is supported independently by AFN, complexity, exemplar comparison, and specification validation.

H2 should be reconsidered only if a materially stronger published transformational-novelty evidence base appears—one capable of prospective or independently constrained model-escape assessment and of changing the exemplar ranking.

All empirical-boundary pause criteria are satisfied: AFN requires external validation; complexity requires stronger conjunction evidence; exemplar-reporting guidance is mature; another broad search has low expected gain; mechanism contrasts are inadequate; and H2 is unlikely to alter the causal state under the evidence already mapped. Certainty is not required for this research-scale stop.

## 34. Strongest positive interpretation

## 35. Strongest skeptical interpretation

The criticism is incomplete because the repository adds a precise, locally verified map: which hallmark links succeed, which fail, which model–measure–result packages remain useful, which reporting deficits matter, and which external resources would change current conclusions. The accomplishment is refinement and research-boundary localization, not theoretical discovery.

`MET` does not mean the project discovered exemplar scarcity. It means it established a precise local research boundary strong enough to prevent premature hypothesis generation.
