# Evidence Matrix 001 — Ongoing Adaptive Functional Novelty

Research date: 2026-09-08  
Operation type: cross-system evidence localization; not Q028  
Scope: published evidence only; no model execution, experiment, mechanism search, or hypothesis generation

## 1. Why this evidence operation was selected

[Synthesis 003](SYNTHESIS_003_Q022_Q027.md) closed, decomposed, or empirically blocked the repository's current general mechanism families without explaining OEE. It diagnosed frontier-family and search-representation exhaustion rather than field exhaustion, then selected one hallmark before any further mechanism search:

> Construct a cross-system evidence matrix for ongoing adaptive functional novelty, using only independently grounded selected-effect, causal persistence/reproduction, ecological-interaction, or explicitly specified task-function claims, and record separately the origin, heritable transmission, adaptive consequence, ecological establishment, recurrence, and eventual saturation of each claimed function.

This pass conducts exactly that operation. It asks where the published evidence chain breaks, not why it breaks and not whether a system has an unobserved latent capacity.

## 2. Hallmark under study

The outer field-level target is the **ongoing generation of adaptive novelty**. The 2024 OEE Special Issue editorial treats behavioral hallmarks as more agreed than sufficient mechanisms and includes new adaptive properties and interactions, new kinds of entities, and major evolutionary transformations among possible forms of adaptive novelty. [Channon et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_e_00445)

This operation narrows that target to:

\[
\boxed{\text{ongoing adaptive functional novelty}}
\]

Here, a function is a newly arisen capability, interaction, or organization with an independently defensible causal contribution to reproduction, persistence under selection, resource acquisition, successful ecological interaction, or another system-grounded evolutionary consequence. An externally specified task can support this inference, but its externally supplied relevance must remain visible. Morphology, complexity, novelty, or evolutionary activity alone is insufficient.

## 3. Evidence decomposition

The common logical questions are:

- **N — Novelty:** Did a historically new phenotype, behavior, interaction, organization, reproductive strategy, reaction rule, or ecological relation arise?
- **F — Function:** Did that novelty make a causal contribution to an evolutionary outcome rather than merely appear as a new pattern?
- **A — Adaptation:** Is the function connected to heritable differential reproductive success or selection?
- **O — Ongoingness:** Did multiple temporally separated adaptive functional novelties arise, rather than one novelty followed by optimization, drift, or cycling?

The target evidence chain is:

\[
\boxed{N\land F\land A\land O}.
\]

This is an evidence decomposition, not a universal definition or metric of OEE. In particular:

\[
F\not\Rightarrow A,
\qquad
A\not\Rightarrow \text{new functional class},
\qquad
N\land F\land A\not\Rightarrow O.
\]

## 4. Function operationalization

Function is assigned system by system using the strongest scientifically grounded relation available:

1. direct causal contribution to copying, reproduction, survival, or resource capture;
2. an ecological or social interaction whose alteration changes those outcomes;
3. a collective organization whose removal changes group or constituent reproductive success;
4. an explicit external task whose successful execution is rewarded and inherited.

Historical novelty means only that an object or state has not previously appeared in the observed history. **Functional-class novelty** requires a new effective capability or relation, not merely a changed parameter of an already established capability. The boundary can be system-specific and graded; where the literature does not justify it, the result is `FUNCTIONAL CLASS NOVELTY NOT ESTABLISHED`.

The matrix standardizes questions, not observables:

\[
\boxed{\text{common evidence questions}\neq\text{common measurement variable}}.
\]

Stringmol reaction strategies, DISHTINY collective behaviors, Genelife connected patterns, Flow-Lenia parameter species, and Outlier causal replicators are not treated as interchangeable novelty units. Stepney and Hickinbotham's analysis is especially important here: generic evolutionary-activity measures localized interesting periods, but dedicated Stringmol measures were required to interpret the innovations. [Stepney & Hickinbotham 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00399)

## 5. Evidence-status vocabulary

Every status cell uses exactly one of:

- `DIRECTLY SUPPORTED`
- `STRONGLY SUPPORTED`
- `PARTIALLY SUPPORTED`
- `NOT ESTABLISHED`
- `EVIDENCE AGAINST`
- `NOT APPLICABLE`

`NOT ESTABLISHED` means that the cited publications do not supply the required evidence. It does not mean the system cannot produce the phenomenon. `EVIDENCE AGAINST` is reserved for a reported result that directly undermines the focal attribution, not mere non-observation.

## 6. Systems included

The six required families span different evidential positions:

- **Evoloops:** intrinsic Darwinian baseline with constructive reproduction.
- **Stringmol:** strongest candidate for evolving interaction and replication strategies under intrinsic host–parasite selection.
- **DISHTINY:** strong intervention evidence for adaptive collective functions in a substantially pre-enabled hierarchical setting.
- **Genelife:** measured genetic and spatial innovation with an author-identified functional shortfall.
- **Flow-Lenia:** continuous mass-conservative substrate with locally embedded parameters and reported evolutionary activity.
- **Outlier CA:** causal, distributed, multiscale self-replication without an established Darwinian population.

One optional contrast is included:

- **Avida:** necessary to fill the otherwise empty contrast class of strong adaptation and complex functional acquisition where the rewarded functional repertoire is explicitly enumerated. [Lenski et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568)

Tierra is not added: Stringmol already supplies the stronger intrinsic parasite/interaction case, so Tierra would broaden the catalogue without resolving a new evidence cell.

## 8. System-specific operationalizations

### Evoloops

```text

Novelty object: viable loop genotype, loop size, reproductive timing, and colony/spatial pattern
Functional object: construction and copying performance or spatial persistence under loop competition
Adaptive criterion: inherited variants systematically replace or persist against alternatives through intrinsic local reproduction
Hereditary relation: circulating construction sequence passed through parent-built offspring, including collision-generated variants
Ongoingness evidence: repeated evolutionary change is reported, but no series of distinct adaptive functional classes
Primary evidence source: Sayama 1999; Salzberg & Sayama 2004; Sayama & Nehaniv 2025
Main ambiguity: full-genotype adaptation and colony ecology are richer than size change, but published effects remain variants of reproduction/persistence rather than demonstrated new functions
```

## 9. Evoloops

**Facts.** Evoloops construct offspring through local CA interactions, transmit a circulating construction sequence, generate viable inherited variants through collisions, and undergo intrinsic differential reproduction. Smaller loops commonly spread because they reproduce faster; later analyses distinguish genotypes and colony patterns among equally small loops and report differential persistence beyond size alone. The system therefore establishes Darwinian evolution and adaptive change rather than mere replication. [Sayama 1999](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/ALIFE5-4-343.pdf), [Salzberg & Sayama 2004](https://doi.org/10.1002/cplx.20015)

**Evidence boundary.** Faster construction, smaller size, altered genotype, and colony persistence modify established reproduction and competition. No cited experiment identifies a historically new capability or interaction, verifies its adaptive contribution, and then documents additional distinct functional innovations. Hostile-environment variants externally add pathogens or clearing and measure loop-size change; those results do not show that the original system evolved a new ecological function. The 25-year review likewise identifies long-run dominance and theoretical generalizability as open issues rather than reporting ongoing AFN. [Sayama & Nehaniv 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00451)

**Classification.** Strong negative control for **adaptation without established qualitative functional novelty**. This does not show that Evoloops cannot generate such novelty.

## 10. Stringmol

**Facts.** Stringmol strings are executable molecular analogues whose sequences determine binding and reaction pathways. They copy bound strings with mutation; fitness is intrinsic to being copied rather than assigned through an external task. In spatial runs, parasites arise rapidly. Eight of twenty runs survived to two million timesteps, and the surviving ecologies evolved non-complementary binding, self-scanning, reciprocal execution constraints, replication slowing, increased effective mutation, explicit self/non-self discrimination, and sometimes short hypercycles. New parasites continually arose from mutated replicators; complex defenses could later be lost when parasites became rare and renewed when parasites returned. [Hickinbotham, Stepney & Hogeweg 2021](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.210441)

The 2024 analysis applies generic activity measures and then system-specific reaction analyses to these eight histories. It supports a range of innovative, possibly open-ended responses and explicitly argues that generic metrics are insufficient once evolution escapes their fixed behavioral model. [Stepney & Hickinbotham 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00399)

**Functional-class audit.** Replication speed and affinity changes are optimization within known capabilities. Non-complementary binding, self/non-self checks, reciprocal code execution, hypercyclic mutual replication, and evolved mutational operators are stronger candidates for new capabilities or relations. Yet the publications do not supply one event-indexed catalogue demonstrating that several of these arose at separate times, were inherited, improved reproductive persistence against contemporaneous alternatives, and constituted distinct functional classes rather than alternative implementations of parasite management. Defense loss and reappearance also make a monotonic innovation count inappropriate.

**Classification.** Strongest AFN candidate in the matrix, but not an established positive case for ongoing AFN. Its unresolved link is primarily system-specific validation of functional-class identity and temporal recurrence, not absence of novelty or selection.

## 11. DISHTINY

**Facts.** DISHTINY evolves cell-level programs in spatial resource competition with configured hereditary-group identifiers and nested levels. Published case studies identify propagule organization, gene-regulated life cycles, cell–cell messaging, resource-sharing policies, gradient-conditioned behavior, morphology, and apoptosis. Several functional claims reach causal Level 3: messaging, sharing, regulation, interior propagules, and apoptosis were altered or knocked out, and wild types outcompeted the corresponding variants. Morphological stringiness, by contrast, failed its competition test and therefore supplies novelty without demonstrated adaptation. [Moreno & Ofria 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2022.750837)

**Evidence boundary.** Endpoint strains were selected after screening replicate runs and were explicitly described as anecdotal case studies rather than an exhaustive temporal history. Multiple adaptive functions across strains are not the same as multiple temporally separated functional origins in one continuing lineage. The substrate also supplies group identifiers, recognition, resource tasks, messaging instructions, and strict levels. It does not prescribe each evolved program or collective solution, but repeated use of these channels does not establish ongoing creation of new functional classes. [Moreno & Ofria 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00284), [Q012](Q012_TRANSITION_PROPENSITY.md)

**Classification.** Strong positive case for particular adaptive collective functions; candidate AFN without established ongoingness. It also demonstrates why organizational novelty and adaptive function must be tested separately.

## 12. Genelife

**Facts.** Genelife gives live cells inherited, mutable genomes that affect local birth and survival. The authors track every new genome and connected spatial pattern and use activity statistics to identify conditions with continuing genetic and spatial innovation. Selection is mixed: local birth/survival consequences operate in the CA, while some experiments bias ancestor choice using a deliberately imposed genome-order criterion. [Packard & McCaskill 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00426)

**Evidence boundary.** The paper's own abstract says the innovation “appears to fall short of functional biological innovation.” Activity establishes appearance, persistence, and turnover of novelty, not a causal capability. No reported intervention or comparison connects a particular novel pattern to a new heritable contribution to reproductive success. The correct classification is therefore evidence against the claim that the reported innovation already constitutes functional biological innovation, not evidence that Genelife is incapable of it.

**Classification.** Strongest negative control for **novelty without established function or AFN**.

## 13. Flow-Lenia

**Facts.** Flow-Lenia is a continuous, mass-conservative CA in which rule parameters can be embedded in and transported with system mass. It supports multiple local parameter species. The published paper separately reports (a) externally optimized creatures for directed motion, rotation, obstacle navigation, and chemotaxis, and (b) intrinsic runs with local parameter mutation, mass or resource competition, species turnover, and evolutionary-activity statistics. [Plantec et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00471)

**Evidence boundary.** The optimized behaviors show that the substrate can realize functions, but those tasks were external objectives and their optimization is not the paper's intrinsic evolutionary process. In the intrinsic runs, each distinct parameter point counts as a species even if its phenotype is unchanged. The paper infers selection-like nonrandom parameter dynamics and reports possible cooperation or symbiosis, while also identifying organism, phenotype, and species definitions as limitations. It does not independently establish reproducing individuals, a trait-level hereditary relation, or a causal test of a newly evolved intrinsic function.

**Classification.** Promising substrate and evolutionary-activity case whose current evidence breaks before a securely operationalized intrinsic function. Morphological richness and externally optimized behavior cannot fill that gap.

## 14. Outlier CA

**Facts.** The Outlier is a fixed binary CA rule found during novelty search over rules, not a population of rules evolving inside the observed CA. From random sparse initial conditions it produces formations that replicate at multiple scales. The 2026 causal analysis reconstructs parent-dependent, branching lineages across generations and shows distributed multicomponent reproduction; it also identifies new c2-like replicators arising through debris and collision recombination. [Yang 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00449), [Hintze & Bohm 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-026-00074-2)

**Evidence boundary.** Causal production makes reproduction a genuine emergent function, not a visual metaphor. Different developmental paths and separately generated replicators do not, however, establish heritable phenotypic variation with differential reproductive success. The external GP search that found the CA rule must not be conflated with Darwinian evolution among Outlier replicators inside the rule.

**Classification.** Strongest negative control for **self-replication and complex organization without established adaptive evolution**; also a case of a new function without a demonstrated adaptive origin.

## 15. Optional contrast systems

### Avida — included to isolate explicit functional enumeration

Avida organisms copy inherited instruction sequences with mutation. Performing specified Boolean logic operations increases computational merit and hence reproductive rate. Lenski et al. reconstructed the evolutionary origin of the complex EQU operation, including mutations and intermediate simpler functions; EQU evolved in 23 of 50 populations in the principal environment. This supplies unusually strong evidence for heritable adaptation and a new capability relative to the lineage. [Lenski et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568)

The contrast is not that the evolved program was externally specified—it was not—but that the rewarded functional vocabulary was a finite designer-provided list. The result demonstrates adaptation and complex functional acquisition within an enumerated repertoire. It does not demonstrate continuing creation of new functional classes. Avida therefore fills a cell no required system fills as cleanly: **multiple or complex functions with explicitly external relevance and strong causal evolutionary evidence**.

No second optional system is needed.

## 17. Strongest positive candidate

\[
\boxed{\text{NO CURRENT POSITIVE CASE ESTABLISHED IN MATRIX}}
\]

**Exactly one strongest candidate: Stringmol.**

- **Directly/strongly supported:** active reproduction with mutation; intrinsic differential copying; recurrent parasite emergence; spatially sustained host–parasite ecologies; evolved changes in binding, replication timing, recognition, interaction, and mutation processes; multiple long histories analyzed with system-specific measures.
- **Inferential:** that self/non-self discrimination, reciprocal execution constraints, hypercycles, and new mutational operators constitute multiple distinct adaptive functional classes rather than different strategies within one continuing replication–parasitism relation.
- **Strongest missing link:** a temporally indexed analysis showing at least two separate innovations that are heritable, causally or comparatively improve reproductive persistence against contemporaneous alternatives, and introduce different effective capabilities or relations rather than parameter refinement, defense cycling, loss, or reacquisition.

This ranking does not declare Stringmol open-ended.

## 21. Causal evidence audit

Evidence ladder:

- **Level 0 — Appearance only:** a novel structure exists.
- **Level 1 — Correlation:** a novel trait correlates with persistence or reproduction.
- **Level 2 — Comparative evolutionary evidence:** variants bearing the trait systematically outperform alternatives under relevant conditions.
- **Level 3 — Causal/intervention evidence:** independently altering the trait or relation changes the reproductive consequence.

| System | Strongest function evidence | Strongest adaptive-function link | Principal limitation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Evoloops | Level 2 | Level 2 | Comparisons support replication/persistence effects, not a new functional class. |
| Stringmol | Level 2 | Level 2 | Reaction reconstruction and cross-run convergence are strong; trait-specific removal/competition across distinct innovation events is incomplete. |
| DISHTINY | Level 3 | Level 3 | Knockouts and competitions validate selected endpoint functions; temporal recurrence is not tested. |
| Genelife | Level 1 | Level 1 | Activity/persistence does not identify a trait's causal function. |
| Flow-Lenia | Level 1 intrinsic; Level 2 external | Level 1 intrinsic | External optimized tasks and intrinsic evolution are separate evidence streams. |
| Outlier CA | Level 3 for reproduction | Level 0 for adaptation | Causal tracing proves reproduction; no selected heritable variant is identified. |
| Avida | Level 3 | Level 3 | Strong within an explicit task ontology; does not establish unenumerated function classes. |

Level 3 is not required to recognize historical digital adaptation. The table shows why a high causal level for one layer cannot be borrowed by another.

| DISHTINY | multiple adaptive endpoint phenotypes sampled from treatments and replicates | Plural functions, not a lineage-ordered series of new functions. |
| Genelife | continuing activity of new genomes and connected patterns | Ongoing novelty, not ongoing adaptive function. |
| Flow-Lenia | long intrinsic parameter-species turnover and diversity/activity dynamics | Ongoing evolutionary activity, not event-resolved AFN. |
| Outlier CA | branching replication across generations and multiple developmental paths | Ongoing reproduction, not adaptive innovation. |
| Avida | accumulation of simpler rewarded functions along paths to EQU | One bounded complex-function origin with useful intermediates, not open-ended recurrence. |

No equal spacing or infinite observation is required. The failure is more basic: no publication establishes multiple temporally separated events satisfying N, F, and A as distinct functions.

## 23. Cross-system bottleneck

The most consistent **terminal evidential break** is:

\[
\boxed{\text{qualitative adaptive functional novelty}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{multiple temporally separated AFN episodes}}.
\]

`Multiple AFN episodes` is not strongly or directly supported for any system. Stringmol and Avida receive partial support for different reasons: Stringmol has intrinsically grounded strategy succession but unresolved functional-class identity; Avida has a reconstructed sequence of functional acquisition but an enumerated task ontology and no evidence of continuing class creation.

This terminal regularity is **not yet a common causal failure**. It partly follows from selecting an ongoing hallmark and from finite, nonuniform publications. The proximate evidence breaks occur earlier and differ by system:

- Outlier: heredity/selection link;
- Genelife: novelty-to-function link;
- Flow-Lenia: entity/heredity and intrinsic function-to-adaptation links;
- Evoloops: adaptation-to-qualitative-function link;
- DISHTINY: function plurality-to-temporal ongoingness link under strong pre-enablement;
- Stringmol: functional-class validation and event-based ongoingness;
- Avida: enumerated-function acquisition-to-unenumerated ongoingness.

The evidence matrix therefore does not justify a targeted common-bottleneck mechanism program.

## 24. Heterogeneity audit

The systems fail differently in scientifically consequential ways:

1. **Replication can precede Darwinian evolution:** Outlier.
2. **Measured novelty can precede function:** Genelife.
3. **Intrinsic evolution can be reported before organisms and functions are adequately identified:** Flow-Lenia.
4. **Adaptation can optimize established reproductive performance without a new functional class:** Evoloops.
5. **Causally validated functions can be plural without being a recurrent temporal sequence:** DISHTINY.
6. **Intrinsic arms races can generate candidate functional innovations whose classes require system-specific adjudication:** Stringmol.
7. **Strong adaptation can produce complex new lineage capabilities inside a fixed external function vocabulary:** Avida.

Natural calibration does not make the test absurd. Antibiotic resistance through a new degradation enzyme would satisfy N/F/A with comparative or intervention evidence; the repeated origin of distinct feeding, defense, or symbiotic capabilities along a documented lineage would add O. By contrast, a continuously changing neutral sequence or repeated optimization of enzyme affinity would not. These are calibration examples only, not matrix rows.

## 25. Evidence versus capacity

The matrix licenses only claims of the form:

\[
\boxed{\text{system }X\text{ has not demonstrated evidence layer }Y
\text{ in the cited publications}.}
\]

It does not license:

\[
\boxed{\text{system }X\text{ cannot generate }Y.}
\]

Specific limits must be kept separate:

- **Phenomenon evidence absent:** no functional or adaptive relation was reported.
- **Validation limit:** a plausible function is reported without the comparison, lineage, or intervention needed to link it to adaptation.
- **Measurement limit:** a generic metric detects evolutionary activity but cannot characterize a new system-specific function.
- **Representation limit:** a function vocabulary is externally enumerated or a species definition counts parameter differences without phenotypic equivalence.

Stringmol is primarily a measurement/validation limit at the final two layers. Flow-Lenia combines representation, measurement, and validation limits. Genelife's explicit shortfall is a phenomenon-level statement about the reported innovation, not a proof of incapacity. Outlier lacks evidence for a Darwinian population despite exceptionally strong reproduction evidence.

## 26. Hallmark-decomposition decision

\[
\boxed{\textbf{B — HETEROGENEOUS BOTTLENECKS}}
\]

Different systems fail at different stages of the N/F/A/O chain. The universal absence of strongly demonstrated O is a terminal evidence gap, but it does not isolate one shared causal bottleneck: in several rows N, F, or A is already unresolved, while the two closest cases fail for different reasons. The matrix remains scientifically meaningful, so E is rejected. Evidence is sufficient to localize the links, so C is too strong. No system supports D. A would mistake a common endpoint non-demonstration for a common mechanism failure.

This result implies that “adaptive functional novelty” should retain its decomposed evidence layers. It does not yet require subdivision into new causal hallmark kinds.

## 27. Research-scale decision

\[
\boxed{\textbf{T2 — SYSTEM-SPECIFIC VALIDATION}}
\]

A targeted mechanism program is not justified because the proximate bottlenecks are heterogeneous. Generic functional-novelty formalization is not the dominant obstacle: system-specific function assignments can be made, though sometimes only provisionally. The highest information gain comes from the strongest near-positive case, where one decisive analysis can discriminate repeated adaptive functional innovation from refinement or cycling.

This decision does not conduct that validation and does not create a hypothesis.

## 28. Highest-information next research operation

> **Apply an event-indexed validation protocol to the existing published Stringmol histories: determine whether at least two temporally separated claimed innovations each have a lineage-supported hereditary basis and comparative reproductive advantage, and whether the later event adds a new effective interaction capability rather than optimizing, losing, reacquiring, or cycling an established host–parasite function.**

This is exactly one proposed next operation. It is not performed here.

## 29. Stop condition

The Stringmol validation branch must stop and consolidate when the first of these occurs:

1. at least two temporally separated events satisfy N, F, and A under explicit system-specific criteria and survive the refinement/cycling counteranalysis;
2. the strongest events reduce to optimization, loss/reacquisition, or strategies within one established interaction function;
3. published histories lack the lineage, reaction, or competition information needed for the decision, making the branch empirically/measurement blocked;
4. functional-class assignments remain underdetermined after reaction-level causal roles are specified, requiring a separate formalization or measurement operation.

No mechanism search, Q028, implementation, simulation, experiment, model modification, or hypothesis follows in this pass.
