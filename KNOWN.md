## K001 — Conceptual boundaries

Terminology varies historically. Here, **replication** means copying; **self-replication** means producing copies through the entity's own activity in its medium; **self-reproduction** additionally permits inherited variation, following Sayama and Nehaniv's convention. Older papers also call exact copying “self-reproduction.” Heritable variation means descendants transmit differences, not merely that an offspring looks different. Darwinian evolution requires population change involving heredity, variation, and differential reproductive success; self-reproduction alone is insufficient. The review distinguishes these explicitly. [Sayama & Nehaniv, §§2.5–3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.03961v2)

## K002 — What original Evoloops established

Evoloops construct offspring and transmit circulating construction signals under deterministic, local, nine-state CA rules. Collisions can alter developing bodies and the genetic sequences passed onward; some variants reproduce across generations. Dissolution frees occupied space; increased robustness allows some altered offspring to remain fertile. Differential reproductive success arises from spatial interactions without an assigned fitness score, external mutation operator, or organism-level selection controller. This constructively demonstrates Darwinian evolution with intrinsic selection. The rules and ancestor were designed: the experiment did not demonstrate their spontaneous origin. Much reproductive machinery resides in the rules, rather than being genetically described. [Sayama 1999, §§3–4](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/ALIFE5-4-343.pdf)

## K003 — Original convergence was a scoped observation

In the principal experiments, smaller loops' faster reproduction favored replacement toward size 4. Other initial conditions yielded larger variants, persistent reproduction without detected size evolution, or fixed points/short cycles. Original “species” labels counted growth signals; they did not resolve full genotypes. Consequently, size convergence is not evidence that all genetic evolution stopped. [Sayama 1999, §§3.4–3.9, Figs. 9–13](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/ALIFE5-4-343.pdf)

## K004 — Later work corrected the size-only account

Detailed genetic identification revealed different colony patterns, competitive persistence, and mutational tendencies among equal-sized loops. Selection improved colony density and competitive persistence even at equal reproduction time. Thus, adaptation continued within the smallest size class; loop size alone does not determine fitness. These findings used the Evoloop rules, without requiring a new reproductive system. [Salzberg & Sayama 2004, *Complexity* 10(2), 33–39, Figs. 2–3](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/Complexity-preprint.pdf)

## K005 — Accessibility and ancestry constrain trajectories

Researchers observed conserved subsequences in viable reproductive transitions and used them to choose ancestors with larger minimum descendant sizes. This weakened size-based fitness differences and prolonged exploration. The conference report's example exceeded six million updates before extinction. Conservation was an empirical finding explicitly lacking a rigorous explanation; an exhaustive partition theorem for every possible interaction was not established. Designed starting genomes must be distinguished from evolution discovering those genomes. [Salzberg, Antony & Sayama 2004, *Complex Genetic Evolution of Self-Replicating Loops*, pp. 5–6 of PDF, Figs. 8–9](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/alife9-evoloop.pdf)

## K006 — Environmental disturbance changed the balance of forces

Hostile-environment experiments used an introduced pathogen (additional state/counters) or externally imposed periodic clearing. Under some conditions, reduced direct competition allowed biased mutation toward larger loops to shape population change. Their “complexity” measure was genome length/loop size; growth could oppose the prevailing selection pressure. The demonstrated increase therefore cannot be equated with adaptive functional innovation. These are modified environments, not evidence that original Evoloops evolved a pathogen or new ecological role. [Salzberg, Antony & Sayama 2004, *BioSystems* 78, 119–134, §§2.2–4; author manuscript dated 2005](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/BioSystems-preprint.pdf)

## K007 — What the OEE comparison establishes

OEE has multiple definitions. Taylor distinguishes ongoing adaptive exploration from expansive and transformational novelty, and effective from strictly unlimited open-endedness. **Diversity** counts differences; **novelty** requires something new relative to a specified history/description; **adaptive novelty** additionally requires adaptive significance. Neither a large repertoire nor repeated turnover establishes ongoing adaptive novelty. Genome length is not a general measure of functional complexity. Fixed microscopic laws must also be distinguished from changing biotic context and effective fitness. [Taylor 2019, §§2–4](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/taylor2019evolutionary.preprint.pdf)

The Evoloop findings above establish evolution and bounded observations of adaptation/exploration, not a demonstrated capacity for indefinitely sustained innovation. The later review still identifies eventual dominance and the lack of general principles preventing stagnation as open problems. Its caution about dynamic environments failing over longer periods cites **Swarm Chemistry**, not an Evoloop-specific impossibility proof. [Sayama & Nehaniv, §4; 2024 preprint, published 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.03961v2)

## K008 — A mathematical bound is not a convergence mechanism

**Fact / deduction:** A deterministic, autonomous CA on a fixed finite grid of N nine-state cells has at most 9^N configurations. Repeating a configuration repeats its future, so every trajectory eventually becomes periodic. This excludes endlessly distinct global configurations under those assumptions. It neither explains rapid biological-level stagnation nor bounds transients usefully; it does not apply unchanged to an infinite grid or unmodelled external forcing. It also does not settle effective OEE, which distinguishes practical novelty production from mathematical infinity. [Definition boundary: Taylor 2019, §2](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/taylor2019evolutionary.preprint.pdf)

## K009 — OEE definitions distinguish different targets

The York workshop explicitly endorses pluralism about OEE, distinguishing novelty/adaptation, new evolutionary entities, major transitions, and evolution of evolvability. Complexity growth is required by some definitions, not all. [Taylor et al. 2016, §3](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210)

## K010 — Bounded adaptive activity is not inactivity

Bedau et al.'s original class 2 has positive new adaptive activity with bounded diversity and mean cumulative activity; Echo exhibited this class. Their neutral shadow generates new genotype identities without genotype-specific adaptive significance. Activity statistics are evaluated against that control, not raw change alone. [Bedau, Snyder & Packard 1998, classification and results](https://sfi-edu.s3.amazonaws.com/sfi-edu/production/uploads/sfi-com/dev/uploads/filer/b3/e9/b3e9317b-5798-485b-8bb1-3465cd336e0e/98-03-025.pdf)

## K011 — Change, novelty, complexity, and ecology are separate measurements

MODES separates these four dimensions. Change can count returning components; novelty excludes previously recorded qualifying components. Stabilizing selection can inflate cumulative activity without innovation. Persistence does not perfectly exclude drift. NK fitness sharing maintained change and ecological diversity without increased final novelty. [Dolson et al. 2019, §§2–3, 5](https://cse.msu.edu/~dolsonem/pdfs/modes_paper.pdf)

## K012 — Novelty classes are model-relative

Banzhaf et al. reserve open-ended events for model/meta-model changes; Taylor additionally recognizes effective exploratory OEE within a fixed model. Their reference baselines differ: current versus initial model. Taylor explicitly distinguishes expansion of a descriptive model from expansion of actual physical state space. [Banzhaf et al. 2016, novelty definitions](https://www.cs.mun.ca/~banzhaf/papers/OEE_2016.pdf), [Taylor 2019, §§2, 4.3.1](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/taylor2019evolutionary.preprint.pdf)

## K013 — Formal dynamical open-endedness has a restricted meaning

Adams et al. define unbounded evolution through exceeding an equivalent isolated subsystem's recurrence horizon, and innovation through trajectories absent from its isolated counterfactuals. Their dynamical framework does not require Darwinian heredity/selection or increasing state complexity. [Adams et al. 2017, definitions 1–2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-00810-8)

## K014 — Complexity requirements concern specified objects

Corominas-Murtra et al.'s general postulates concern normalized evolutionary-history complexity, unboundedness, and a separate heredity principle. They permit decreases in individual-state complexity; their stronger version constrains state complexity. This is a stipulated formal account, not evidence that genome length or mean organism complexity must continually rise. [Corominas-Murtra, Seoane & Solé 2018, §§3–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6303796/)

## K015 — Fitness plateaus do not establish exhausted potential

Wiser observed long fitness gains in fixed Avida task environments. Later continuations exceeded fitted earlier asymptotes. Finite fitness trajectories therefore can mislead about boundedness; an unbounded fit likewise does not prove infinite adaptation or new functional domains. [Wiser 2015, chapter 4](https://d.lib.msu.edu/etd/3694), [Wiser et al. 2018, preprint](https://peerj.com/preprints/27246.pdf)

## K016 — Current phenotype does not determine evolutionary potential

Neutral-network analysis distinguishes a genotype's local alternatives from alternatives accessible across a phenotype's network. Cryptic RNA variation also experimentally altered subsequent adaptation under a changed substrate condition while preserving the original measured phenotype. Potential is conditional on variation mechanisms and environment; present phenotypic equivalence is insufficient. [Wagner 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2562401/), [Hayden et al. 2011](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature10083)

## K017 — Arrival, benefit, and establishment differ

Genotype–phenotype bias can favor frequently generated phenotypes over rarer, fitter alternatives. In LTEE reconstructions, citrate-use activation could provide a small ancestral benefit; its establishment also depended on genetic background and competition. Engineered expression does not itself measure spontaneous arrival probability. [Schaper & Louis 2014](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0086635), [Leon et al. 2018](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1007348)

## K018 — Reachability claims require specified dynamics and targets

Probabilistic verification separates zero/positive reachability, hitting probabilities, and finite-horizon/path properties. **Deduction:** finite non-detection alone generally cannot distinguish impossibility from sufficiently rare arrival. A complete transition graph or invariant can establish exclusion of a specified target; observation without such constraints cannot supply that proof. This is target-relative exclusion, not a universal OEE diagnosis. [Forejt et al. 2011, §§4–6](https://www.prismmodelchecker.org/papers/sfm11.pdf)

## K019 — Known rules need not make eventual occurrence decidable

For Day's computable unbounded evolutionary formalization, deciding which states ever occur is equivalent to existence of a computable progressive recoding. This formal progression need not be fitness or complexity growth. The result is not a prohibition on specific predictions or a general undecidability result for finite state spaces. [Day 2012](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.2982)

## K020 — Predictive and behavioral equivalences retain specified outcomes

Causal states group histories by conditional future-observation distributions, with minimality/uniqueness relative to the specified process. Probabilistic bisimulation matches outputs/rewards and transition probabilities into equivalence blocks. These provide constrained state reductions, not an automatic choice of evolutionary significance. Shalizi and Moore explicitly retain an initial choice of observables. [Shalizi & Crutchfield 2001](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9907176), [Givan et al. 2003](https://cs.brown.edu/people/tdean/publications/archive/GivanetalAIJ-03.pdf), [Shalizi & Moore 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0303625)

## K021 — Endogenous significance has prior art with explicit conditions

Intrinsic emergence already connects emergent structures to their use within the system. Semantic information operationalizes causal necessity for viability under interventions, explicitly allowing choices of viability function, boundary, horizon, and interventions. Functional information instead quantifies configuration rarity for a specified function threshold. These are distinct formulations, not an established intrinsic OEE criterion. [Crutchfield 2001](https://csc.ucdavis.edu/~cmg/papers/EvRevInno.pdf), [Kolchinsky & Wolpert 2018](https://artemyk.github.io/assets/pdf/papers/Kolchinsky_Wolpert_2018_Semantic%20information,%20autonomous%20agency%20and%20non-equilibrium%20statistical%20physics.pdf), [Hazen et al. 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1876432/)

## K022 — Successful descendants do not require fixation of every intermediate

Stochastic tunneling permits an advantageous descendant through a neutral or deleterious intermediate without that intermediate fixing. Thus intermediate fixation cannot be imposed as a general condition of evolutionary accessibility. [Iwasa, Michor & Nowak 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1470783/)

## K023 — Organizational closure concerns maintenance dependencies

Closure of constraints formalizes mutual maintenance across specified timescales. Its boundary analysis permits partial descriptions and hierarchical organizations. This is distinct from evolutionary-possibility closure. [Montévil & Mossio 2015, §§3–6](https://montevil.org/publications/articles/2015-mm-organisation-closure-constraints/)

## K024 — Self-production and regulation ground different claims

Di Paolo adds adaptivity to autopoiesis to account for distinguishing and regulating viability-threatening tendencies. Bich's regulated-closure account distinguishes establishing/serving goals from actively pursuing them through coordinated control. Neither definition by itself establishes evolutionary creation of new functional kinds. [Di Paolo 2005](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11097-005-9002-y), [Bich 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/rati.12405)

## K025 — Changing biological norms and organizations have direct prior art

Di Paolo explicitly allows time-varying viability sets. Montévil et al. discuss integration of new organizational constraints; Tahar explicitly theorizes historically changing evolutionary norms. These are published theoretical accounts, not general proofs of norm generation or OEE. [Di Paolo, *Overcoming Autopoiesis*](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/dipaolo-overcoming-autopoiesis.pdf), [Montévil et al. 2016, §4](https://montevil.org/publications/articles/2016-mmp-theoretical-principles-variation/), [Tahar 2022](https://philpapers.org/archive/TAHBCA.pdf)

## K026 — A new obligatory component need not supply a new function

Ancestral reconstruction and genetic manipulation support evolution of the fungal V-ATPase ring from two paralogous components to three through duplication and complementary interface losses. Both daughter components became obligatory, without apparent acquisition of novel function. Increased dependence therefore does not by itself establish a newly enabled functional capacity. [Finnigan et al. 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3979732/)

## K027 — Semantic approaches distinguish grounding from metric choice

Kolchinsky and Wolpert propose optimizing semantic measures over decompositions and timescales, while retaining a specified viability measure. Sowinski et al.'s fixed forager grounds failure in resource depletion but chooses expected lifetime as the viability statistic. These accounts do not demonstrate evolution of the viability criterion. [Kolchinsky & Wolpert 2018, §VI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.08053), [Sowinski et al. 2023](https://artemyk.github.io/assets/pdf/papers/Sowinski-SemanticInformationInAModelOfResourceGatheringAgents.pdf)

## K028 — Objectivity, normativity, and changing purposiveness remain disputed

Cusimano and Sterner challenge constraint individuation under redescription; Corti challenges the inference from conditions of existence to norms. Cuciniello distinguishes maintenance and regulative accounts and explicitly questions available-function switching versus new functions. These establish active criticism and prior art, not a settled refutation of organizational accounts. [Cusimano & Sterner 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10441-019-09365-9), [Corti 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-023-04255-7), [Cuciniello 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40656-026-00731-8)

## K029 — Organizational function is not universal indispensability

The organizational function account distinguishes contributions required in one self-maintenance regime from components indispensable across every regime of the relevant system class. [Mossio et al. 2009, §5.1](https://www.ias-research.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Mossio_25592036.pdf)

## K030 — Constructive Neutral Evolution can increase dependency without increased function

Constructive Neutral Evolution (CNE) models complexity growth through effectively neutral, buffered changes that establish new interdependencies; purifying selection then maintains the dependency. The higher-level function can remain relatively unaltered while reversibility decreases in practice because returning to independence becomes increasingly unlikely. CNE therefore supplies a strong negative case against inferring functional novelty or future generativity from dependency/complexity accumulation. [Stoltzfus 1999](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00006540), [Muñoz-Gómez et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00239-021-09996-y)

## K031 — Generative entrenchment means downstream dependency, not future evolutionary openness

Wimsatt and Schank define generative entrenchment by the number of downstream features depending on an entity. Its “generative” sense concerns developmental production/dependence and predicts developmental constraint or lock; it does not by itself mean that the entity increases the set of future evolutionary possibilities. [Wimsatt & Schank 1986](https://doi.org/10.1086/psaprocbienmeetp.1986.2.192789)

## K032 — Combinatorial entrenchment is prior art for constraint enabling combination

Wimsatt uses combinatorial entrenchment for standardized components that function as a constructional alphabet for many adaptive devices or forms. This explicitly captures constraint at one level enabling combinatorial freedom at another, but it presupposes a component/construction space and does not by itself establish heritability, nontrivial future capacity, or open-endedness. [Wimsatt 2013](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/9780262019552.003.0004)

## K033 — Developmental bias and evolvability concern conditional access to heritable variation

Developmental bias describes nonuniform production of phenotypic variants; genotype–phenotype maps, neutral networks, robustness, facilitated variation, and modularity describe how developmental organization changes variation, accessibility, and pleiotropic interference. These theories distinguish alteration of variational distributions from the independent question of whether a new character, function, or future-capacity class exists. [Uller et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1534/genetics.118.301760), [Wagner & Altenberg 1996](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1996.tb02339.x)

## K034 — Trait evolvability and innovative evolvability are distinct

Wagner distinguishes evolvability as heritable variation in an existing activity from the stronger ability to acquire an innovative property such as a new catalytic activity. A system can vary and adapt an existing trait without being capable of generating a new functional kind on the relevant timescale. [Wagner 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2005.01.063)

## K035 — Novelty, character identity, function, and innovation are separable

Müller and Wagner distinguish new individuated characters from modification of existing characters; Wagner grounds character identity in historical continuity of developmental regulatory networks. Erwin distinguishes novel attributes, new functions, ecological exploitation, and innovation, and describes potentiation, novelty formation, refinement, and ecological establishment as separable stages. A new character or function therefore does not automatically constitute future generativity. [Müller & Wagner 1991](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.es.22.110191.001305), [Wagner 2014](https://doi.org/10.23943/princeton/9780691156460.001.0001), [Erwin 2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/brv.12643)

## K036 — Potentiation is target-relative historical access, not general evolvability

In the LTEE citrate case, earlier mutations increased the later tendency to evolve aerobic citrate utilization without producing the full phenotype; replay experiments established a conditional historical effect. Potentiation is therefore distinguishable from generic contingency when a prior state changes access to a specified later target, but it remains target-, dynamics-, and horizon-relative and is not equivalent to general future possibility expansion. [Blount, Borland & Lenski 2008](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0803151105)

## K037 — Exaptation separates reuse from historical origin

Exaptation distinguishes a current use or fitness contribution from the historical process that produced a structure. Reuse of an old structure can support a new function and later refinement, but exaptation alone does not imply a new character identity, a reusable primitive, or expansion of future evolutionary possibilities. [Gould & Vrba 1982](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0094837300004310)

## K038 — No unified criterion was found for new heritable future capacity

Existing theories provide partial criteria for dependency, downstream entrenchment, combinatorial reuse, variational bias, character identity, target potentiation, and exaptation. The reviewed literature does not provide one accepted, representation-robust criterion that identifies a nontrivial increase in heritable future trajectories while excluding dependency, one-off reuse, observer-selected tasks, and inert differences. This is a bounded literature finding, not an impossibility theorem. [Q005](Q005_ENTRENCHMENT_VS_CAPACITY.md)

## K039 — Adjacent-possible expansion is established prior art

Kauffman’s adjacent possible describes possibilities available from the current actual through a specified one-step process; realizing one changes the actual and therefore the next adjacent possible. Urn-with-triggering and related models formally implement expanding possibilities. Expansion of accessible outcomes is therefore not a new workspace concept. [Kauffman 2000, summarized by Griesemer 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/phisci/pyad018), [Loreto et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0179303)

## K040 — OEE theory already distinguishes exploration from door-opening states

Taylor distinguishes ongoing exploratory open-endedness from expansive/transformational forms and describes states that open an expanded phenotype space. The same framework discusses evolving genetic operators, evolving genotype–phenotype maps, non-additive compositional systems, and transdomain bridges. The workspace transitions \(\Omega_t\to\Omega_{t+1}\) and \(\mathcal O_t\to\mathcal O_{t+1}\) substantially overlap these established formulations. [Taylor 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01883), [Banzhaf et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_a_00210)

## K041 — Changed generative map is prior art under evolution of evolvability

Evolvability research distinguishes accessible heritable outcomes from changes in the mechanisms producing them. Mutation/recombination modifiers, evolving genetic operators, gene-regulatory networks, developmental systems, and genotype–phenotype maps can alter the distribution or accessibility of future variation. Such second-order changes are established as evolution of evolvability or representation, but they do not necessarily add a qualitatively new phenotype dimension. [Wagner & Altenberg 1996](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1996.tb02339.x), [Crombach & Hogeweg 2008](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000112)

## K042 — Reuse and compositional construction are established mechanisms

Developmental modules, conserved regulatory/toolkit elements, protein domains, gene duplication, exaptation, and combinatorial entrenchment provide prior art for reuse across contexts. Taylor explicitly identifies non-additive compositional systems as a route to expansive or transformational OEE. Reuse or combinatoriality does not by itself establish heritable open-endedness: outcomes may be nonviable, unreachable, observer-defined, or terminal. [Gerhart & Kirschner 1997](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.94.16.8420), [Wimsatt 2013](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/9780262019552.003.0004), [Taylor 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01883)

## K043 — Major transitions can change the evolutionary process itself

Major-transition theory already treats new levels of individuality, inheritance channels, and changes in storage/transmission of heritable information as transformations of what selection can act upon. These are recognized process-level changes, stronger than simple phenotype expansion, but they are not a new “generator” category. [Maynard Smith & Szathmáry 1995](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-major-transitions-in-evolution-9780198502944), [Szathmáry 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421398112), [Jablonka & Lamb 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2005.08.038)

## K044 — Expanding adjacent possible does not imply changing its generator

Adjacent-possible models can keep adding or exposing new outcomes under a fixed triggering or construction rule. Likewise, a fixed grammar or compositional system can produce a growing set of combinations without evolution of the generative mechanism. Therefore expansion of the adjacent possible does not by itself establish recursive evolution of generators. [Loreto et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0179303), [Taylor 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01883)

## K045 — Recursive evolutionary innovation has conceptual prior art

Frank describes evolutionary innovation as a hierarchy of recursive processes in which innovations can alter the processes generating and transmitting later innovations. This is close prior art for one-shot versus recursive generativity, though it is not a generally accepted observer-independent operational criterion. [Frank 2016](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.9568.1)

## K046 — No unified criterion was found for reusable or recursive generators

Existing theories distinguish accessible outcome expansion, changed variation/development/inheritance maps, reuse, compositionality, downstream entrenchment, and process-level major transitions. The reviewed literature does not supply one criterion that is simultaneously heritable, causal, prospective, representation-robust, and independent of a prespecified future-function catalogue. The remaining gap is a bounded synthesis problem, not evidence for a new “generator” hypothesis. [Q006](Q006_GENERATIVE_CAPACITY.md)

## K047 — Ongoing Type 2 is a hallmark, not a one-off event

OEE literature explicitly separates the ongoing evolution of evolvability from a single increase in evolvability. Taylor et al. state that one instance of evolvability evolution falls short of genuinely open-ended evolution. The revised Tokyo categories designate ongoing evolution of evolvability as Type 2 OEE, although earlier York-based presentations used a different numbering. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Packard et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00291), [Channon 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00430)

## K048 — The meta-evolution regress is recognized

Meta-genetic programming explicitly recognizes that evolved genetic operators themselves require operators for their evolution. Proposed responses include recursive operator application and self-application. Autoconstructive evolution embeds reproduction and variation in evolving individuals, but retains a specified programming language, interpreter, and evolutionary substrate. This establishes a finite response pattern, not a proof that recursive closure sustains ongoing Type 2 OEE. [Edmonds 2001](https://journals.tubitak.gov.tr/elektrik/vol9/iss1/2/), [Spector 2001](https://faculty.hampshire.edu/lspector/pubs/ace.pdf)

## K049 — Evolved open-endedness does not solve repeated closure

Pattee and Sayama define evolved open-endedness as open-endedness and its mechanisms becoming outcomes of evolution. They identify evolved symbolic control and higher organizational levels as historical mechanisms, and call for taxonomy, measurement, and selection studies. They do not provide a necessary-and-sufficient criterion explaining why increased open-endedness must continue rather than stabilize or exhaust its repertoire. [Pattee & Sayama 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00276)

## K050 — Self-reference is neither sufficient nor necessary under one formal OEE framework

State-dependent cellular automata can use the current state and rule to update the next rule, and Adams et al. report this as a scalable mechanism for their formal unbounded-evolution and innovation criteria. The same framework notes that closed self-referential systems need not be unbounded, while non-self-referential external rule modulation and stochastic rule mutation can also produce the formal conditions. Their result concerns dynamical novelty, not repeated Darwinian evolution of new classes of evolutionary operators. [Pavlic et al. 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.4070), [Adams et al. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-00810-8)

## K051 — Fixed microphysics can support changing effective evolutionary organization

Biological theory permits fixed physical laws with changing effective evolutionary mechanisms: conserved cellular/molecular processes can be redeployed by evolved regulation and development, while major transitions can establish new selectable wholes or inheritance channels. This supports the distinction between fixed microphysics and changing (E_t), but does not by itself establish an indefinitely extensible sequence of such changes. [Gerhart & Kirschner 1998](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC33871/), [Szathmáry 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421398112), [Q007](Q007_META_EVOLUTION_CLOSURE.md)

## K052 — Environmental openness can produce subsystem unboundedness while relocating the question

In Adams et al.'s formalism, unbounded evolution and innovation are defined for a subsystem coupled to an environment and compared with an isolated counterfactual. Environmental perturbation can prevent subsystem recurrence, but it can also inject the effective unboundedness or move the open-endedness question to the larger coupled system. It is not by itself an explanation of endogenous Type 2 OEE. [Adams et al. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-00810-8)

## K053 — Coevolution promotes evolvability without guaranteeing continuation

Host–parasite coevolution in Avida produced more complex traits and favored more evolvable hosts. Coevolutionary models also admit extinction, stable coexistence with constant phenotypes, or cyclic Red Queen dynamics depending on parameters. Reciprocal modification therefore supplies moving selection pressures but is not a general anti-closure principle. [Zaman et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002023), [Marrow, Law & Cooper 1995](https://doi.org/10.1006/jtbi.1995.0179)

## K054 — Major transitions are mechanisms and hallmarks, not an infinite hierarchy theorem

Major-transition theory formalizes historical changes in individuality, organization, and inheritance, and OEE literature treats ongoing major transitions as a powerful form of open-endedness. But the York report notes that major transitions are contingent and that there may be no further transitions; finite resources also limit successive transitions. One or several transitions therefore do not entail an indefinitely rising meta-level. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Szathmáry 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421398112)

## K055 — No general Type 2 mechanism or test is established

OEE literature explicitly distinguishes behavioral hallmarks from hypothesized mechanisms and reports less agreement about mechanisms than hallmarks. The 2024 testing procedure targets Tokyo Type 1 and describes Type 2 as ongoing evolution of evolvability, but it does not supply a necessary-and-sufficient test for Type 2. The current boundary is an underexplored causal assay, not a justified new mechanism. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Channon 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00430)

## K056 — “New effective operator” remains representation-dependent

Autoconstructive evolution, evolving GP maps/operators, symbolic control, and major transitions all provide recognized cases in which effective evolutionary organization changes while lower-level rules remain fixed. A fixed language, grammar, interpreter, or constructional substrate can also generate many effective procedures without changing its meta-rule. No accepted observer-independent equivalence criterion currently decides when a procedure is a genuinely new evolutionary operator rather than a new output of an old one. [Edmonds 2001](https://journals.tubitak.gov.tr/elektrik/vol9/iss1/2/), [Adams et al. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-00810-8), [Q007](Q007_META_EVOLUTION_CLOSURE.md)

## K057 — A fixed universal mechanism need not have a finite effective repertoire

Universal interpreters, constructors, and physical substrates can use extensible descriptions or configurations to realize an unbounded class of effective structures or transformations without changing the underlying mechanism. Therefore fixed meta-rule does not imply closure or a finite future catalogue. [Salo & Törmä 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.03988), [Hutton 2002](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~turk/bio_sim/articles/hutton_rep_molecules.pdf)

## K058 — Universality establishes capacity, not evolutionary accessibility

Computational universality, physical universality, and von Neumann-style universal construction establish conditional realization of suitable descriptions, programs, or transformations. They do not establish that mutation, development, reproduction, selection, and ecology can discover and maintain those descriptions through viable intermediate histories. Constructor-theoretic possible transformations likewise do not supply a lineage-level path or probability. [Deutsch 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.7439), [Salo & Törmä 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.03988), [Q008](Q008_UNIVERSALITY_VS_ACCESSIBILITY.md)

## K059 — Evolutionary accessibility formalizes only part of the capacity-to-history gap

Fitness-landscape and genotype-network theory quantify accessible paths, path probabilities, ruggedness, sign epistasis, neutral networks, robustness, and valley crossing. These show that only a small fraction of theoretically possible trajectories may be accessible in particular landscapes. They do not yet provide a general criterion for repeated endogenous reorganization—including gains and losses—of accessible classes while the map, inheritance system, and selectable units change. [Lobkovsky, Wolf & Koonin 2011](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002302), [Q011](Q011_EVOLUTIONARY_REGIME_BRIDGING.md)

## K060 — Indefinite scalability is enabling infrastructure, not an OEE mechanism

Ackley’s indefinite scalability addresses the absence of a hard architectural ceiling as resources increase. A scalable substrate can remain evolutionarily simple, and scalability does not establish that evolution discovers or exploits larger organizations. It is therefore a possible prerequisite or test dimension, not a sufficient OEE condition. [Ackley 2016](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v30i1.9802)

## K061 — Extensible descriptions are enabling but not sufficient

Variable-length or otherwise extensible hereditary descriptions permit a fixed interpreter or constructor to express increasingly complex objects. Description growth can nevertheless be neutral, redundant, or functionally stagnant; bounded descriptions may still support some effective organization growth through interactions, development, ecology, or other inheritance channels. Description length is not equivalent to functional or organizational novelty.

## K062 — Stringmol demonstrates endogenous constructional change, not sustained OEE

Clark, Hickinbotham, and Stepney report an artificial chemistry in which genomic material, copier, and constructor evolve together and produce viable offspring, with transitions between semantically closed states. This is strong evidence that fixed lower-level dynamics can host endogenous changes in constructional organization. It does not establish indefinitely repeated accessibility growth or the absence of eventual closure. [Clark, Hickinbotham & Stepney 2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2016.1033)

## K063 — Scaffolded and cumulative accessibility is established but can plateau

Potentiation, facilitated variation, developmental reuse, exaptation, major transitions, autocatalytic organization, and cumulative cultural evolution provide prior art for innovations that alter later accessibility. Such scaffolds may be one-off, finite, lost, or followed by a plateau. The existence of a reusable scaffold does not establish a general rule that search complexity continues to fall or that later construction remains open-ended. [Q006](Q006_GENERATIVE_CAPACITY.md), [Q007](Q007_META_EVOLUTION_CLOSURE.md), [Q008](Q008_UNIVERSALITY_VS_ACCESSIBILITY.md)

## K064 — The relevant set is the evolving accessible region, not the universal capacity set

For a fixed substrate, the constructible set \(\mathcal U\) can be infinite while the region reachable from a current population under mutation, development, ecology, selection, and reproduction \(\mathcal E(X_t)\) is sparse, disconnected, low-probability, or effectively finite. OEE-relevant claims therefore require more than \(\lvert\mathcal U\rvert\) or computational universality; they require a historical account of how \(\mathcal E(X_t)\) changes and is exploited. [Q008](Q008_UNIVERSALITY_VS_ACCESSIBILITY.md)

## K065 — No new hypothesis survived the universality/accessibility firewall

“Endogenously exploitable generative capacity” clarifies the connection between universal capacity and evolutionary history, but substantially overlaps established evolutionary accessibility, evolvability, facilitated variation, adjacent possible, potentiation, major transitions, and cumulative evolution. No separate hypothesis is justified by Q008. `HYPOTHESES.md` remains unchanged.

## K066 — Continuing viable pathways are already an OEE requirement

Taylor's OEE requirements go beyond one currently reachable novelty: the earlier formulation asks for mutational pathways of practically unlimited length, and the later formulation says the search space must typically offer routes among viable and potentially fitter individuals. This qualitatively implies recurring accessibility, but does not specify how path probability or discovery time scales with organizational complexity and evolutionary resources. [Taylor 2012](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/taylor2012exploring.pdf), [Taylor 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07403)

## K067 — Path existence, abundance, and feasible discovery are distinct

Accessibility-percolation theory distinguishes the expected number of monotonic paths from the probability that any path exists; rare path-rich landscapes can make the expectation misleading. First-passage theory further shows that targets can be reachable yet require expected time exponential in sequence length, even with gradients or many targets. Existential reachability is therefore insufficient for effective evolutionary accessibility. [Krug 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11913), [Chatterjee et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003818)

## K068 — Scalable evolvability is explicit prior art

Watson and Pollack use scalable evolvability for recursive composition that reuses adapted entities at successive hierarchical levels and avoids the increasing difficulty faced by mutation in their scale-invariant problem. Turney separately proposed indefinitely increasing evolvability, although his implemented fixed-length model approached equilibrium and unbounded increase was conjectured for extensible genomes. These are conditional models, not general OEE criteria, but they prevent treating accessibility scaling as a new principle. [Watson & Pollack 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0303-2647(02)00135-1), [Turney 1999](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0212042)

## K069 — Complexity has no established universal effect on accessibility

Fisher-geometric models predict a cost of complexity under specified pleiotropic assumptions, while modularity can mitigate it. Fixed genotype–phenotype models exhibit opposite trends: added genes and organizational levels increase robustness/evolvability in toyLIFE, whereas increasing regulatory interaction concentrates common derived phenotypes in a smaller morphospace in Borenstein and Krakauer's model. Digital-organism results likewise show that complexity can increase robustness or evolutionary opportunity, and a broad review finds weak general empirical support for complexity itself reducing evolvability. Search hardness is also target-dependent: it can make a specified innovation infeasible while delaying arrival at an optimum and thereby prolonging adaptive activity. Any accessibility-scaling claim must specify complexity, novelty classes, evolutionary dynamics, targets, and resources. [Orr 2000](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0014-3820.2000.tb00002.x), [Lenski et al. 1999](https://doi.org/10.1038/23245), [Catalán et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2017.0516), [Borenstein & Krakauer 2008](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000202), [Kaznatcheev 2019](https://doi.org/10.1534/genetics.119.302000), [Houle & Rossoni 2022](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-ecolsys-102320-090809)

## K070 — Accessibility scaling remains a measurement/theoretical gap

OEE supplies qualitative continuing-path and ongoing-evolvability requirements; landscape theory supplies scale-dependent path and hitting-time results for fixed representations; composition, duplication, and developmental-bias models provide conditional ways to make later search easier. No reviewed framework provides a resource-normalized test that remains comparable when genotype–phenotype maps, inheritance systems, ecological contexts, and selectable units change. Q009 therefore justifies no new scientific hypothesis. [Q009](Q009_ACCESSIBILITY_SCALING.md)

## K071 — First passage is coordinate invariant only with all semantics transported

An invertible relabeling of a stochastic process preserves a first-passage distribution when the initial law, target event, and time/resource functional are pushed forward with the process. Encoding-dependent distances or fractions of a represented search space need not be invariant. First passage is therefore representation-robust for the same matched event, not intrinsically immune to target or resource redefinition. [Q010](Q010_MEASUREMENT_INVARIANCE.md)

## K072 — Coarse-grained first passage has exact, property-relative conditions

If a target is a union of partition blocks, its hitting time equals pathwise the hitting time of the corresponding target in the exact projected process. Strong lumpability makes that projection an autonomous Markov chain for every initial distribution; weak lumpability is initial-distribution dependent, and non-lumpable projections generally retain memory. Bisimulation, semi-Markov models, history augmentation, and exact milestoning can preserve selected passage properties under explicit conditions, but preserving a mean or slow mode does not generally preserve a full distribution. [Jernigan & Baran 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-7152(03)00126-3), [Geiger & Temmel 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.4375), [Aristoff et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4879838/)

## K073 — Causal equivalence constrains but does not uniquely choose novelty classes

Predictive causal states, probabilistic bisimulation, and intervention-preserving causal abstractions define rigorous equivalences relative to specified future observations, labels/rewards, macrovariables, or intervention families. They can validate a proposed cross-level mapping but do not select an observer-free evolutionary ontology. A genuinely new post-transition class may therefore have no defined pre-transition first-passage comparator. [Shalizi & Crutchfield 2001](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9907176), [Givan, Dean & Greig 2003](https://cs.brown.edu/people/tdean/publications/archive/GivanetalAIJ-03.pdf), [Rubenstein et al. 2017](https://is.mpg.de/uploads/publication_attachment/attachment/427/UAI_2017_Rubensteinetal.pdf)

## K074 — OEE metrics standardize within chosen ontologies, not across changing ones

Evolutionary activity and MODES compare realized histories after component identities, relevance filters, activity or distance definitions, and clocks are supplied. Adams et al.'s formal innovation likewise depends on an organism–environment partition, update clock, and isolated counterfactual. These frameworks do not provide latent first-passage accessibility or a transport rule across changes in individuality, heredity, or novelty vocabulary. MODES explicitly leaves measurement of potential for major transitions as future work. [Bedau, Snyder & Packard 1998](https://sfi-edu.s3.amazonaws.com/sfi-edu/production/uploads/sfi-com/dev/uploads/filer/b3/e9/b3e9317b-5798-485b-8bb1-3465cd336e0e/98-03-025.pdf), [Dolson et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00280), [Adams et al. 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5430523/)

## K075 — No universal evolutionary resource clock survives major transitions

Physical time, generations, birth events, mutation opportunities, population-time, evaluated variants, energy, and computational or causal events normalize different opportunity sets. Major transitions can shift reproduction and fitness from lower-level entities to collectives, so identically named generations need not be commensurate. Physical time or energy can span levels but does not equalize reproductive or mutational opportunity. No reviewed multilevel or OEE theory derives one representation-independent evolutionary resource unit. [Michod 2007](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0701489104), [West et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547252/), [Q010](Q010_MEASUREMENT_INVARIANCE.md)

## K076 — Accessibility is partially, not universally, comparable across transformations

Full first-passage distributions preserve finite-budget and tail information lost by means, and stochastic dominance can partially order them once targets and resources are matched. Across transformational novelty, only conserved or explicitly mapped classes, initial ensembles, dynamics, and clocks support comparison; unmatched new classes are undefined rather than zero or infinitely difficult. The result is conditional families of comparisons with explicit incomparabilities, not a universal scalar or new OEE mechanism. Q010 therefore justifies no scientific hypothesis. [Q010](Q010_MEASUREMENT_INVARIANCE.md)

## K077 — ETIs preserve continuity through multilevel overlap

New evolutionary individuals need not acquire complete reproduction, heredity, and individuality before higher-level selection begins. Lower-level reproduction, nascent life cycles, or ecology can generate collective variation, differential success, and parent–offspring resemblance; lower- and higher-level selection then coexist while collective development and transmission evolve. MLS1-to-MLS2 is a useful diachronic description, not an instantaneous replacement of levels. [Szathmáry 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421398112), [Ratcliff et al. 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5665808/)

## K078 — Ecological scaffolding initiates but does not complete a transition

Patch structure, growth, extinction, and dispersal can externally supply collective birth, death, variation, and heredity before collectives reproduce autonomously. Removing such a scaffold can remove the Darwinian-like collective regime, so scaffolded collective selection is not sufficient evidence of a completed ETI. [Black, Bourrat & Rainey 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-1086-9)

## K079 — Endogenization and post-scaffold persistence are explicit prior art

Endogenization names evolved retention or reconstruction of properties initially supplied by an ecological scaffold. Models formalize persistence after scaffold alteration or removal through trade-offs and evolutionary hysteresis. This is a strong scaffold-dependence test within that framework, but it neither requires independence from every environment nor guarantees full organismality, further novelty, or OEE. [Bourrat 2025](https://doi.org/10.1086/719118), [Doulcier et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-50625-1)

## K080 — New-level heredity can be built from old-level inheritance

Clonal growth and bottlenecks can immediately generate heritable collective traits; reproducible development can reconstruct collective organization from lower-level carriers; and ecological interactions can evolve developmental correction that yields community-level parent–offspring resemblance. A physically separate higher-level code is not required, and no universal ordering of collectivity, reproduction, individuality, and heredity is established. [Zamani-Dahaj et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.3390/genes14081635), [Doulcier et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.53433)

## K081 — Major transitions reorganize rather than monotonically include possibilities

Transitions can open higher-level developmental and reproductive capacities while formerly independent constituents lose genes, autonomy, reproductive fate, or access to environments. Reversal becomes difficult in some cases but is not definitional. Possibility-space claims across transitions must therefore specify level and entity; \(\mathcal O_t\subseteq\mathcal O_{t+1}\) is not a justified default. [Griesemer et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547265/), [Rebolleda-Gómez 2019](https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.13654)

## K082 — Endogenous scaffold generation has direct adjacent prior art

Niche construction, ecological inheritance, social niche construction, and constructionist ecological scaffolding already describe evolutionary entities modifying environmental or population structure that changes subsequent selection and can help individuate collectives. The distinct unresolved issue is complete causal demonstration that one endogenized regime generates, initiates, and permits endogenization of a subsequent regime—not the conceptual possibility of self-produced scaffolds. [Ryan, Powers & Watson 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4686542/), [Bourrat et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.70661)

## K083 — Transition mechanisms are fragmented and one transition does not imply another

Ecological scaffolds, nascent life cycles, bottlenecks, conflict mediation, fair transmission, developmental reconstruction, and information-system changes solve different transition problems. Repeated and recursive transitions are prior art, but no reviewed framework establishes a generic or indefinitely repeatable generate–initiate–endogenize mechanism. One endogenized transition therefore does not establish continued transformational evolution. Q011 justifies no new scientific hypothesis. [Szathmáry 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421398112), [Q011](Q011_EVOLUTIONARY_REGIME_BRIDGING.md)

## K084 — Ongoing transitions are a hallmark, not a feedback mechanism

OEE literature identifies ongoing major transitions as an especially strong form of open-endedness while explicitly noting that known transitions were contingent, that there may be no more, and that individual transitions can spur adaptations without constituting OEE. A realized sequence therefore does not explain its continuation. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Packard et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00291)

## K085 — Transition-to-transition effects are conditionally meaningful

No standard scalar “transition propensity” was found. Target-specific evolvability, conditional character-transition rates, key innovations, facilitating evolutionary transitions, and causal prerequisite relations can express bounded comparisons when transition classes, environments, and resources are sufficiently aligned. This distinguishes occurrence, further reachability, and a causal change in reachability without overriding Q010's partial-comparability limit. [Pagel 1994](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.1994.0006), [Robin et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2021.711556), [Q012](Q012_TRANSITION_PROPENSITY.md)

## K086 — DISHTINY pre-enables rather than evolves its recursive hierarchy

DISHTINY evolves transition-relevant allocation, reproduction, apoptosis, communication, and life-history strategies. Its experiments nevertheless supply level-specific hereditary identifiers, group-forming choices, resource scales, and strict nesting; the original two-level study found that level-two individuality could bypass level one. Later SignalGP work expanded evolved behavior but retained configured group levels. The system demonstrates exploitation of a designer-provided hierarchical channel, not one transition creating the next level's availability. [Moreno & Ofria 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00284), [Moreno & Ofria 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2022.750837)

## K087 — Transition products have target-specific downstream effects

Snowflake multicellularity generated a single-cell bottleneck and thereby higher-level evolvability; eukaryotic conflict mediation plausibly facilitated repeated multicellularity; and information-system transitions can be prerequisites for later organization. These show downstream evolutionary consequences, but not a general increase in access to another regime transition. Increased genome size or information bandwidth is likewise not sufficient. [Ratcliff et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7102), [Blackstone 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3685466/), [Elliott & Gregory 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4571570/)

## K088 — No direction of hierarchical transition feedback is established

Prior theory explicitly compared possible upward bias, unbiased diffusion, and hierarchical loss. Greater hierarchy may offer more combinations for higher-level association, but also more routes to disassembly; evidence for either a systematic upward or downward bias was judged inconclusive. Specific transitions can enable or suppress specific later routes without implying a common sign. [McShea 2001](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1420-9101.2001.00283.x)

## K089 — Resource costs and reversions oppose ladder models

Successive hierarchy can increase organismal resource demands, lengthen life cycles, reduce higher-level population sizes, and add coordination costs. Transitions can also be reversed physiologically or undermined by renewed lower-level conflict. Transition accessibility is consequently a context-dependent directed network with losses and reversions, not an irreversible ordered ladder. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Rebolleda-Gómez 2019](https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.13654), [Schenkel, Ågren & Patten 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/jeb/voag007)

## K090 — Cumulative transition history lacks a general causal account

Facilitating transitions, key innovations, evolved open-ended culture, recursive-transition descriptions, and predefined artificial hierarchies document dependencies and repeated outcomes. No reviewed controlled sequence shows that a product evolved during one completed transition increases resource-normalized access to several distinct later regimes whose channels were not pre-enabled. The boundary is historical but not general, and Q012 justifies no new hypothesis. [Borg et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00406), [Q012](Q012_TRANSITION_PROPENSITY.md)

## K091 — Unprescribed collective reproduction has direct prior art

Bottom-up multicellularity models can keep lower-level dynamics fixed, omit collective identifiers and collective fitness terms, and still evolve adhesion, collective behavior, or multicellular life cycles through cell-level ecology and co-option. A 2026 preprint extends this to regulated unicellular and multicellular propagule modes that are not preselected by the model. These results establish model-relative possibility within supplied behavioral affordances; they do not by themselves identify a new collective hereditary/selectable unit. [Staps, van Gestel & Tarnita 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-0940-0), [Colizzi, Vroomans & Merks 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.56349), [Fernandes, Vroomans & Colizzi 2026, preprint v3](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199)

## K092 — Evidence for endogenous organizational-type creation is clausewise, not conjunctive

Snowflake yeast combines emergent collective reproduction and collective-trait evolution with reverse-genetic restoration of unicellularity, but its selection protocol explicitly targets settling and size. Predator-evolved *Chlamydomonas* supplies ecologically selected, stable heritable multicellular life cycles without a multicellularity objective, but lacks targeted disruption/rescue and an explicit collective selection partition. Ecological-scaffolding experiments supply collective selection and removal tests but predefine the collective cycle. No reviewed case satisfies all Q013 constraints in one system. [Ratcliff et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1115323109), [Ratcliff et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7102), [Herron et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-39558-8), [Q013](Q013_ENDOGENOUS_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K093 — Ablation and rescue are neither necessary nor sufficient for a new evolutionary type

Destroying an evolved adhesion, developmental, or scaffold relation can establish causal necessity for a specified phenotype or reproductive relation in the tested background. Rescue strengthens specificity and helps exclude general damage. Neither intervention establishes historical origin, ancestral absence, collective heredity, level-specific selection, constitutive identity, or evolutionary individuality; accepted historical and experimental transition claims can also rely on convergent non-ablation evidence. Intervention is strongest after the type-defining relation has been independently specified. [Baumgartner & Gebharter 2016](https://doi.org/10.1093/bjps/axv003), [Q014](Q014_EVIDENCE_FOR_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K094 — External selection pressure does not specify the evolved organizational solution

An experiment may impose a fitness outcome while leaving the mechanism that achieves it unspecified. Settling selection did not encode `ACE2` loss, persistent mother–daughter attachment, branching geometry, or fracture-based reproduction; primary studies nevertheless treat snowflake yeast as a de novo transition toward multicellular individuality. “Untargeted selection” is therefore not necessary for endogenous solution construction, although a supplied collective boundary or reproductive cycle limits stronger claims about level discovery. [Ratcliff et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1115323109), [Ratcliff et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7102), [Q014](Q014_EVIDENCE_FOR_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K095 — Origin, heredity, selection, and transition completion are different events

Evolutionary-novelty theory separates formation of a new attribute from adaptive refinement and ecological success. ETI theory separates group formation, maintenance, transformation, and collective life-cycle closure. A collective phenotype can therefore originate before it is heritable, become heritable before collective selection acts, and undergo later adaptive refinement without a unique category-independent moment of “organizational creation.” [Erwin 2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/brv.12643), [Doulcier, Takacs & Bourrat 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.162), [Q014](Q014_EVIDENCE_FOR_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K096 — Collective heredity requires reconstruction, not a separate higher-level code

For a heritable collective organization, type-relevant parent–offspring resemblance must follow a defensible collective ancestry relation rather than independent environmental recurrence. Lower-level genetic inheritance, developmental interactions, and environmental resources may reconstruct that organization; a distinct collective genome is unnecessary. Collective-trait heritability can arise immediately from clonal group formation and remains trait-, population-, and environment-relative. [Zamani-Dahaj et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-018-0612-6), [Zamani-Dahaj et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.3390/genes14081635), [Q014](Q014_EVIDENCE_FOR_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K097 — Selection at the emergent level is required for adaptation, not first origin

A new collective organization can first arise through mutation, development, drift, or selection on correlated lower-level traits. Actual collective selection is required to claim realized collective adaptation, but not to date the phenotype's origin. Price, contextual, and counterfactual-fitness analyses answer different level-specific questions; no single nonzero statistic establishes collective individuality without a collective ancestry and reproductive interpretation. [Damuth & Heisler 1988](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00647962), [Shelton & Michod 2020](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2019.0364), [Q014](Q014_EVIDENCE_FOR_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K098 — Latent and newly constructed capacities are distinguishable only over a bounded assay

Ancestral or ancestral-proxy and descendant reaction norms can be compared in common environments. Expression by the ancestor under some condition supports latent capacity; expression only by descendants under matched conditions supports evolved construction or accommodation within the tested domain. Co-option of old components can still create a new causal organization, while a new genotype can merely shift an ancestral threshold. No finite assay proves universal ancestral incapacity. [Levis & Pfennig 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tree.2016.03.012), [DiFrisco, Love & Wagner 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10539-020-09762-2), [Q014](Q014_EVIDENCE_FOR_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K099 — Evidence for a new organizational type is a conditional conjunction

Heredity, selection, intervention, and absence of predefined labels can all hold for a new value of an existing trait, so the Q013 conjunction was insufficient as a type criterion. A defensible domain-specific claim additionally declares entities, collective ancestry, environments, and a causal equivalence over reproduction/inheritance; contrasts descendant construction with matched ancestral capacity; identifies internally generated individuating mechanisms; and tests robustness under causally adequate recodings. Untargeted selection and ablation/rescue are optional evidence, not universal gates. If “new organizational type” means a new Darwinian individual, established ETI terminology is sufficient; otherwise the phrase must be replaced by the specific relation-level claim. [Clarke 2013](https://doi.org/10.5840/jphil2013110817), [Bourrat 2025](https://doi.org/10.1086/719118), [Q014](Q014_EVIDENCE_FOR_ORGANIZATIONAL_TYPE_CREATION.md)

## K100 — Unbounded reachability is not latent ancestral capacity

Environmental plasticity, standing or cryptic variation, recombinational capacity, local mutational accessibility, and long-run historical evolvability use different ancestral ensembles and transformation operators. If arbitrary mutation, recombination, manipulation, environment, and evolutionary duration are admitted, “latent” collapses into positive reachability and no longer distinguishes an ancestrally instantiated developmental capacity from an evolved one. Mutational neighborhoods should therefore be described as accessibility, not strict developmental latency. [Fontana & Schuster 1998](https://doi.org/10.1006/jtbi.1998.0771), [Hayden, Ferrada & Wagner 2011](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10083), [Q015](Q015_CONSTRUCTION_VS_LATENT_CAPACITY.md)

## K101 — Plastic phenotype origin and organizational construction can occur at different times

Plasticity-first methods compare ancestor or ancestral-proxy and descendant reaction norms in common environments. An ancestor's induction of a phenotype supports environmentally latent phenotypic capacity, while genetic accommodation can later alter its expression threshold, frequency, robustness, form, or causal reconstruction. Plastic origin therefore does not exclude later construction of a new inherited developmental, reproductive, or inheritance relation; the phenotype's first appearance and the relation's evolutionary establishment must be dated separately. [Levis & Pfennig 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tree.2016.03.012), [Sikkink et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1534/g3.114.010553), [Q015](Q015_CONSTRUCTION_VS_LATENT_CAPACITY.md)

## K102 — Component novelty does not determine organizational novelty

Co-option, exaptation, and facilitated variation establish that conserved genes, pathways, structures, and core processes can be redeployed through new regulatory or causal relations to produce new traits or functions. Conversely, developmental-system drift shows that a homologous character can retain identity while its underlying mechanisms diverge. Neither the age of components nor the magnitude of genetic or mechanistic change determines causal organizational identity. [Gould & Vrba 1982](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0094837300004310), [Gerhart & Kirschner 2007](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0701035104), [True & Haag 2001](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1525-142x.2001.003002109.x)

## K103 — Reconstruction and reversion establish bounded causal claims

Ancestral resurrection and common-garden assays measure specified ancestral reaction norms; mutation reconstruction measures sufficiency and epistasis in a chosen background; derived reversion measures dependence and reversibility in another background; replay estimates contingency under its protocol. None alone excludes all alternative routes, proves universal ancestral incapacity, or identifies a causal type change. These stronger claims additionally require an explicit ancestral bound and independently justified equivalence. [Blount, Borland & Lenski 2008](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0803151105), [Blount et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11514), [Bridgham, Ortlund & Thornton 2009](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08249)

## K104 — Construction versus latency is conditionally testable, not absolute

A descendant supports a bounded construction claim when it recurrently produces an organization not detected in—or formally excluded from, if the assay is exhaustive—the ancestral environmental and standing-variation repertoire, inherited changes causally account for that difference, and ancestor and descendant fall into different classes under a predeclared intervention-preserving equivalence over the relevant developmental, reproductive, or inheritance relations. The result remains conditional on the ancestral ensemble, transformations, resources, variables, and interventions. Character-identity mechanisms address anatomical homology; causal abstraction validates specified mappings; neither supplies a universal equivalence for all reproduction and inheritance organizations. At the broader level, evolved change to variation-generating organization is already covered by evolution of evolvability and developmental-system evolution; one such change is not OEE. [DiFrisco, Love & Wagner 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10539-020-09762-2), [Beckers, Eberhardt & Halpern 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v115/beckers20a.html), [Q015](Q015_CONSTRUCTION_VS_LATENT_CAPACITY.md)

## K105 — Developmental System Drift is the decisive type-preserving rewiring case

Homologous characters can retain recognizable anatomy, developmental output, and historical identity while gene expression, regulatory dependencies, individual gene functions, and loss-of-function responses diverge. Complete molecular intervention equivalence is therefore too strict for biological type identity, while phenotype identity alone is too weak. Character Identity Mechanisms instead seek a level-specific, traceable causal profile that permits etiological variation. [True & Haag 2001](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1525-142x.2001.003002109.x), [Verster et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0090258), [DiFrisco, Love & Wagner 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10539-020-09762-2)

## K106 — Network topology and causal abstraction do not select biological identity

Different regulatory-network topologies can realize the same phenotype, and networks within one phenotype-neutral set can expose different mutational neighborhoods. Topological edit distance, attractor change, limited input–output agreement, and future variation therefore do not independently determine organizational type. Exact, approximate, bisimulation, and intervention-preserving equivalences can validate a declared mapping, but remain relative to variables, interventions, outputs, and tolerances; they do not decide whether the identity bearer is a character, reproducer, inheritance relation, individual, or autonomous closure. [Ciliberti, Martin & Wagner 2007](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0705396104), [Rubenstein et al. 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.00819), [Beckers & Halpern 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03789)

## K107 — Organizational identity has domain-specific invariants, not one general invariant

Morphological homology uses historical continuity and character-identity profiles; reproducer claims use parentage and life-cycle relations; heredity claims use cross-generational transmission or reconstruction; evolutionary individuality uses Darwinian and individuating roles; autonomy theories use closure at a declared boundary and timescale. Rewiring is type-preserving when it replaces realizers while preserving the relevant domain relation, and type-changing when that independently motivated relation changes. This is conditionally objective but does not define a universal natural kind called “organizational type.” [Wagner 2007](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg2099), [Clarke 2013](https://doi.org/10.5840/jphil2013110817), [Doulcier, Takacs & Bourrat 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.162), [Q016](Q016_CAUSAL_REWIRING_VS_TYPE_CHANGE.md)

## K108 — Reproduction, closure, and causal emergence are claim-specific, not universal gates

A changed reproduction relation is necessary for a new-reproducer claim but neither necessary for every organizational novelty nor sufficient for a Darwinian individual. Constraint closure can delimit autonomy but underdetermines organismality and heredity. Causal emergence can identify a consequential macrodescription in systems with no reproduction, heredity, or evolutionary history. None alone is a necessary-and-sufficient organizational-type criterion. [Godfrey-Smith 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421378112), [Mossio & Moreno 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21162371/), [Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi 2013](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314922110)

## K109 — Relational novelty and multiple realization are established prior art

Co-option, exaptation, ChIM theory, relational accounts of pregnancy, and multiple-realization accounts of individuality already explain how old components can enter new identity-bearing relations and how distinct mechanisms can realize a stable higher-level role. “New relations among old parts” is therefore not a new OEE hypothesis. The scientific task is to show that the changed relation individuates a character, reproducer, hereditary organization, evolutionary individual, or autonomous system rather than merely adding a network edge. [DiFrisco, Love & Wagner 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semcdb.2022.03.030), [Nuño de la Rosa, Pavličev & Etxeberria 2021](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.572106), [Q016](Q016_CAUSAL_REWIRING_VS_TYPE_CHANGE.md)

## K110 — Q016 leaves a definition/formalization test, not a mechanism

No cross-domain invariant was found beyond preserving an independently chosen identity-bearing relation under admissible implementation replacement. A proposed relation must classify recognized same-type and different-type controls under one variable set, intervention family, and tolerance. Until such a domain-specific equivalence is validated, asking which dynamics generate “organizational-type changes” is premature; no scientific hypothesis is justified. [Q016](Q016_CAUSAL_REWIRING_VS_TYPE_CHANGE.md)

## K111 — Collective-reproducer identity is carried by parentage and cycle structure

For a collective-reproducer claim, a domain-motivated equivalence can preserve a genealogical/material parent–offspring relation, at least one collective multiplicative transition, and closure of a predeclared stage cycle while allowing size, timing, transition rates, propagule size, bond topology, and molecular implementation to vary. The entity rule, stage partition, intervention family, and detection tolerance still must be fixed independently; life-cycle graph structure does not choose its own biological interpretation. [Godfrey-Smith 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421378112), [Doulcier, Takacs & Bourrat 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.162), [Q017](Q017_SNOWFLAKE_COLLECTIVE_REPRODUCER_EQUIVALENCE.md)

## K112 — One reproducer relation separates early from later snowflake evolution

Functional `ACE2` produces post-budding cell separation in the assayed unicellular ancestor. `ACE2` loss produces persistent clonal attachment, multicellular propagules, juvenile and adult collective stages, and a recurrent growth–fracture cycle; knockout is sufficient and complementation of an early strain restores unicellularity. Later anaerobic lineages evolved roughly \(2\times10^4\)-fold larger and \(10^4\)-fold tougher clusters through elongated cells and branch entanglement while retaining the clonal multicellular life cycle. The same collective-parentage/closure criterion therefore classifies the first contrast as a new collective reproducer and the second as within-type refinement. [Ratcliff et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1115323109), [Ratcliff et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7102), [Bozdag et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06052-1)

## K113 — The snowflake equivalence is structurally supported but not fully intervention-validated

The published comparisons establish collective propagules and cycle structure in early snowflake yeast, direct `ACE2` knockout/complementation for the early transition, and retention of the clonal life cycle in macroscopic lineages. They do not report one matched association-removal, propagule-isolation, reconstruction, and tolerance protocol applied to the unicellular ancestor, early snowflake, and macroscopic snowflake genotypes. Structural equivalence is therefore supported; the richer intervention-preserving equivalence remains a measurement and causal-validation gap. [Q017](Q017_SNOWFLAKE_COLLECTIVE_REPRODUCER_EQUIVALENCE.md)

## K114 — A new collective reproducer is neither a completed ETI nor OEE

A collective growth–fragmentation cycle adds an embedding parent–offspring domain while cellular reproduction persists. This is established major-transition and nascent-life-cycle territory, not a distinct workspace concept. Life-cycle closure can mark a new reproducer under one ETI account, while broader individuality theories also track heritable fitness variation, integration, conflict mediation, and de-Darwinization. One such transition—however well established—does not demonstrate ongoing transformational novelty or OEE. [Ratcliff et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0420), [Taylor 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00290), [Q017](Q017_SNOWFLAKE_COLLECTIVE_REPRODUCER_EQUIVALENCE.md)

## K115 — Fernandes is collective-level blind in several distinct senses

Fernandes, Vroomans, and Colizzi explicitly model Cellular Potts cells, cell division, death, food, chemotaxis, adhesion, a fixed-topology regulatory architecture, and mutation. They do not encode collective identifiers, memberships, parentage, offspring, collective generations, collective fitness used in evolution, or hierarchical nesting channels. Cell survival and division receive direct reproductive consequences; collective chemotaxis, dispersal, and propagule production alter those consequences through ecology. The model is therefore selection-, entity-, and reproduction-blind with respect to collectives, and scale-blind above the explicit cell level, but it is not blind to cell reproduction or every scale. [Fernandes, Vroomans & Colizzi 2026, uncertified preprint v3](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199), [Q018](Q018_FROZEN_EQUIVALENCE_TRANSFER.md)

## K116 — Fernandes reports recurrent aggregative life cycles, not clonal daughter-association groups

The preprint reports strictly unicellular, multicellular plus unicellular-propagule, and strictly multicellular life cycles. Strictly multicellular cases range from differential-adhesion multicellular propagules to high-adhesion clusters that tear rarely. The clusters form aggregatively, and Supplementary Figure S3 shows that both propagule-forming and high-adhesion clusters contain many distantly related cell lineages. The strongest single-cell-propagule sequence is an adhering migrating cluster, detachment of one dividing cell, division, and re-adhesion of its daughters into a new cluster. [Fernandes et al. 2026, Figs. 2–4 and Supplementary Fig. S3](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199), [Q018](Q018_FROZEN_EQUIVALENCE_TRANSFER.md)

## K117 — The frozen Q017 entity relation has no positive Fernandes case

Q017 defined a candidate collective as a cohesive clonal assembly produced by continuing genealogical association of daughter cells and required an offspring to contain at least two clonally related cells at separation. Applied unchanged, the first clause excludes Fernandes' aggregative, genetically heterogeneous clusters, while the second independently excludes its single-cell propagules. Material detachment, tearing, and recurrent author-defined life cycles remain real process evidence, but they are not parentage, multiplication, and closure among Q017 units. The correct frozen-transfer decision is SYSTEMS NOT IN SAME IDENTITY DOMAIN, not evidence that Fernandes lacks multicellular reproduction under every defensible domain model. [Q017](Q017_SNOWFLAKE_COLLECTIVE_REPRODUCER_EQUIVALENCE.md), [Q018](Q018_FROZEN_EQUIVALENCE_TRANSFER.md)

## K118 — Generic affordances are distinct from enumerated collective reproduction

The Fernandes substrate makes adhesion, sensing, behavioural switching, aggregation, and cell division available and fixes the regulatory architecture, but it does not enumerate single-cell propagules, multicellular propagules, tearing, or high-adhesion collective life cycles as selectable modes. These arise from evolved regulatory parameters and cell-level ecological interactions. This differs from DISHTINY's configured group IDs and nesting levels while remaining a bounded, strongly structured substrate rather than an unconstrained discovery space. [Colizzi, Vroomans & Merks 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.56349), [Vroomans & Colizzi 2023](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12862-023-02133-x), [Fernandes et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199)

## K119 — Structural vocabulary transfers more widely than the frozen equivalence

Parentage, multiplication, and recurrent reconstruction remain meaningful for aggregative and single-cell-propagule systems, but Q017 bundled them with clonal development and a multicellular-at-separation cutoff. Its exact intervention family is unperformed or partly undefined in Fernandes, and its multiplication/reconstruction probability threshold has no numerical value. Thus the transfer failure combines a decisive mismatch with the frozen Q017 identity relation and additional evidential and numerical failures. Q019 subsequently establishes that clonality and offspring size are snowflake-specific realizers in the broader collective-life-cycle domain; mechanism analysis remains premature. [Q018](Q018_FROZEN_EQUIVALENCE_TRANSFER.md), [Q019](Q019_COLLECTIVE_REPRODUCER_DOMAIN_BOUNDARY.md)

## K120 — Collective life-cycle reproduction does not generally require clonal formation or a multicellular propagule

Doulcier, Takacs, and Bourrat describe aggregation, transformation, and multiplication as general life-cycle processes and use both sexual metazoan cycles and clonal growth–fragmentation as examples. Godfrey-Smith treats a one-cell bottleneck as a dimension that makes collective reproduction more definite, not as an exclusion of the multicellular parent from reproduction. Accepted sexual cycles, aggregative social cycles, and Griesemer-style biofilm reproduction therefore rule out clonality, continuing daughter association, physical fragmentation, genetic homogeneity, and at least two cells at separation as universal collective-reproduction requirements. [Doulcier, Takacs & Bourrat 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.162), [Godfrey-Smith 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421378112), [Ereshefsky & Pedroso 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421377112), [Q019](Q019_COLLECTIVE_REPRODUCER_DOMAIN_BOUNDARY.md)

## K121 — Multiplication and closure identify a broad life-cycle relation but not strong individuality

In the stage-structured account, multiplication requires a fertility transition producing another candidate stage instance; closure requires every declared stage to be reachable from every other along the cycle, corresponding to irreducibility of the projection matrix. Recurrence without multiplication and multiplication among disconnected stage sets are negative cases. These conditions classify a collective life cycle only relative to a defensible candidate-unit and stage coarse-graining. They do not by themselves establish reproductive autonomy, heritable fitness-relevant variation, conflict suppression, or a joint evolutionary lineage. [Doulcier, Takacs & Bourrat 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.162), [Bourrat 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-024-04880-w)

## K122 — Formation mode, relatedness, and bottlenecks affect individuality without defining reproduction

Staying together and coming together are established formation operations with different evolutionary problems. Clonal formation and narrow bottlenecks raise relatedness, reduce within-collective conflict, and are strongly associated with obligate and more complex multicellularity; aggregative formation permits chimerism and separate lower-level lineages. These facts make formation mode and relatedness central to the stability and degree of Darwinian individuality, but not necessary conditions for the causal multiplication and reconstruction of a collective life cycle. [Tarnita, Taubes & Nowak 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2012.11.022), [Fisher, Cornwallis & West 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2013.05.004), [West et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421402112)

## K123 — Reproducee, reproducer, and Darwinian individual are distinct axes of the Q018 comparison

Bourrat defines a reproducee through temporal succession, causal production, and membership in the same relevant class; a reproducer additionally has reproductive autonomy across relevant backgrounds; a paradigmatic Darwinian individual has high reproductive causal control. Wilmsen and Kost separately classify physiological integration and the evolutionary component that carries heritable fitness-relevant variation in a traceable lineage. Aggregative or scaffolded cycles can therefore multiply and close while lacking, or not yet demonstrating, autonomous collective reproduction or a joint evolutionary lineage. [Bourrat 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-024-04880-w), [Wilmsen & Kost 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/bies.70098)

## K124 — Q018 found the boundary of its frozen model, not an ontological clonal/aggregative split

The frozen Q017 transfer remains failed. Independent theory nevertheless places snowflake growth–fragmentation and Fernandes' reported aggregate–propagule–aggregate regimes in one broad structural collective-life-cycle domain based on causal multiplication and recurrent stage reconstruction. Snowflake has stronger evidence for collective heredity and Darwinian individuality; given Fernandes' many-lineage aggregation and cell-level fitness semantics, its collective parentage fidelity and reproductive autonomy remain unresolved. The canonical qualification is therefore: failure of a frozen equivalence establishes failure of that equivalence, not ontological non-equivalence. The Q019 decision is **REPRODUCTION SHARED, INDIVIDUALITY DIFFERS**, with **CONDITIONALLY READY** mechanism status and no new hypothesis. ## K125 — Collective genealogy may be reticulate but requires traceable causal descent

A collective offspring need not have a unique parent: sexual and aggregative systems can be represented by directed pedigrees or parentage hypergraphs with several causally weighted parents. Aggregation therefore does not by itself destroy collective genealogy. If collective boundaries, contributions, and descent events cannot be assigned non-arbitrarily, however, only the genealogy of lower-level cells is identifiable. Temporal recurrence or co-membership alone is insufficient. [Bourrat 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12064-019-00294-2), [Q020](Q020_FERNANDES_COLLECTIVE_INDIVIDUALITY.md)

## K126 — Fernandes supports a local collective parentage edge but no persistent collective pedigree

In the reported single-cell-propagule regime, a cell leaves an identified source cluster, divides, and its daughters re-adhere as a newborn cluster. This directly supports a local source-cluster → propagule → newborn-cluster relation; the unicellular stage need not terminate the collective life cycle. The publication does not assign persistent cluster identities, concatenate such edges across generations, or resolve split/merge ancestry. Its phylogenies trace cells, not parent and offspring collectives. A short-range genealogical reproducer is therefore supported, while a multigeneration collective genealogy is not established. [Fernandes, Vroomans & Colizzi 2026, uncertified preprint v3](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199), [Bourrat 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-024-04880-w), [Q020](Q020_FERNANDES_COLLECTIVE_INDIVIDUALITY.md)

## K127 — Cellular inheritance can realize collective heredity, but reconstruction alone does not demonstrate it

Inherited lower-level programs can reconstruct higher-level traits through a single-cell bottleneck; a separate collective genome is unnecessary. Fernandes' mutation-free cellular-MRCA assays establish inherited cell programs and repeatable collective phenotypes, not parent–offspring collective resemblance, because the assay begins with cloned cells rather than pedigree-linked offspring collectives. Moreover, positive heritability of additive collective traits can mathematically mirror particle-level heritability without establishing stronger individuality. Parent-specific transmission of declared collective traits, especially nonlinear or composition-dependent traits, remains unmeasured. [Herron, Zamani-Dahaj & Ratcliff 2018](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-018-0612-6), [Bourrat 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12064-019-00294-2), [Q020](Q020_FERNANDES_COLLECTIVE_INDIVIDUALITY.md)

## K128 — Fernandes establishes MLS1-like effects, not an MLS2-like process

Collective chemotaxis, cluster size, spatial integrity, and dispersal alter food acquisition, survival, and division of constituent cells, satisfying an MLS1-like contextual description. The paper's fitness measure is steady-state food depletion by a cell population, and its competitions track cell-genetic lineages or populations. It does not count descendant collectives of identified parents or show that collective variants differ in collective reproductive output. An explicit group-fitness function is unnecessary for emergent MLS2, but a collective pedigree, heritable collective variation, and differential collective output must be demonstrable; they are not reported here. [Damuth & Heisler 1988](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00647962), [Gardner 2015](https://doi.org/10.1111/jeb.12566), [Fernandes et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199), [Q020](Q020_FERNANDES_COLLECTIVE_INDIVIDUALITY.md)

## K129 — Fernandes is a genealogical reproducer in a limited sense, not an established Darwinian individual

Endogenous state-dependent adhesion organizes propagule release without imposed collective passage, and some propagule-forming cellular lineages persist across reported environments; these facts support partial, background-relative reproductive autonomy. Cells nevertheless retain independent evolutionary capacity, genetically distant lineages aggregate, and no collective pedigree, collective heritability, fitness-2, or MLS2 analysis is reported. Wilmsen and Kost's categories therefore support at most a Type IV-like classification from current evidence, with possible physiological coherence but no demonstrated joint evolutionary component. Q020's decision is **B — GENEALOGICAL REPRODUCER**, limited to local parentage; mechanism readiness is **NOT READY**, and no new hypothesis is justified. [Bourrat 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-024-04880-w), [Wilmsen & Kost 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/bies.70098), [Q020](Q020_FERNANDES_COLLECTIVE_INDIVIDUALITY.md)

## K130 — MLS1 and MLS2 are descriptions, not evidential thresholds

Bourrat's reappraisal argues that particle and collective reproductive outputs in one finite physical system are in principle connected by a map; treating MLS1 and MLS2 as objectively decoupled natural processes is therefore unwarranted. The perspectives can differ greatly in practical tractability and predictive value, particularly during an ETI, but neither the label “MLS2” nor the existence of a fitness-2 description establishes collective heredity. The relevant evidence is an independently defined collective genealogy, transmitted collective variation, and—if selection is claimed—a mapping from lower-level events to differential collective-lineage contribution. [Bourrat 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-023-04285-1), [Q021](Q021_MINIMAL_COLLECTIVE_HEREDITY_TEST.md)

## K131 — Collective heredity is a population relation over declared genealogical units

For a declared collective trait, heredity requires independently defined parent and offspring collectives, between-parent variation, measurements at homologous life-cycle stages, and parent-specific prediction of descendant traits relative to appropriate nonparents under specified environments. No estimator or positive numerical threshold is constitutive, and heritability is conditional on population, trait, genealogy, environment, and measurement design. A one-generation cohort with many families can estimate one transmission step, but a continuing collective lineage additionally requires concatenated edges in which some offspring later reproduce; no universal number of generations determines statistical sufficiency. [Lewontin 1970](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.es.01.110170.000245), [Bourrat 2015](https://doi.org/10.1093/bjps/axu015), [Q021](Q021_MINIMAL_COLLECTIVE_HEREDITY_TEST.md)

## K132 — Reticulate collective heredity can preserve all material contributors

Aggregation and fusion permit one offspring collective to have several parents. A defensible genealogy records every nonzero tracked material contribution in a directed temporal parentage network or hypergraph rather than assigning a unique parent by an arbitrary majority rule. Parent-specific prediction can use the full contribution vector or a justified contribution-weighted parental phenotype. Fernandes' single-cell propagule supplies a locally unique parent until the newborn merges; after a detected merger, ancestry is reticulate, while an unobserved merger requires censoring rather than statistical repair. [Bourrat 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12064-019-00294-2), [Q021](Q021_MINIMAL_COLLECTIVE_HEREDITY_TEST.md)

## K133 — Reconstruction through cell heredity can count, but repeated expression is not enough

A unicellular propagule may inherit lower-level information that reconstructs a multicellular phenotype; ordinary sexual cycles and snowflake yeast show that neither direct preservation of the mature form nor a separate collective genome is necessary. The higher-level claim requires inherited lower-level differences to predict offspring collective differences along a collective genealogy. A monomorphic repeated phenotype supplies developmental fidelity but no estimable heritable variation; independent evolutionary runs are not collective generations; and shared environmental causes can create resemblance without genealogical transmission. Cell genotype should therefore be retained as a possible transmission pathway, while same-environment nonparent comparisons distinguish it from external recurrence. [Herron, Zamani-Dahaj & Ratcliff 2018](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-018-0612-6), [Kruuk & Hadfield 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1420-9101.2007.01377.x), [Q021](Q021_MINIMAL_COLLECTIVE_HEREDITY_TEST.md)

## K134 — Heredity and differential reproductive consequence are sequential tests

Collective heredity can exist for a neutral trait, and differential collective persistence can occur without generational transmission. Only after a pedigree and variable transmitted trait are established should the same lower-level event history be mapped to each collective's descendant contribution. A Price-style covariance plus transmission account can then test predicted change in the collective-trait distribution without installing an explicit group-fitness variable in the simulator. An observed response is strong evidence for a useful higher-level Darwinian description, but it is not required for the narrower heredity claim and does not by itself establish autonomy or adaptation. [Frank 2012](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1420-9101.2012.02498.x), [Bourrat 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-023-04285-1), [Q021](Q021_MINIMAL_COLLECTIVE_HEREDITY_TEST.md)

## K135 — Fernandes requires event-level collective-lineage instrumentation for the minimal heredity assay

The manuscript reports instantaneous clusters, sampled cell positions, cellular phylogenies, cell GRN inheritance, and one representative source-cluster → propagule → newborn-cluster event. It does not report population-wide propagule origins, persistent collective birth/split/merge identities, descendants that later reproduce, or paired parent–offspring collective phenotypes. Periodic non-evolutionary snapshots are not demonstrably sufficient to reconstruct every event. The minimum decisive analysis is one pedigree-indexed concatenated transmission assay for realized propagule-production organization, using same-environment nonparents as a control. Unless a complete undocumented trajectory archive exists, new lineage recording is required. Q021's decision is **C — NEW LINEAGE INSTRUMENTATION REQUIRED**, mechanism readiness is **NOT READY**, and no new hypothesis is justified. [Fernandes, Vroomans & Colizzi 2026, uncertified preprint v3](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199), [Q021](Q021_MINIMAL_COLLECTIVE_HEREDITY_TEST.md)

## K136 — A missing evidential conjunction is not automatically a missing mechanism

Q013's initial conjunction combined level blindness, unprescribed selection, collective reproduction/heredity/selection, and ablation/rescue. ## K137 — Instrumentation dependence is a research stop condition, not theoretical depth

When independent theory supplies a falsifiable criterion but published data lack the events needed to evaluate it, the residual is an empirical validation gap and, if those events were not recorded, an instrumentation gap. Additional terminology or theoretical subdivision does not resolve the missing evidence. Under the workspace's no-implementation policy, the question should retain provenance in an empirically blocked section and downstream questions depending on the same histories should not remain active theoretical frontiers. ## K138 — Strong niche-construction theory already includes initiation of new selection

Niche construction can initiate as well as modify selection on the constructor, unrelated traits, descendants, and other species. Its operational pathway distinguishes a constructor, organism-mediated environmental change, altered selection on a recipient, and a caused evolutionary response; “inceptive” niche construction explicitly covers novel constructed sources of selection. Ecological inheritance can persist a modified selective niche or organism–environment feature–factor relation across generations. “Creation of a new selection pressure” is therefore established NCT prior art, not a distinct OEE mechanism. [Laland, Odling-Smee & Feldman 1999](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.96.18.10242), [Matthews et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1890/13-0953.1), [Laland, Odling-Smee & Endler 2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsfs.2016.0147), [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md)

## K139 — Constructors, recipients, and purely ecological beneficiaries are already separated

NCT allows the constructor and recipient of modified selection to be the same or different populations and requires a caused evolutionary response only for evolution by niche construction. Ecosystem engineering separately covers physical creation or modification of habitat and resource availability for other species even when effects are trivial, do not feed back to the engineer, or cause no evolutionary response. A new beneficiary is thus an established ecological or niche-construction relation, not an independent selection-structure category. [Jones, Lawton & Shachak 1997](https://doi.org/10.1890/0012-9658(1997)078%5B1946:PANEOO%5D2.0.CO;2), [Matthews et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1890/13-0953.1), [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md)

## K140 — Ecological roles and network edges require domain-specific operationalization

Resource use, trophic transfer, host use, mutualistic service, habitat occupancy, partner dependence, and indirect community effects have established operational treatments, but “ecological role” has no general identity criterion. Ecological-network zeros can represent forbidden links, failed co-occurrence, rare encounter, missed detection, or thresholding; binary topology discards interaction probabilities and strengths. Edge addition or deletion therefore cannot by itself define a new evolutionary kind. [Poisot et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12468), [Jordano 2016](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.12763), [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md)

## K141 — Historically new interactions do not imply evolutionary construction

Ecological fitting allows organisms to persist in novel environments, use new resources, or form new species associations through traits already carried at encounter. Migration, disturbance, range change, or stochastic encounter can therefore activate a new fitness-relevant interaction without an evolved trait constructing it. Conversely, interaction-origin theory and experiments already cover evolved origins of mutualism, antagonism, host use, and trophic links. A bounded construction claim must show that a specified evolved change is necessary for the recipient-specific selection effect under matched encounter conditions. [Agosta & Klemens 2008](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1461-0248.2008.01237.x), [Hansen et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05514), [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md)

## K142 — Dependency and obligacy are established outcomes with established mechanisms

Adaptive gene loss, relaxed selection, drift, specialization, partner fidelity, cotransmission, and trade-offs can move lineages from facultative association toward background-relative obligacy. Black Queen theory explicitly generates helper–beneficiary dependencies when costly leaky functions become dispensable to recipients, and experimental syntrophy shows rapid loss of independent function. Such dependence can reduce autonomy, persistence outside the partnership, or evolutionary rescue; it is not inherently a new causal ontology, increased evolvability, or OEE. [Morris, Lenski & Zinser 2012](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.00036-12), [Hillesland et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1407986111), [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md)

## K143 — Irrelevance-to-relevance is a conditional causal test, not a new mechanism

Within a declared ecological model, environmental and trait manipulations can distinguish a change in the magnitude of an existing selection pathway from a factor becoming detectably causal for differential reproduction. Selection-agent methodology already supplies this logic. The result remains relative to chosen entities, traits, fitness outcomes, ecological backgrounds, interventions, timescales, and resolution; structural-causal notation cannot select these. A successful test identifies an evolved constructed source or agent of selection, not a universal “selection-structure” transition. [Wade & Kalisz 1990](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1990.tb04301.x), [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md)

## K144 — Q022 dissolves F5 as a distinct mechanism frontier

NCT, ecological inheritance, ecosystem engineering, ecological opportunity, ecological fitting, frequency/density dependence, coevolution, and interaction/dependency evolution jointly absorb every scientifically defensible single-transition reading of “creation of new selection structure.” The Q022 decision is **A — EXISTING THEORY SUFFICIENT**; mechanism readiness is **NOT READY**; the remaining issue is case-specific causal validation; and no hypothesis is justified. One such ecological transition would not establish OEE in any event. [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md)

## K145 — Evolvability determinants and their evolution are established prior art

Mutation and recombination processes, genetic and developmental architecture, robustness, modularity, cryptic variation, and adaptive-landscape structure can alter the production of heritable adaptive variation. The genotype–phenotype map and the architecture producing variation can themselves evolve. Consequently, neither “the generator changes” nor “evolution improves later accessibility” identifies a new OEE mechanism. [Wagner & Altenberg 1996](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1996.tb02339.x), [Hansen 2006](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.ecolsys.37.091305.110224), [Payne & Wagner 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41576-018-0069-z), [Q023](Q023_SUSTAINED_ACCESSIBILITY_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K146 — Facilitated variation and evolutionary generalization remain scope-relative

Structured environmental variation can evolve modularity, weak regulatory linkage, reduced pleiotropy, and regulatory architectures that generate adaptive variants efficiently while preserving robustness. Generalization to held-out challenges has been demonstrated when those challenges reuse a supplied set or distribution of subgoals. Such results establish more than replay of one phenotype, but not access after the target-generating basis or developmental organization itself changes. [Kashtan & Alon 2005](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0503610102), [Crombach & Hogeweg 2008](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000112), [Parter, Kashtan & Alon 2008](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000206), [Q023](Q023_SUSTAINED_ACCESSIBILITY_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K147 — Robustness, potentiation, and modifiers have no unconditional retention effect

Mutational robustness can facilitate or impede adaptation depending on population size, mutation rate, recombination, and landscape structure. Mutator and other evolvability modifiers can rise through linkage to beneficial descendants, but recombination, direct costs, and deleterious load can break or reverse that advantage. Cryptic variation and historical potentiation can open adaptive paths while remaining target- and background-specific. None has a universal positive sign for retaining future access. [Draghi et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08694), [Díaz Arenas & Cooper 2013](https://doi.org/10.1111/1574-6976.12008), [Blount, Borland & Lenski 2008](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0803151105), [Zheng, Payne & Wagner 2019](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax1837), [Q023](Q023_SUSTAINED_ACCESSIBILITY_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K148 — Scalable composition solves a supplied recursive family, not endogenous continuation

Recursive reuse of adapted components can prevent search difficulty from increasing on a scale-invariant hierarchical task. This is explicit scalable-evolvability prior art. Its canonical model supplies the composition operation, hierarchy, and recursively decomposable fitness landscape, so it does not explain why evolution should preserve access when later challenges do not reuse that decomposition or when the organization generating variation changes unpredictably. [Watson & Pollack 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0303-2647(02)00135-1), [Q023](Q023_SUSTAINED_ACCESSIBILITY_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K149 — Sustained accessibility can be audited as a chain of local causal claims

No universal phenotype catalogue, evolutionary clock, resource currency, or identity relation is required to ask whether a specified organization improves resource-bounded access at one stage. A sequence of intact-versus-ablated or ancestral comparisons can establish repeated local causal effects under stage-specific target families and budgets. It does not yield a universal scalar or make unmatched novelty classes comparable, and repetition of the assay is not itself a generating mechanism. [Q009](Q009_ACCESSIBILITY_SCALING.md), [Q010](Q010_MEASUREMENT_INVARIANCE.md), [Q023](Q023_SUSTAINED_ACCESSIBILITY_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K150 — F3 retains a conditional mechanism gap, not a hypothesis

Established theory absorbs fixed-target accessibility, one evolved change in genetic/developmental architecture, and repeated compositional search over a supplied hierarchy. No reviewed framework establishes a general process that repeatedly retains broad-scope access-enhancing organization despite delayed benefits, direct costs, broken linkage, specialization, and changes in the organization producing variation. Q023 classifies this narrow residual as **DISTINCT MECHANISM GAP** with **CONDITIONALLY READY** status. No specific causal relation survives strongly enough to justify a hypothesis. [Q023](Q023_SUSTAINED_ACCESSIBILITY_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K151 — Evolutionary generalization learns shared structure, not arbitrary future tasks

Parter-style modularly varying goals and Kouvaris-style evolutionary connectionism demonstrate transfer to unselected phenotypes when training and test challenges share a supplied compositional language or statistical structure. Valiant-style evolvability with drifting targets permits indefinite tracking under bounded target drift, but retains a fixed concept class, distribution, representation class, and neighborhood. These results establish substantial within-family generalization, not preparedness after every possible replacement of task and representation. [Parter, Kashtan & Alon 2008](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000206), [Kouvaris et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005358), [Kanade, Valiant & Vaughan 2010](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3566), [Q024](Q024_BROAD_SCOPE_EVOLVABILITY.md)

## K152 — Frank's regulatory-dimensionality proposal remains class-relative

Frank proposes that overparameterized regulatory circuits should generalize better across novel challenges and predicts broader generalization in more highly parameterized and older biological circuits. The proposal evaluates performance over an abstract class or potential set of environments and does not formalize replacement of task variables, developmental primitives, inheritance channels, or modifier–descendant associations. It therefore strengthens an evolutionary-generalization research program but does not supply cross-structural retention. [Frank 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/evolut/qpag111), [Q024](Q024_BROAD_SCOPE_EVOLVABILITY.md)

## K153 — Indirect selection requires association, not foresight or one privileged mechanism

A costly evolvability modifier can spread or persist through direct current benefits or statistical association with selected descendants. Tight physical linkage and recurrent environmental structure are sufficient routes in some models but are not individually necessary; clonal descent, assortment, population structure, cotransmission, and present functional benefit can also preserve association. If a change in developmental or hereditary organization destroys every such relation, selection cannot retain the modifier because of benefits to causally unrelated future variants. [Carja, Liberman & Feldman 2014](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1417664111), [Altenberg, Liberman & Feldman 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1619655114), [Q024](Q024_BROAD_SCOPE_EVOLVABILITY.md)

## K154 — Universal broad-scope evolvability violates a no-free-lunch boundary

Systematic performance beyond selected histories requires a restriction or relation connecting those histories to later challenges. Without a priori distinctions among possible learning problems there is no universal off-training-set advantage; biological evolution likewise cannot select present organization for wholly unrelated arbitrary future demands. A richer regularity linking regimes restores a coherent problem, but makes the claim conditional generalization over that larger process. This rules out preparedness for arbitrary novelty as an OEE requirement without ruling out repeated local reconstruction of evolvability. [Wolpert 1996](https://doi.org/10.1162/neco.1996.8.7.1341), [Ben-David et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10994-009-5152-4), [Q024](Q024_BROAD_SCOPE_EVOLVABILITY.md)

## K155 — Q024 closes F3 as a general mechanism frontier

Evolution of evolvability explains changing variation generators; modifier theory explains retention through current effects and associations; facilitated variation explains future versatility as a by-product of current utility; evolutionary generalization explains transfer under shared structure; and developmental or major-transition theory explains local changes in developmental and hereditary organization. What remains is to specify the transmitted correspondence or environmental regularity connecting declared regimes. Q024 therefore decides **E — NO-FREE-LUNCH LIMIT**, assigns **NOT READY**, classifies the residue as **FORMALIZATION**, and justifies no hypothesis. [Q024](Q024_BROAD_SCOPE_EVOLVABILITY.md)

## K156 — Organizational closure already combines relative stability with changing realizers

Closure-of-constraints theory grounds present biological stability in mutual maintenance among constraints defined at relevant timescales, not in persistence of every material component. Its companion variation theory explicitly permits constraints to change, disappear, or become integrated while closure is re-established in successive organized regimes. This supplies a principled description of organization changing while remaining organized, but not a universal population-genetic mechanism, heredity criterion, or OEE guarantee. [Montévil & Mossio 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2015.02.029), [Montévil et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pbiomolbio.2016.08.005), [Q025](Q025_SELF_MAINTAINING_PLASTICITY.md)

## K157 — Developmental-system drift separates trait identity from mechanism identity

Homologous traits can remain conserved while their genetic and regulatory underpinnings diverge. Robust neutral change, mutation, loss or addition of components, and selection on pleiotropically related traits followed by compensatory evolution are established routes. DSD is therefore an outcome class with several mechanisms: extensive developmental rewiring can preserve trait identity without generating novelty, a new evolutionary individual, or OEE. [True & Haag 2001](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1525-142x.2001.003002109.x), [McColgan & DiFrisco 2024](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.203054), [Q025](Q025_SELF_MAINTAINING_PLASTICITY.md)

## K158 — Plasticity can bridge change without maintaining itself

Phenotypic accommodation can make an induced phenotype viable before genetic change, after which selection can genetically accommodate the response. Quantitative theory permits an initial rise in plasticity after an extreme environmental shift followed by genetic assimilation and reduced plasticity. Thus evolutionary continuity may use a transient plastic bridge; persistence does not require indefinite plasticity, and plasticity alone does not establish transformational novelty. [West-Eberhard 2005](https://doi.org/10.1002/jez.b.21071), [Lande 2009](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1420-9101.2009.01754.x), [Q025](Q025_SELF_MAINTAINING_PLASTICITY.md)

## K159 — Identity-preserving bridges are claim-specific and already causal

Redundancy or compensation can preserve a phenotype during developmental change; regulation and closure can preserve current viability; plasticity can preserve performance during an environmental shift; and life cycles, bottlenecks, ecological scaffolding, and endogenization can help establish a higher-level hereditary individual. Each supports a domain-specific counterfactual intervention, but the outcomes and identity relations differ. Adding the mechanisms together does not produce a further causal variable called “self-maintaining plasticity.” [Draghi et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08694), [Black, Bourrat & Rainey 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-1086-9), [Bourrat 2025](https://doi.org/10.1086/719118), [Szathmáry 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421398112), [Q025](Q025_SELF_MAINTAINING_PLASTICITY.md)

## K160 — Q025 closes F4 as terminological repackaging

F4 conflates persistence of a material token, maintenance of an organized function, trait homology, genealogical continuity, and continuity of an evolutionary individual. Once the bearer and identity relation are declared, closure/regulation, developmental-system drift, genetic accommodation, robustness/evolvability, or transition theory supplies the relevant causal candidates. Without such a declaration the residue is definition/formalization, not a missing mechanism. Q025 decides **TERMINOLOGICAL REPACKAGING**, assigns **NOT READY**, and justifies no hypothesis. [Q025](Q025_SELF_MAINTAINING_PLASTICITY.md)

## K161 — Major-transition theory supplies event mechanisms, not a recurrence law

Major-transition and ETI theory explain specified transitions using changes in information transmission, cooperation, multilevel selection, life cycles, bottlenecks, conflict mediation, division of labour, ecological scaffolding, and integration. Some conditions recur within transition classes, but no reviewed theory establishes that completion of one transition regenerates the causes of a later qualitatively different transition. The major-transition classifications also include non-hierarchical changes such as sex, coding, and language, so repeated regime transformation is broader than nested individuality. [Maynard Smith & Szathmáry 1995](https://academic.oup.com/book/40636), [Szathmáry 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421398112), [West et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421402112), [Jablonka & Lamb 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2005.08.038), [Q026](Q026_REPEATED_REGIME_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K162 — Ongoing major transitions are a hallmark; EOE is an explanatory perspective

The York OEE synthesis treats ongoing major transitions as an especially strong OEE hallmark while separating hallmarks from candidate mechanisms. Pattee and Sayama's evolved-open-endedness view makes open-endedness-enabling conditions evolutionary outcomes and organizes established mechanism families and historical questions; it does not specify a recurrence-specific causal variable. Q026 therefore classifies EOE as **EXPLANATORY PERSPECTIVE**, not mechanism. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Pattee & Sayama 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00276), [Q026](Q026_REPEATED_REGIME_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K163 — Cultural evolved open-endedness does not require repeated regime change

Cumulative culture can be supported by a conjunction of social learning, teaching, language, cooperation, institutions, recombination, externalized information, and resource-supported search. Cultural-system/search-space coevolution can generate open-ended-looking growth under one conditional macro-level process. This makes cultural evolution a useful EOE stress test but does not establish repeated changes of cultural heredity, reproduction, or individuality or a general recurrence mechanism. [Borg et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00406), [Tennie, Call & Tomasello 2009](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2009.0052), [Winters & Charbonneau 2026](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2025.0255), [Q026](Q026_REPEATED_REGIME_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K164 — Stabilization does not regenerate transition conditions

Ecological scaffolding can initiate collective selection, evolutionary hysteresis can endogenize a scaffolded state, and ratcheting can suppress reversion by reducing revertant fitness or accessibility. These mechanisms explain entry into and retention of one regime. They do not generally create conditions for a subsequent different transition and can instead reduce local transformability. Thus irreversibility is not open-endedness. [Black, Bourrat & Rainey 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-1086-9), [Doulcier et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-50625-1), [Libby et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2015.0444), [Q026](Q026_REPEATED_REGIME_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K165 — A finite transition sequence does not establish recurrence capacity

A finite history of regime changes is compatible with a long transient ending in a stable regime. Non-absorption can be defined only relative to declared dynamics and transition classes, is observationally confounded with very long waiting times, and adds no mechanism beyond conditional accessibility unless a causal generator is specified. F2 remains semantically distinct from ordinary adaptation because its targets are changes in evolutionary relations, but a general probability of later transitions inherits F3's scope and no-free-lunch boundaries. [Q003](Q003_CAPACITY_AND_OBSERVER.md), [Q009](Q009_ACCESSIBILITY_SCALING.md), [Q024](Q024_BROAD_SCOPE_EVOLVABILITY.md), [Q026](Q026_REPEATED_REGIME_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K166 — Q026 closes F2 as requiring no general recurrence mechanism

Transition-specific mechanisms, evolved enabling conditions, key innovations, evolution of evolvability, and endogenous or exogenous historical contingency absorb the demand for a common recurrence explanation. Their conjunction is not a new mechanism, and requiring every regime to generate the cause of the next is unsupported. Q026 decides **B — NO GENERAL RECURRENCE MECHANISM REQUIRED**, assigns **NOT READY**, classifies the live residue as **VALIDATION** of particular cross-transition prerequisite claims, and justifies no hypothesis. This does not establish that every historical transition is explained or that natural evolution must eventually close. [Q026](Q026_REPEATED_REGIME_TRANSFORMATION.md)

## K167 — Material, genealogical, hereditary, selective, and process continuity are distinct

Material contribution can occur without a higher-level hereditary lineage; traits can be reconstructed despite turnover of focal material carriers; selection can pause during dormancy; and two causally unrelated systems can realize the same abstract Darwinian roles. Heredity, reproduction, and selection can also be assigned to non-coextensive units. Consequently, no one of these continuity relations is universally necessary or sufficient for every evolutionary transition. [Godfrey-Smith 2009](https://academic.oup.com/book/4808), [Veigl, Suárez & Stencel 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-022-03810-y), [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md)

## K168 — Major transitions need not contain an instantaneous Darwinian handoff

Darwinian-population and ETI theories permit transition intervals in which lower-level variation and selection continue while higher-level heredity, reproduction, and selection become increasingly well defined. Life-cycle accounts derive candidate higher-level stages from lower-level trajectories and require multiplication plus cycle closure. These relations can connect the processes without preserving one unit, but the higher-level interpretation still requires biological domain justification. [Godfrey-Smith 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547265/), [Doulcier, Takacs & Bourrat 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.162), [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md)

## K169 — Fitness transfer and Price mappings do not supply a conserved bridge variable

Particle and collective fitness can be defined on different entities and timescales; fitness reorganization during an ETI is not conservation or literal transfer of one quantity. The Price equation is exact after an ancestor–descendant or set mapping is supplied, but does not select the biologically meaningful entities, traits, levels, or causal interpretation. Cross-level fitness comparisons and formal mappings must therefore be justified independently or left undefined. [Bourrat 2015](https://doi.org/10.3998/ptb.6959004.0007.001), [Bourrat et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73715), [Okasha & Otsuka 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7133506/), [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md)

## K170 — Reproducer and reconstitutor accounts ground different partial continuities

Griesemer's reproducer framework uses material overlap carrying developmental capacity to ground biological reproduction. The reconstitutor framework instead grounds trait recurrence in difference-making processes that recreate a variant and separates Stability of Traits from Stability of Lineages. Material overlap is neither sufficient for higher-level heredity nor general across cultural and ecological inheritance; trait reconstitution without a justified ancestry or source–recipient history is insufficient for a cumulative evolutionary lineage. [Griesemer 2000](https://doi.org/10.1556/Select.1.2000.1-3.7), [Veigl, Suárez & Stencel 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-022-03810-y), [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md)

## K171 — Evolutionary ancestry can be reticulate, non-hierarchical, and reversible

Symbiogenesis, aggregation, fusion, hybridization, horizontal transmission, and sex defeat a universal one-parent–one-lineage criterion. Major-transition classifications also include changes in information transmission without a new higher-level individual. Paradigmatic and agential reversions show that organizational and selective-level change need not be monotonic. A continuity account must therefore admit partial many-to-one and one-to-many relations and cannot require a permanently ascending hierarchy. [Maynard Smith & Szathmáry 1995](https://academic.oup.com/book/40636), [Godfrey-Smith 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547265/), [Schenkel, Ågren & Patten 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41604221/), [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md)

## K172 — Bare historical causation is weaker than evolutionary continuity

A later evolutionary system can be causally enabled by an earlier system despite total loss of the earlier system's genealogy, heredity, reproduction, and reconstructive relations. Such adjacency is not accumulated evolutionary continuity. Conversely, inactivity during dormancy does not break a lineage when ancestry and developmental capacity persist. The defensible minimal schema is a domain-justified ancestry, transmission, reproduction, reconstruction, or selected-contribution relation connecting the earlier process to a later organization that supports its own Darwinian relations. This is a relational classification of the causal history, not a new mechanism. [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md)

## K173 — Q027 closes continuity across changing units as an independent theoretical frontier

Multilevel overlap, causal ancestry, reproduction and reconstruction, life-cycle closure, reticulate histories, and transition-specific domain theory absorb the continuity question. No persistent unit, conserved fitness, or conserved information is required; arbitrary causal succession is insufficient. Q027 therefore decides **A — EXISTING RELATIONAL THEORY SUFFICIENT**, assigns **NOT READY**, classifies the residue as **VALIDATION** of case-specific partial mappings, and justifies no hypothesis. [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md)

## K174 — Q022–Q027 exhaust the repository's current general mechanism families

F5 is closed by established ecological theory; F3 by conditionality and no-free-lunch limits; F4 as terminological repackaging; F2 because heterogeneous transition histories require no general recurrence mechanism; and F1 by decomposition into domain theories with an empirically blocked residue. Q027 additionally closes cross-unit continuity through existing relational theory. This is **frontier-family exhaustion**, not evidence that OEE itself has been explained. [Q022](Q022_ECOLOGICAL_SELECTION_STRUCTURE.md), [Q024](Q024_BROAD_SCOPE_EVOLVABILITY.md), [Q025](Q025_SELF_MAINTAINING_PLASTICITY.md), [Q026](Q026_REPEATED_REGIME_TRANSFORMATION.md), [Q027](Q027_CONTINUITY_ACROSS_CHANGING_UNITS.md), [Synthesis 003](SYNTHESIS_003_Q022_Q027.md)

## K175 — OEE is represented in current field literature by multiple behavioral hallmarks

The OEE literature distinguishes ongoing adaptive novelty, complexity-related growth, evolving evolvability, major transitions, semantic evolution, and exploratory/expansive/transformational novelty, and reports less agreement about generating requirements or mechanisms than about behavioral hallmarks. No reviewed result establishes that these targets form one mechanistic natural kind or share one sufficient mechanism. A mechanism question must therefore declare which hallmark it explains. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Taylor 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00290), [Channon et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_e_00445), [Synthesis 003](SYNTHESIS_003_Q022_Q027.md)

## K176 — Transition and unit theory do not by themselves explain ongoing adaptive functional novelty

Q011–Q027 clarify how evolutionary organization, heredity, individuality, regime transformation, and cross-unit continuity can change. Those results neither imply nor explain a sustained sequence of newly originating and established adaptive functions. Genetic or spatial novelty, evolutionary activity, self-replication, and multiscale organization remain weaker evidence: Genelife explicitly reports open-ended innovation that falls short of functional biological innovation, while Flow-Lenia and Outlier establish different substrate, activity, and replication results without independently establishing ongoing adaptive functional novelty. [Packard & McCaskill 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00426), [Plantec et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00471), [Hintze & Bohm 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-026-00074-2), [Synthesis 003](SYNTHESIS_003_Q022_Q027.md)

## K177 — Novelty, function, adaptation, and ongoingness are empirically separable

The cross-system matrix finds all required dissociations: Outlier supplies causal self-replication without established heritable adaptive variation; Genelife supplies continuing genetic and spatial novelty without established function; Evoloops supplies intrinsic adaptation without established qualitative functional novelty; and DISHTINY supplies causally validated adaptive collective functions without a lineage-ordered series of new functions. Avida additionally shows complex functional acquisition inside an explicitly enumerated task vocabulary. Therefore reproduction, novelty, function, adaptation, and ongoingness require separate evidence. The same questions can be asked across systems, but the novelty objects and causal measures must remain system-specific. [Sayama 1999](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/ALIFE5-4-343.pdf), [Lenski et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568), [Moreno & Ofria 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2022.750837), [Packard & McCaskill 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00426), [Hintze & Bohm 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-026-00074-2), [Evidence Matrix 001](EVIDENCE_MATRIX_001_ADAPTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_NOVELTY.md)

## K178 — No reviewed artificial system establishes ongoing adaptive functional novelty

At the cross-system matrix checkpoint, no row had strong evidence for multiple temporally separated events combining historical novelty, causal function, heritable adaptation, and functional-class distinctness. Stringmol was the strongest candidate because its spatial host–parasite histories support intrinsic selection and evolved replication slowing, binding changes, recognition, reciprocal execution, hypercycles, and mutation-process changes. Validation 001 subsequently resolves a finite two-event recurrence in Stringmol (K180) but not a continuing post-discrimination sequence (K181). Thus ongoing AFN remains unestablished, and non-observation remains a validation/measurement limit rather than evidence of system incapacity. [Hickinbotham, Stepney & Hogeweg 2021](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.210441), [Stepney & Hickinbotham 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00399), [Evidence Matrix 001](EVIDENCE_MATRIX_001_ADAPTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_NOVELTY.md), [Validation 001](VALIDATION_001_STRINGMOL_ADAPTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_NOVELTY.md)

## K179 — Adaptive-functional-novelty evidence has heterogeneous proximate bottlenecks

Every reviewed system lacks strong ongoingness evidence, but this common terminal gap does not identify one shared causal failure. The earlier break differs: adaptation for Outlier; function for Genelife; intrinsic entity/function attribution for Flow-Lenia; functional-class novelty for Evoloops; temporal recurrence under strong pre-enablement for DISHTINY; continued post-discrimination functional recurrence for Stringmol after Validation 001; and unenumerated relevance for Avida. Evidence Matrix 001 therefore decides **B — HETEROGENEOUS BOTTLENECKS** and selects system-specific validation of the strongest near-positive case rather than a common-mechanism search. AFN requires genuine scheme-independent interpretive validation and richer recurrence histories; complexity requires strong same-object temporal/scaling evidence; and the global exemplar problem requires a compelling auditable positive history. `Empirical boundary` is therefore a valid research-scale umbrella for dependence on unavailable evidence, not a claim that the boundaries are operationally identical or that the OEE field is exhausted. ## K244 — Mature evidence guidance does not make reporting the main global explanation

The EX2 architecture is retrospectively validated, low-overhead, historically fair, and compatible with every reviewed substrate. It exposes consequential run/history, provenance, semantics, censoring, selection, and validation losses. Those losses can hide or weaken claims locally, but better reporting cannot produce ongoing AFN, create a strong same-object S/I conjunction, recruit independent validators, or generate a broad artificial-OEE exemplar. Broad exemplar scarcity therefore cannot be attributed mainly to reporting within the reviewed program, while genuine phenomenon scarcity remains unproved. ## K245 — The repository establishes no general cause of open-endedness versus closure

The durable results constrain explanations but do not identify a general causal relation. Ecology, resource scaling, major transitions, self-reference, autopoiesis, compositionality, and meta-evolution are not established as causes of OEE. Aevol `(S2,I0)` and Geb `(S1,I4)` differ in substrate, complexity semantics, evolutionary architecture, resource structure, and protocol, so their comparison is descriptive rather than a causal mechanism contrast. The broader component-exemplar mosaic is likewise pedagogically useful but causally underdetermined.
