# Evidence Matrix 002 — Complexity Growth

## 1. Why complexity growth was selected

[Synthesis 005](SYNTHESIS_005_NEXT_OEE_HALLMARK.md) paused the adaptive-functional-novelty (AFN) program at its external-independent-validation boundary and selected **H1 — COMPLEXITY GROWTH NEXT** as the highest-information remaining OEE hallmark.[^1] This artifact performs exactly the selected first operation:

\[
\boxed{\text{E1 — COMPLEXITY-GROWTH CROSS-SYSTEM EVIDENCE MATRIX}.}
\]

It does not perform Q028, reactivate AFN, search for a complexity-growth mechanism, formulate a hypothesis, run a system, or analyze new empirical data.

The exact evidence question is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{What kinds of complexity growth have actually been demonstrated in artificial evolutionary systems,}
\atop
\text{and where does the evidence chain toward ongoing nontrivial complexity growth fail?}
}
\]

## 2. Hallmark under study

**Fact.** The 2024 OEE editorial identifies ongoing adaptive novelty and ongoing complexity growth as two central behavioral hallmarks. Tokyo Type 1 combines them, but does not make either one evidence for the other.[^2]

This pass studies only:

\[
\boxed{\text{ongoing growth of complexity}.}
\]

The target is long-run continuation in the production or frontier of a declared, scientifically interpretable complexity object. Local decreases are permitted. A larger final value alone is insufficient.

The following boundaries remain canonical:

\[
\boxed{\text{complexity growth}\not\Rightarrow\text{AFN}}
\qquad
\boxed{\text{AFN}\not\Rightarrow\text{monotonic complexity growth}.}
\]

Adaptive maintenance is recorded as an evidence dimension, not imposed as a gate. Otherwise this program would silently reactivate AFN.

## 3. Why complexity is not one metric

**Fact.** The reviewed papers measure different bearers and different properties:

- Geb: diversity of active genes involved in an individual's neural development;
- Avida: genomic information about a fixed task environment, or the maximum count of fitness-informative sites among persistent genomes;
- Tierra: genotype information conditional on an ecologically defined phenotype-equivalence relation;
- Hintze's control: compressed description size of path-encoding genomes;
- Stringmol: active replication-strategy elaboration, supported by program length, reaction time, and reconstructed reaction protocols; and
- Flow-Lenia.png: multiscale compressibility of final spatial states.

These quantities cannot be placed on one magnitude scale:

\[
C_{\mathrm{Geb}}\neq C_{\mathrm{Avida}}\neq C_{\mathrm{Tierra}}
\]

in object, units, and interpretation. Cross-system comparison is nevertheless possible at the logical level:

\[
\boxed{
\text{growth evidence under a declared system-specific complexity measure}
\neq
\text{cross-system comparison of complexity magnitude}.
}
\]

**Interpretation.** The useful common object is an evidence claim: whether a predeclared measure tracks active or evolutionarily relevant organization, increases within evolutionary history, continues across the observation window, and exhibits a scalable frontier. This reuses the AFN lesson that logical comparability does not require metric commensurability.

## 4. Evidence decomposition

Each system is audited through:

\[
\boxed{X\rightarrow M\rightarrow E\rightarrow G\rightarrow S}
\]

where:

- **X — Complexity object specified:** the bearer and measured organization are explicit.
- **M — Metric scientifically interpretable:** the measure distinguishes the intended organization from raw amount, padding, or unused capacity.
- **E — Evolutionary grounding:** the observed change is generated inside evolutionary dynamics, with drift, selection, external optimization, and parameter scaling distinguished.
- **G — Growth:** the measure demonstrably increases over evolutionary history.
- **S — Sustainedness:** the growth persists over a substantial stated window without a demonstrated saturation in that window.

This is an evidence decomposition, not a theory or universal law.

Indefinite scalability is a separate axis:

\[
I=\text{evidence that the attainable evolutionary complexity frontier continues to increase as relevant external bounds are scaled}.
\]

A history can support \(S\) without a resource-scaling study. A family of runs can support \(I\) even when no fixed run shows ongoing growth. Neither substitutes for the other.

The local evidence levels survive as descriptive labels:

- **C0 — SIZE / RESOURCE GROWTH ONLY**
- **C1 — VALID MEASURED COMPLEXITY**, with temporal growth not established
- **C2 — EVOLUTIONARY COMPLEXITY INCREASE**
- **C3 — SUSTAINED COMPLEXITY GROWTH** over a stated finite window
- **C4 — INDEFINITELY SCALABLE COMPLEXITY FRONTIER** to the tested extent
- **C5 — STRONGER / HIGHER-ORDER ONGOING COMPLEXITY GROWTH**, only when directly supported

C3 and C4 answer different questions; the labels are not a single total ordering.

## 5. Triviality firewall

**Fact.** Hintze's deliberately simple model evolves genomes over five million generations in an unbounded two-dimensional path space. Genome compressibility and sequence length grow, and an exponential trend fits the observed compression proxy better than the tested saturating alternative. Yet the paper's conclusion is that weak OEE requirements let an evolutionarily underwhelming system pass and that definitions of complexity and diversity do the decisive work.[^9]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{unbounded numerical metric growth}\not\Rightarrow\text{interesting OEE complexity}.}
\]

The firewall rejects a positive hallmark inference when growth can be accounted for only as:

- neutral padding or junk code;
- unused genes, instructions, neurons, or cells;
- a larger genome, world, population, or runtime without greater active organization;
- direct relaxation of an external ceiling;
- accumulation of visited states, identifiers, counters, or records; or
- incompressibility that does not distinguish organized structure from noise.

The requirement that blocks the Hintze false positive is **M**, not **E**, **G**, or finite-window **S**. Selection genuinely produces longer, less-compressible path strings, but the measure does not demonstrate increasing organized capability beyond ever-longer trajectories in a fixed three-symbol behavior language. This is a principled metric-object failure, not an ad hoc exclusion of the control.

## 6. Systems included

Six systems fill distinct evidential roles:

1. **Geb** — the strongest published indefinite-scalability calibration for active individual complexity.
2. **Avida** — the strongest reviewed time-resolved increase under an explicitly information-bearing genomic measure.
3. **Tierra** — a peer-reviewed size-without-complexity negative control.
4. **Hintze 2019** — an adversarial formal-growth-without-interesting-organization control.
5. **Stringmol** — a non-monotonic gain/loss/regain case in active replication organization.
6. **Flow-Lenia.png** — a continuous-morphology contrast in which compression-based state complexity is directly optimized by an external genetic algorithm.

No further systems are added. MODES is treated as methodological evidence rather than a system row because its NK and Avida demonstrations validate metric behavior, not a new evolving platform.[^6]

## 7. Complexity-object operationalizations

### 7.1 Geb

```text
System: Geb
Complexity bearer: Individual embodied agent
Complexity object: ACTIVE PROGRAM COMPONENTS / INDIVIDUAL ORGANIZATION
Metric: Number of different active genes involved in the agent's neural development
What an increase means: A developed individual uses a larger diversity of distinct developmental genes in constructing its neural controller
What an increase does NOT mean: More available neurons, more genome material, richer behavior, new functional classes, AFN, or one fixed world growing forever
Evolutionary grounding: Components and individuals arise under Geb's biotically selected evolutionary dynamics; the 2019 study compares maxima across replicated parameter-scaled runs
Temporal statistic: Maximum individual complexity; in the precursor analysis, late-run values were averaged over time steps 2,000,000–3,000,000 and over 20 runs per parameter combination
Resource bounds: World length L (population at most L^2), maximum neurons per individual, run time, and computer memory
Environment: FIXED substrate with ENDOGENOUSLY CHANGING biotic context
Scalability evidence: Joint scaling of world length and maximum neurons yields successively higher maxima, logarithmic in the lower limiting resource, to the tested extent
Main validity concern: Active-gene count ignores interaction structure and behavior; cross-run scalable maxima do not establish one fixed-history ongoing trajectory
Primary sources: Channon 2019; Channon OEE2/OEE3 reports
```

The exact active-gene interpretation and two co-limiting resource bounds are explicit in Channon's primary reports.[^3][^4]

### 7.2 Avida

```text
System: Avida
Complexity bearer: Digital organism genome; in MODES, a persistent genome/component
Complexity object: GENOMIC INFORMATION / FITNESS-INFORMATIVE PROGRAM SITES
Metric: Physical complexity C = genome length minus estimated per-site entropy; MODES uses the maximum number of fitness-informative sites among persistent components
What an increase means: More genome sites are constrained by, and store information about, the declared environment; under MODES, more null-sensitive meaningful sites occur in the most complex persistent genome
What an increase does NOT mean: Longer genome, arbitrary code, universal organismal complexity, a new task family, unbounded growth, or indefinite scalability
Evolutionary grounding: Mutation, heredity, and differential replication in Avida; the 2000 experiment used a fixed 100-instruction genome and fixed environment
Temporal statistic: Approximate genomic complexity through 100,000 updates (roughly 10,000–20,000 generations); MODES tracks population maximum at sampled timepoints after a forward persistence filter
Resource bounds: Fixed 100-instruction genome in the 2000 experiment, finite 28-instruction alphabet, finite task/environmental information, population, memory, and observation time
Environment: FIXED; the focal information-rich treatment has a fixed repertoire of rewarded computations
Scalability evidence: None in the reviewed studies
Main validity concern: Per-site approximation omits epistasis unless separately assayed; finite genome and environmental information bound the measure
Primary sources: Adami, Ofria & Collier 2000; Dolson et al. 2019
```

Adami et al. define genomic complexity as information stored about the environment, approximate it from per-site substitution entropy, hold sequence length at 100 instructions, and report a steadily increasing trajectory with local post-transition relaxation.[^5] MODES later removes sites whose null substitution does not reduce fitness, filters for descendant persistence, and measures the maximum informative-site count among surviving components.[^6]

### 7.3 Tierra

```text
System: Tierra
Complexity bearer: Digital organism genotype interpreted through its interaction phenotype
Complexity object: GENOME / PROGRAM INFORMATION CONDITIONAL ON PHENOTYPIC EQUIVALENCE
Metric: Neutral-equivalence complexity estimated by C2; C_NV counts non-volatile sites and C_ss uses single-site neutral alternatives as tractable proxies
What an increase means: Fewer same-length genotypes are phenotypically equivalent under the declared pairwise-interaction tournament context
What an increase does NOT mean: Longer genome, more occupied memory, more instructions executed, or greater ecological complexity
Evolutionary grounding: Mutation and competition in a size-neutral Tierra run; archetypes are recovered from the run and tested in pairwise tournaments
Temporal statistic: Complexity of 26 archetypal phenotypes by first appearance across 6 x 10^9 executed instructions
Resource bounds: Finite soup memory, 32-instruction alphabet, finite sampled archetype set, pairwise tournament context, and run time
Environment: FIXED substrate with ENDOGENOUSLY CHANGING organism interactions
Scalability evidence: None
Main validity concern: Complexity depends strongly on the phenotype-equivalence context; the expensive neutral-network estimate is truncated and the proxy can underestimate it
Primary sources: Standish 2003; Standish 2006
```

In the 2003 size-neutral run, organism length rose from 80 to 526 instructions by \(1.4\times10^9\) executed instructions, but neither \(C_{\mathrm{NV}}\) nor the fuller \(C_2\) estimate showed a temporal increase over the \(6\times10^9\)-instruction history.[^7] A later pilot found a few finite higher-complexity organisms under intermediate parsimony settings, but explicitly did not establish open-ended complexity growth.[^8]

### 7.4 Hintze's 2019 adversarial model

```text
System: Hintze's ever-evolving path model
Complexity bearer: Genome encoding an agent trajectory
Complexity object: COMPRESSED GENOME DESCRIPTION
Metric: Zlib compressed size as a proxy for Kolmogorov complexity
What an increase means: Evolved right/left/forward strings require more bytes under the chosen compressor
What an increase does NOT mean: New behavior primitives, new organizational levels, increased active causal structure, biological complexity, or OEE
Evolutionary grounding: A Moran process selects paths inversely by prior visitation; insertion, deletion, and substitution mutate genomes
Temporal statistic: Mean genome compression proxy across 100 replicate populations over 5,000,000 generations
Resource bounds: Population fixed at 100; genome and 2D path length are unbounded in the model but bounded in execution by time and memory
Environment: MIXED — fixed evaluator plus a continually accumulated visitation record generated by prior populations
Scalability evidence: Formal extensibility and a finite-window trend, but no independent parameter-scaling test of an organized complexity frontier
Main validity concern: Longer incompressible path strings are rewarded without evidence of richer organization; the state-record and unbounded path afford a trivial growth route
Primary sources: Hintze 2019
```

The paper's own purpose and conclusion make this a control, not a disputed positive exemplar.[^9]

### 7.5 Stringmol

```text
System: Spatial Stringmol
Complexity bearer: Replicator program and its pairwise reaction protocol; secondarily the replicator-parasite ecology
Complexity object: ACTIVE REPLICATION / RECOGNITION ORGANIZATION
Metric: No single validated scalar; median/IQR program length and reaction execution time plus reconstructed control flow and classified reaction properties
What an increase means: Replicators execute additional active steps and more elaborate partner-discrimination/replication protocols, sometimes with longer programs and reactions
What an increase does NOT mean: Genome length alone, species count alone, monotonic growth, a permanently retained defense, or an indefinitely expanding complexity frontier
Evolutionary grounding: Mutating variable-length programs reproduce and interact in a spatial arena under intrinsic differential replication
Temporal statistic: Population length/reaction summaries across eight surviving runs and detailed protocol reconstruction in focal histories through 2,000,000 timesteps
Resource bounds: 12,500-cell arena, finite run, finite computation/memory; a maximum string length is not reported as the focal constraint in the paper
Environment: ENDOGENOUSLY CHANGING ecological context in a fixed spatial substrate
Scalability evidence: None
Main validity concern: 'Increasingly complex' is supported by active program reconstruction but not reduced to a predeclared scalar or an upper-envelope series
Primary sources: Hickinbotham, Stepney & Hogeweg 2021
```

Of 20 runs, eight survived to two million timesteps. The authors report increasingly intricate self/non-self discrimination, later loss of costly elaborate programs when parasites became rare, and renewed parasite pressure with reversal of simplification.[^10] This is direct evidence of active organizational gain and loss, not of a sustained upward frontier.

### 7.6 Flow-Lenia.png

```text
System: Flow-Lenia.png experiments
Complexity bearer: Final multichannel Flow-Lenia spatial state rendered as an image
Complexity object: PHENOTYPE / MULTISCALE SPATIAL PATTERN
Metric: Mean inverse PNG compression ratio after resampling across declared spatial scales
What an increase means: Final patterns become less compressible across the chosen raster resolutions
What an increase does NOT mean: Intrinsic evolutionary adaptation, continuing organismal complexity growth, active organization, or OEE
Evolutionary grounding: An external genetic algorithm directly optimizes distance to specified complexity targets or extrema
Temporal statistic: Best/mean/min-max metric over 50 genetic-algorithm generations; each candidate's final state is measured after 2,000 Flow-Lenia updates
Resource bounds: 256 x 256 world, three channels, 12 kernels, declared scales, 50 candidates, 50 generations, fixed hyperparameter search space
Environment: FIXED and designer-specified
Scalability evidence: None; the reported accessible range for the fixed configuration is approximately 0.21–0.62
Main validity concern: Complexity is the explicit external objective; compressibility can reward texture/noise and does not establish active evolutionary organization
Primary sources: Adachi et al. 2024 preprint
```

The multiscale and polar-coordinate corrections improve the image measure, but the result remains a finite observed, directly optimized final-state complexity landscape rather than an intrinsic ongoing evolutionary history.[^11]

## 9. Geb

### Strongest positive interpretation

**Fact.** Geb counts different genes that remain active in neural development, thereby excluding merely available neurons and inactive potential. Maximum individual complexity is evolved under biotic selection. When either world length or maximum neurons is scaled alone, the attained maximum approaches an asymptote; when both are scaled together, successively higher maxima occur. The maximum is approximately logarithmic in the lower of maximum population size and maximum neuron count.[^3][^4]

The strongest defensible interpretation is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Geb establishes an indefinitely scalable active-component individual-complexity frontier}
\atop
\text{to the tested joint-resource range.}
}
\]

### Attack

- **Bloat:** substantially controlled because only distinct genes involved in neural development are counted.
- **Latent capacity:** additional permitted neurons are not counted unless development uses genes that create them, but the metric still counts component diversity rather than interaction topology or behavior.
- **Resource bounds:** world length and neuron count are both explicit and co-limiting.
- **Within-run growth:** the 2019 claim is built from maximum attained complexity across a family of long, scaled runs; it is not an uninterrupted trajectory in one fixed world.
- **Fixed-limit saturation:** asymptotes under one-parameter scaling diagnose co-limitation, not by themselves temporal saturation below the ceiling in every fixed run.
- **Censoring:** years-long runs and billions of reproductions are unusually strong, but remain finite.
- **Scaling:** logarithmic growth is supported only across the evaluated parameter range.

Geb therefore supports **C4** to the tested extent, with only partial evidence for the temporal **G** link and no established **S** in one fixed regime. It does not support **C5**.

## 10. Avida

### What is measured

**Fact.** Adami et al. define the physical complexity of a genome as information stored about its environment. With logarithms normalized to the instruction alphabet, per-site information is one minus site entropy and approximate genome complexity is:

\[
C\approx L-\sum_{i=1}^{L}H_i.
\]

Conserved sites in the mutation-selection ensemble contribute more information; maximally variable sites contribute none. The focal experiment fixes \(L=100\), starts from a replicator containing mostly nonsense code, and measures a fixed single-niche environment with rewarded computations.[^5]

MODES supplies a related but not identical operationalization. It nulls or removes sites, treats fitness-reducing sites as meaningful, applies a forward descendant-persistence filter, and records the largest meaningful-site count among persistent population components.[^6] Despite occasional references to “complexity potential,” the measured quantity is **realized maximum component complexity after filtering**, not unused possibility-space capacity or indefinite scalability.

### Growth result and bounds

The 2000 trajectory covers \(10^5\) updates, about \(10^4\)–\(2\times10^4\) generations at the reported five-to-ten updates per generation. Approximate complexity rises from roughly the low 50s to above 80 instructions, with local overshoot/relaxation around transitions. MODES independently reports an upward trajectory in the fixed Logic-9 environment, while its empty environment rises briefly, declines, and levels off.[^5][^6]

This establishes:

\[
\boxed{\text{finite evolutionary increase in fitness-relevant genomic information}.}
\]

It does not establish unbounded growth. In the focal experiment \(C\leq100\) by construction, and the information that can be stored about a fixed finite task environment is finite. No reviewed study scales genome and environmental information capacity in a way comparable to Geb's joint-bound study.

### Strongest positive and attack

- **Positive:** the object and metric are explicit, length is fixed, neutral code contributes entropy rather than complexity, the trajectory is time-resolved, and the measure is tied to environmental performance.
- **Metric limitation:** the per-site approximation neglects epistatic information unless multi-site effects are measured.
- **Fixed repertoire:** the environment supplies a finite set of rewarded relations; its complexity is not expanded by evolution in the focal study.
- **Censoring:** no plateau within the plotted window is not evidence that no plateau exists.

Avida is the strongest reviewed case for realized within-history **C2**, with partial finite-window **C3** evidence. It has no established **I**.

## 11. Tierra

### Strongest positive interpretation

Standish does not equate code length with complexity. The phenotype context is built from pairwise tournaments among recovered Tierra genotypes. Complexity estimates the information required to specify a genotype given the density of genotypes with the same interaction phenotype. Non-volatile sites and single-site neutrality provide tractable proxies for a more expensive neutral-network estimate.[^7]

This is a scientifically meaningful organismal-program measure: sites count because changing them changes an ecologically evaluated phenotype, not merely because they occupy memory.

### Negative result and attack

In the focal size-neutral run:

\[
\boxed{\text{genome length }80\rightarrow526\quad\text{while measured complexity shows no increasing trend}.}
\]

The result is bounded to one \(6\times10^9\)-instruction run and one declared phenotype relation. It does not establish that Tierra can never evolve complexity. The later parsimony/randomness pilot observed isolated genotypes above ancestral complexity under intermediate settings but explicitly found no open-ended complexity growth.[^8]

Tierra is therefore the central control for:

\[
\boxed{\text{size growth}\not\Rightarrow\text{complexity growth}.}
\]

The focal run remains **C1**: a valid measure is applied and yields evidence against **G** and **S**. The source does not discriminate environmental-information ceiling, dynamical saturation below the substrate ceiling, and observation limit strongly enough to diagnose one universal cause.

## 12. Hintze control

### What grows

One hundred agents carry genomes composed only of `right`, `left`, and `forward`. Each genome traces a path from the origin. Selection rewards visitation of less-used locations; insertions allow arbitrarily long strings. The model was run for five million generations in 100 replicates, and zlib compressed genome size increased with a better fit to the tested nonsaturating curve than to the tested hyperbola.[^9]

### Why the positive inference fails

The growth is real for the chosen number, and it is evolutionarily generated. But genome compression size does not show that the system acquired new primitives, relations, organizational levels, or increasingly structured behavior. The fixed three-symbol path language, the direct reward for rare/extended trajectories, and the accumulated visitation record give length and incompressibility a perpetually available route upward.

Thus the evidence chain is:

\[
X\;\checkmark
\quad M\;\boldsymbol{\times}
\quad E\;\checkmark
\quad G\;\checkmark
\quad S_T\;\checkmark,
\]

where \(S_T\) denotes the finite five-million-generation window. Once **M** fails, later links cannot establish the intended hallmark.

Hintze supplies:

\[
\boxed{\text{formal/open-ended metric growth without demonstrated meaningful organizational complexity}.}
\]

## 13. Stringmol

### Strongest positive interpretation

Stringmol's bearer is an executing pairwise molecular program, not merely a string. The 2021 paper reconstructs reaction control flow and classifies replicator, parasite, and recognition behavior. Programs evolve extra active operations, including increasingly intricate self/non-self discrimination. Median program length and reaction time rise in substantial portions of surviving runs.[^10]

This is stronger than a pure bloat claim because the additional structure participates in the observed reaction protocol and has a documented reproductive relation. The paper also reports population/ecosystem elaboration, but does not supply one common scalar for individual and ecological complexity.

### Non-monotonicity

When elaborate defenses coexist with rare parasites, the elaborate programs can be lost and replicators become shorter and simpler; new parasite emergence can reverse the simplification. The record therefore contains the qualitative pattern:

\[
\text{gain}\rightarrow\text{loss}\rightarrow\text{regain}.
\]

This supports condition-dependent active complexity change and warns against demanding monotonicity at every time step. It does not show an advancing long-run upper envelope rather than fluctuation within a bounded repertoire.

Stringmol supports **C2** at the level of reconstructed active program organization. **S** and **I** are not established; the observed reversibility supplies evidence against treating its finite history as sustained upward complexity growth.

## 14. Flow-Lenia

Flow-Lenia is included because it supplies a clean continuous-morphology contrast, not because visual intricacy is itself evidence of complexity growth.

The Flow-Lenia.png preprint evaluates final \(256\times256\), three-channel states by inverse PNG compression after resampling across multiple scales. A genetic algorithm directly selects distance to designer-specified complexity targets. Mean scores approach those targets over 50 generations, and the fixed configuration's observed range is approximately 0.21–0.62.[^11]

The multiscale transform reduces a specific fine-noise failure, and the authors explicitly show that single-scale maximization produces granular near-noise. Nevertheless:

\[
\boxed{\text{rich or less-compressible morphology}\not\Rightarrow\text{intrinsic ongoing complexity growth}.}
\]

The result establishes a measurable and externally optimizable spatial-complexity landscape over the tested search. It does not establish that the observed extrema are true configuration-wide ceilings, intrinsic selection among persisting Flow-Lenia organisms, an active-organizational interpretation of the metric, a continuing temporal frontier, or scalability. Flow-Lenia therefore contributes direct morphological measurement possibilities and a direct-optimization negative control, not a positive OEE complexity case.

## 15. Optional additional systems

No optional system is added. The six selected cases already span:

- active component complexity;
- genomic environmental information;
- phenotype-conditioned program information;
- trivial compression growth;
- qualitative active-program elaboration and reversal; and
- externally optimized continuous morphology.

Adding more systems before stabilizing the growth/scalability distinction would broaden the catalog without filling a missing evidential role.

## 16. Active versus latent complexity

The cases establish a hierarchy of evidential relevance without imposing one universal rule:

| System | Available structure | Measured active/expressed structure | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geb | Maximum permitted neurons and genome capacity | Distinct genes actually used in neural development | Strong active-structure firewall; interaction/behavior complexity still omitted |
| Avida | Genome sites and instruction alphabet | Environment-constrained or null-sensitive informative sites | Strong active/information firewall; epistasis approximated |
| Tierra | Genome length and soup memory | Sites distinguishing a pairwise-interaction phenotype | Strong size firewall; observer context remains decisive |
| Hintze | Arbitrarily extensible path string and grid | No independent active-organization reduction beyond executed path | Latent/raw extensibility dominates the claimed complexity |
| Stringmol | Variable-length opcode strings | Reconstructed executing reaction protocol | Active organization supported, but no scalar frontier |
| Flow-Lenia.png | Fixed state grid and parameter range | Final rendered state, not causally active substructure | State complexity measured; active organization not established |

**Interpretation.** “Active” has system-specific meanings: developmental participation, fitness sensitivity, phenotypic consequence, executed reaction role, or persistence. The common demand is that unused capacity not receive credit merely for existing.

## 17. Within-run versus cross-system scaling

Three evidential objects must remain separate:

1. **Within-history increase:** \(C(t_2)>C(t_1)\) or a time-resolved trend under one parameter regime.
2. **Fixed-limit continuation:** the trend or frontier continues through a substantial window without observed saturation under that regime.
3. **Family-of-systems scalability:** larger external bounds support larger attained maxima across runs.

Avida directly supports (1), partially supports finite-window (2), and does not test (3). Geb strongly supports (3), but its focal 2019 statistic does not by itself establish ongoing (2) inside one fixed world. Hintze supports (1) and observed-window (2) for an invalid hallmark metric. Flow-Lenia.png supports a short externally optimized (1) that converges to a target and reports a fixed-configuration range.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{complexity capacity}\neq\text{realized evolutionary complexity}}
\]

and:

\[
\boxed{\text{scalable ceiling}\neq\text{ongoing realized growth}.}
\]

## 18. Mean versus maximum / frontier complexity

The studies use non-equivalent temporal statistics:

- **Geb:** maximum individual complexity, averaged across late-run windows and replicates in the precursor reports.
- **Avida 2000:** an ensemble-derived approximate genomic complexity at each time; MODES uses the maximum across filtered persistent components.
- **Tierra:** complexity of first-appearing phenotype archetypes, effectively testing whether the historical frontier advances.
- **Hintze:** average compression proxy over replicate populations.
- **Stringmol:** medians and interquartile ranges for program length/reaction time plus selected reaction histories.
- **Flow-Lenia.png:** best, mean, and min–max values across externally optimized populations.

Neither \(\bar C(t)\) nor \(C_{\max}(t)\) is universally privileged. A maximum is relevant to an expanding frontier but vulnerable to a pathological outlier; a mean can rise while the frontier stays fixed. A defensible upper envelope therefore requires a valid **M** and persistence/replication evidence before its trend is interpreted as OEE.

| System | Environment | Principal hard or effective bounds | Does the observed trajectory approach the bound? | Ceiling diagnosis supported now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geb | Fixed substrate; endogenous biotic context | \(L^2\) population maximum, maximum neurons, time, memory | One-parameter scaling approaches asymptotes; joint scaling shifts the frontier | SUBSTRATE CO-LIMITATION under scaling; fixed-run temporal saturation only partially localized |
| Avida | Fixed single niche and fixed task repertoire | 100 sites in 2000 experiment; finite task/environmental information; population/time | Complexity rises toward but does not visibly reach 100 in the plotted window | METRIC/SUBSTRATE and ENVIRONMENTAL INFORMATION CEILINGS exist; observed saturation not established |
| Tierra | Fixed virtual machine; endogenous interactions | Soup memory, instruction alphabet, finite run, sampled phenotype context | Length grows strongly; complexity remains below ancestral/high early values | EVOLUTIONARY DYNAMICAL SATURATION or OBSERVATION LIMIT remains unresolved; not a substrate-ceiling result |
| Hintze | Fixed rarity evaluator; accumulated visitation map | Executed time/memory only in the idealized unbounded grid/string model | No plateau over five million generations for the proxy | No meaningful-complexity ceiling diagnosis because **M** fails |
| Stringmol | Fixed finite arena; endogenous ecology | 12,500 cells, run time, computation/memory | Gain/loss occurs without an advancing measured frontier | FLUCTUATING / REVERSIBLE; other ceiling type unknown |
| Flow-Lenia.png | Fixed designer environment | 256² grid, channels, kernels, scales, search space, 50 generations | External optimization approaches targets; fixed configuration shows a finite observed range | OBSERVED METRIC/CONFIGURATION RANGE; true ceiling not established |

An externally imposed ceiling and evolutionary saturation below it are different. Only Geb explicitly diagnoses interacting substrate bounds across scales. Tierra is especially informative because lack of complexity increase occurs while genome size has room to grow.

## 20. Exogenous complexity injection

Exogenous input appears in three different ways and must not be conflated:

- **Resource relaxation:** Geb's enlarged world and neuron limits reveal additional attainable capacity; increasing the limits is not itself evolutionary complexity growth.
- **Finite environmental specification:** Avida's rewarded Boolean relations make environmental information available; the evolutionary history stores more of that fixed information, but the designer does not continuously add tasks during the focal run.
- **Direct metric optimization:** Flow-Lenia.png makes multiscale compression the external fitness target. The resulting increase demonstrates optimizability of the metric, not spontaneous hallmark production.

Hintze's continually accumulated visitation record is generated by prior populations but used by a fixed external evaluator. It supplies an indefinitely extendable score landscape without demonstrating new organized capability. Stringmol and Tierra evolve within fixed substrate rules with endogenously changing population contexts.

## 21. Fixed-limit saturation

Evidence of saturation is heterogeneous:

- Avida's MODES NK control rises to the genome-length ceiling and saturates, validating the detection logic; the Avida Logic-9 history has not reached a reported plateau within its window.[^6]
- Tierra's focal size-neutral history shows no complexity trend while length grows, but the evidence does not identify whether the cause is a dynamical attractor, environmental limit, or insufficient time.
- Stringmol fluctuates and reverses; oscillation around a bounded band is not ongoing growth.
- Flow-Lenia.png maps a finite observed metric range under one configuration; finite search does not establish the true configuration-wide ceiling.
- Geb's one-parameter asymptotes are resource-scaling asymptotes. They must not be silently redescribed as within-run temporal plateaus.

The common boundary is:

\[
\boxed{\text{no plateau observed by }T_{\max}\neq\text{no plateau exists}.}
\]

## 22. Indefinite scalability

Only Geb provides strong reviewed evidence on \(I\). Its joint resource-scaling experiment shows higher evolved maxima as both population/world capacity and maximum neural capacity rise. The finding should be stated as:

> **INDEFINITELY SCALABLE TO THE EXTENT TESTED.**

It must not be strengthened to mathematical unboundedness or an infinite fixed-world history. The result is right-censored in parameter space and time:

\[
\boxed{\text{scales over tested parameter range}\neq\text{mathematically unbounded}.}
\]

Avida, Tierra, Stringmol, and Flow-Lenia have no comparable joint-bound frontier study in the reviewed sources. Hintze has formal extensibility but no validated organized-complexity object and no empirical resource-scaling frontier; it therefore does not count as positive \(I\).

## 23. Higher-order complexity-growth audit

**Fact.** Channon's 2024 procedure uses “higher order” in direct contrast with Geb's approximately logarithmic scaling of maximum individual complexity with the lower limiting resource. The stated challenge is to obtain a stronger order of complexity scaling, within an already indefinitely scalable system, so that much more complex artifacts or behavior might be reachable on feasible timescales.[^12]

The paper does not establish one universal required power law, nor does it equate higher order with hierarchy, a major transition, or a new complexity object. This pass therefore does not invent a mathematical threshold.

Across the reviewed systems:

\[
\boxed{\text{C5 — HIGHER-ORDER ONGOING COMPLEXITY GROWTH: NOT ESTABLISHED}.}
\]

Tokyo Types 2 and 3 remain proposed routes to this challenge, not requirements and not reopened mechanism programs.

## 24. Observation and scaling censoring

All positive-looking trends are censored:

| System | Observation window | Plateau observed? | Permissible inference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geb | Years-long executions; billions of reproductions; precursor summaries use time steps 2–3 million | Not a single universal temporal result; cross-resource asymptotes are observed | Scalable maxima across the tested joint-resource range, not forever growth in one run |
| Avida | 100,000 updates, about 10,000–20,000 generations in the 2000 focal history | No plateau in focal information-rich trajectory; bound remains finite | Finite sustained trend, not unboundedness |
| Tierra | \(6\times10^9\) executed instructions in focal run | No rising complexity trend despite size increase | Evidence against growth in that run, not impossibility for Tierra |
| Hintze | 5,000,000 generations, 100 replicates | No plateau for compression proxy | Finite proxy trend; meaningful complexity blocked at **M** |
| Stringmol | 2,000,000 timesteps; eight surviving of 20 runs | Reversals and loss/regain occur | Episodic/non-monotonic active complexity, not an advancing frontier |
| Flow-Lenia.png | 50 GA generations; 2,000 state updates per candidate | Target convergence and finite observed extrema | Finite externally optimized observation, not ongoingness or a demonstrated ceiling |

Scaling censoring is additional to time censoring: Geb's finite tested resource series cannot prove an infinite series even though it is the strongest empirical support available.

## 25. Negative controls

The matrix localizes four necessary controls:

### Size growth without complexity growth

\[
\boxed{\text{Tierra: genome length rises while phenotype-conditioned information complexity does not}.}
\]

### Formal/open-ended metric growth without interesting complexity

\[
\boxed{\text{Hintze: compressed path-genome size grows, but organized capability growth is not established}.}
\]

### Complexity increase without sustained growth

\[
\boxed{\text{Stringmol: active replication organization is gained, lost, and regained without an advancing frontier}.}
\]

### Scalable capacity without one-run ongoing growth

\[
\boxed{\text{Geb: joint resource scaling raises attained maxima, but one fixed history growing forever is not shown}.}
\]

Flow-Lenia.png adds a fifth control: direct external optimization can raise a declared complexity proxy without establishing intrinsic evolutionary complexity growth.

## 26. Strongest positive candidate

The required distinctions yield different leaders:

- **Strongest realized-growth evidence:** **Avida**, because a predeclared, non-length information measure rises through a time-resolved evolutionary history in a fixed environment, with a later MODES operationalization adding fitness-sensitive and persistence filters.
- **Strongest scalability evidence:** **Geb**, because active developmental-component maxima increase across jointly scaled population and neural bounds over unusually long replicated experiments.
- **Same system holds both:** **No.** Geb has partial within-history growth evidence but the focal result is the cross-run capacity frontier; Avida has no corresponding scalability test.
- **Strongest overall near-positive for the hallmark:** **Geb**, because it combines an active nontrivial object, evolutionary realization, explicit bound diagnosis, and the only strong \(I\) result.
- **Decisive missing link:** a protocol and result that jointly demonstrate continuing time-resolved realized growth and a scalable frontier without treating one as evidence for the other.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{NO SINGLE POSITIVE CASE DOMINATES ACROSS BOTH GROWTH AND SCALABILITY}.}
\]

Geb is selected as the single strongest overall candidate only after separating those axes, not because it motivated the program.

## 27. Cross-system bottleneck

The most frequent late-stage failure is:

\[
\boxed{\text{evolutionary increase}\rightarrow\text{sustained, non-saturating realized growth}.}
\]

Avida reaches credible **G** but remains finite and bounded. Stringmol reaches active episodic **G** but is reversible. Tierra does not show **G** in its focal history. Flow-Lenia's increase is externally targeted and confined to a finite observation; saturation is not established. Geb supplies the separate \(I\) axis but not a complete fixed-regime **S** record. Hintze supplies apparent **S** only after failing **M**.

The further transition:

\[
\text{realized sustained growth}\rightarrow\text{scalable frontier}\rightarrow\text{higher-order growth}
\]

is unsupported by any one reviewed case. Geb alone reaches the middle term to the tested extent; no case reaches the final term.

## 28. Heterogeneity audit

There is no universal first failure:

- Hintze fails **M**.
- Tierra fails **G** in the focal run.
- Stringmol fails **S** despite active finite gains.
- Avida reaches **G** and partial finite-window **S** but lacks \(I\).
- Geb strongly supports \(I\) but does not join it to an ongoing fixed-history **S** result.
- Flow-Lenia.png lacks intrinsic evolutionary grounding for the target hallmark and provides only a finite fixed-configuration observation; saturation is not established.

**Interpretation.** Bottlenecks are heterogeneous at the system level, while **S** is the most common unresolved late link among the scientifically credible positive cases. This is not a causal hypothesis and licenses no mechanism inference.

## 29. Complexity-family/subdivision audit

The evidence confirms materially different complexity families:

- **individual active-component complexity** — Geb;
- **individual genomic information complexity** — Avida and Tierra under different phenotype/environment relations;
- **active behavioral/program organization** — Stringmol;
- **morphological/state complexity** — Flow-Lenia.png; and
- **ecological complexity** — discussed by MODES and Stringmol but not measured here with the same individual metric.

They are too different for magnitude comparison or one scalar. They are not yet too different for one hallmark program, because all admit the shared **X/M/E/G/S + I** questions and the firewall removes obvious false positives.

The current decision is therefore **not F — HALLMARK REQUIRES SUBDIVISION**. Subdivision becomes necessary only if the next methodological pass cannot state sustainedness and scalability evidence across these declared system-specific objects without importing object-specific causal assumptions.

## 30. Central decision

\[
\boxed{\textbf{C — GEB PROVIDES STRONG NEAR-POSITIVE EVIDENCE}.}
\]

Geb supplies the strongest combined evidence for evolutionarily realized active-component complexity and a scalable complexity frontier. The claim remains near-positive rather than positive because:

1. the focal result is a family-of-scaled-systems maximum, not ongoing growth in one fixed world;
2. fixed-run temporal growth and cross-run resource scaling remain logically separate;
3. empirical scaling is censored to the tested parameter range;
4. the demonstrated order is logarithmic in the lower limiting resource; and
5. no reviewed case establishes the stronger C5 result.

This decision does not assert that Geb is completely open-ended, does not reactivate its AFN analysis, and does not infer why its complexity frontier scales.

## 31. Research-scale decision

\[
\boxed{\textbf{CG3 — SUSTAINEDNESS / SCALABILITY METHODOLOGY}.}
\]

The individual measures are sufficiently interpretable to localize evidence: active developmental genes, environmental genomic information, phenotype-conditioned program information, and active reaction protocols all survive at least system-locally. The dominant unresolved issue is how finite time-series evidence and parameter-scaled frontier evidence should jointly constrain an ongoingness claim without collapsing into one another.

CG1 is premature because Geb's missing link is not merely one additional positive datum. CG2 is unnecessary because metric plurality did not prevent localization. CG4 is held in reserve because the common evidence architecture still works. CG5 is unnecessary because the required contrasts are already present.

## 32. Highest-information next operation

Exactly one operation is selected and not performed:

> **COMPLEXITY-GROWTH SUSTAINEDNESS / SCALABILITY BOUNDARY PASS**

Exact research question:

\[
\boxed{
\text{What minimum evidence protocol distinguishes finite within-history complexity increase,}
\atop
\text{saturation below an imposed ceiling, and a resource-scalable attained frontier,}
\atop
\text{without treating observation-window non-saturation or cross-run scaling as evidence for the other?}
}
\]

This operation is methodological. It must not search for a mechanism, rerun Geb, define one universal complexity scalar, or perform an evidence matrix again.

## 33. Stop condition

The complexity-growth program must pause, consolidate, or return to the global hallmark map when any one of the following occurs:

1. a sustainedness/scalability protocol cannot be stated without a universal metric or a mechanism assumption;
2. Geb's time-resolved and resource-scaled reports cannot supply the observations needed to distinguish realized growth from scalable capacity under current literature-only resources;
3. system-specific complexity objects cease to support a common evidence architecture and formal subdivision becomes necessary;
4. the next decisive inference requires new simulation, new empirical analysis, or unavailable independent validation; or
5. a stable boundary is reached on what finite histories and tested resource series can support.

This artifact stops at E1. It does not conduct CG3.
