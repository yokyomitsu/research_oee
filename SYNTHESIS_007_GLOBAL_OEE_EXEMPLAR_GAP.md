# Synthesis 007 — Global Artificial-OEE Exemplar Gap

Date: 2026-09-09  
Scope: one global synthesis of the paused adaptive-functional-novelty (AFN) and complexity-growth programs. This is not Q028, a candidate-system search, a candidate evaluation matrix, a new hallmark analysis, a mechanism search, or an empirical reanalysis.

## Executive decision

**Interpretation — central decision: G1 — ARTIFICIAL OEE EXEMPLAR GAP SUPPORTED.** In the bounded reviewed case set, several artificial systems are strong scientific exemplars of component accomplishments, but no system establishes a compelling broad or multi-hallmark artificial-OEE exemplar. This is a repository-specific instantiation and refinement of an already explicit field problem, not a novel discovery.

**Interpretation — global program readiness: READY FOR CANDIDATE EXEMPLAR COMPARISON.**

**Interpretation — exact next operation: EX1 — CANDIDATE EXEMPLAR EVALUATION MATRIX.** EX1 should use only a fixed set of already reviewed systems and model–measure packages. It is selected here and not performed.

**Interpretation — mechanism gate: NOT READY FOR MECHANISM.** The reviewed evidence does not yet supply a stable broad positive phenomenon and discriminating matched contrasts.

## 1. Why global exemplar synthesis is required

**Fact.** The AFN program ended at **METHOD PHASE COMPLETE, EXTERNAL VALIDATION PENDING**. Stringmol is the strongest reviewed finite repeated-AFN case, with two supported distinct events; no reviewed ongoing-AFN case was established. The event-history method is conditionally validated, functional-class judgment is structured, and an independent-coder protocol is ready, but genuine external independence is unavailable. [Synthesis 004](SYNTHESIS_004_AFN_HALLMARK_PROGRAM.md)

**Fact.** The complexity program ended at **EVIDENCE PHASE COMPLETE, EXEMPLAR MISSING**. Aevol is the strongest reviewed realized-growth case at `(S2,I0)` and Geb the strongest reviewed scalability case at `(S1,I4)`. No reviewed system combines strong S and strong I. The S/I method is retrospectively validated. [Synthesis 006](SYNTHESIS_006_COMPLEXITY_HALLMARK_PROGRAM.md)

**Interpretation.** These are related stopping points because both prevent a strong artificial-OEE exemplar claim, but they are not the same evidential failure. AFN has a positive finite phenomenon plus a validation and ongoingness boundary. Complexity has two positive component phenomena plus a missing same-object conjunction. A global synthesis is therefore justified to determine what, if anything, generalizes across them without manufacturing a common mechanism.

## 2. Bedau 2024 exemplar prior art

**Fact.** Bedau uses a Kuhnian sense of exemplar: a concrete scientific problem–solution that can organize inquiry, transmit methods and tacit standards, and train researchers. His proposed program includes identifying candidate exemplary OEE models and measures, applying measures across models, comparing their behavior, and refining the candidates through community scrutiny. [Bedau 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00428)

**Fact.** The 2024 special-issue editorial explicitly reports a “relative dearth of exemplars of OEE” and calls for identifying and evaluating candidate models using generally accepted and system-specific measures. It also distinguishes stronger agreement about behavioral hallmarks from weaker agreement about mechanisms. [Channon et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_e_00445)

**Interpretation.** “OEE exemplar scarcity” is prior art. The strongest permissible repository claim is **FIELD PROBLEM INSTANTIATED / REFINED**: the AFN and complexity programs show concretely how the broad field concern decomposes into phenomenon, recording, validation, consensus, and conjunction limits in one bounded evidence map.

## 3. Exemplar terminology

The initial four-way terminology is useful, with one simplification: “scientific-study exemplar” is renamed **component scientific exemplar** because the unit can be a model–measure–result package and need not be a whole study or a complete hallmark.

| Term | Working meaning | What it does not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Component scientific exemplar** | A clear, reusable model–method–result solution to a specific OEE-relevant scientific problem. | That the model displays an OEE hallmark or OEE overall. |
| **Hallmark exemplar** | A system or model–measure package with unusually strong evidence for one recognized OEE hallmark. | Evidence for other hallmarks or broad OEE. |
| **Conjunction exemplar** | One compatible evidential package with strong evidence across multiple specified hallmarks. Tokyo Type 1 is a particular conjunction claim. | Satisfaction of every OEE type. |
| **Broad artificial-OEE exemplar** | A compelling artificial evolutionary system recognized as demonstrating substantial OEE under at least one defensible OEE category. | Simultaneous Types 1, 2, 3, semantic evolution, and every proposed criterion. |

**Interpretation.** These are scopes of scientific claims, not numerical ranks. “Partial hallmark exemplar” remains useful as a maturity description for bounded positive evidence, not as a fifth kind of exemplar. The implication

$$
\text{not full OEE}\Rightarrow\text{not an exemplar}
$$

is rejected. A model can be exemplary precisely because it isolates and solves a narrower problem.

## 4. Exemplar gap decomposition

The phrase **exemplar gap** is decomposed as follows:

- **P — phenomenon gap:** the strong target phenomenon is rarely or not yet convincingly observed.
- **E — evidence/recording gap:** the phenomenon may occur, but published histories do not preserve the records needed to establish it.
- **V — validation gap:** evidence and method exist, but independent or reproducible validation remains inadequate.
- **C — consensus/exemplarization gap:** strong results may exist, but no shared canonical model–measure–result has organized community practice.
- **J — conjunction gap:** relevant positive components occur, but not together in one compatible evidential object.

**Interpretation.** J is best treated as a diagnostic subtype of P, not as an independent ontological kind. It says more than generic phenomenon absence: separate component successes exist, while their conjunction is missing. Retaining J prevents “no positive evidence” from being conflated with “no joint positive case.”

## 5. AFN gap decomposition

**Fact — phenomenon.** Finite repeated AFN exists in the reviewed evidence: Stringmol supports two ordered, heritable, adaptive, functionally distinct events. Ongoing AFN is not established. [Validation 001](VALIDATION_001_STRINGMOL_ADAPTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_NOVELTY.md); [Analysis 001](ANALYSIS_001_STRINGMOL_STATIC_HISTORY_REANALYSIS.md)

**Fact — evidence/recording.** Stringmol's unusually rich interaction histories permit retrospective reconstruction. Avida and Geb support one event each in their strongest continuous histories; other reviewed systems fail at different event-history fields or lack sufficiently resolved continuous records. [Validation 002](VALIDATION_002_AVIDA_GEB_ADAPTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_NOVELTY.md)

**Fact — validation.** The frozen event-history workflow survives cross-system application, and functional-class identity is structured by pairwise causal-role comparison under a frozen contract. No genuinely independent, blinded, scheme-independent coding round has been conducted. [Application 001](APPLICATION_001_AFN_EVENT_HISTORY_CROSS_SYSTEM.md); [Methodology 002](METHODOLOGY_002_AFN_INDEPENDENT_CODING_ROBUSTNESS.md)

**Interpretation — consensus.** No reviewed source establishes a universally accepted artificial ongoing-AFN exemplar. This is a consensus statement, not additional evidence that the phenomenon cannot occur.

**Interpretation — dominant AFN gaps.** V is presently the dominant actionable program boundary. P remains material at the stronger ongoingness claim, and E remains material outside Stringmol. J is not applicable within the AFN-only decomposition.

## 6. Complexity gap decomposition

**Fact — phenomenon.** Artificial systems establish realized complexity increase and resource-scalable attained frontiers separately. Aevol is the strongest reviewed realized-growth record at `(S2,I0)`; Geb is the strongest resource-scalability record at `(S1,I4)` in the qualified published operational sense. [Evidence Map 003](EVIDENCE_MAP_003_COMPLEXITY_STRONG_S_STRONG_I_SEARCH.md)

**Fact — evidence/recording.** Both dimensions can be assessed retrospectively when the object, metric, evolutionary grounding, temporal record, and resource protocol are explicit. The existing literature does not provide a strong compatible same-object S/I record.

**Fact — validation.** The S/I workflow survives retrospective cross-system application and the Tierra/Hintze controls without a system-specific logical exception. Its present obstacle is not an untested coding protocol comparable to AFN's. [Application 002](APPLICATION_002_COMPLEXITY_SUSTAINEDNESS_SCALABILITY_CROSS_SYSTEM.md)

**Fact — conjunction.** Strong S and strong I are most strongly realized in different systems. No reviewed case occupies the strong-S/strong-I quadrant.

**Interpretation — consensus.** Geb is an important complexity-measurement and operational-scalability result, but repository evidence does not establish any current system as a community-canonical complexity-OEE exemplar.

**Interpretation — dominant complexity gaps.** P and its J subtype dominate. E remains moderate because the necessary same-object longitudinal and scaling histories are absent. V is low relative to AFN because the method has already survived the available retrospective tests.

**Interpretation.** These successes do not have equal scope. Evoloops and Outlier are replication/evolution component exemplars; Stringmol, Aevol, and Geb reach bounded hallmark evidence; Genelife and Flow-Lenia primarily exemplify substrates and dynamics relevant to OEE study.

**Fact.** Bedau's program concerns exemplary models and exemplary measures, not model names alone. The reviewed corpus contains several methodological candidates with different provenance.

## 11. The model + measure + interpreted result unit

**Interpretation — supported.** The scientifically useful exemplar unit is often

$$
\boxed{\text{model}+\text{measurement method}+\text{interpreted result}}.
$$

A model name alone underdetermines what was demonstrated; a measure alone underdetermines whether its object is meaningful; a numerical result alone underdetermines its evolutionary interpretation. The package makes the problem, method, and warranted conclusion jointly reusable.

Examples are:

$$
\text{Geb}+\text{active-individual complexity}+\text{joint resource scaling},
$$

and

$$
\text{Stringmol}+\text{generic activity/system-specific reconstruction}+\text{two finite AFN events}.
$$

**Interpretation.** This package aligns more closely with a Kuhnian exemplar than a bare system name because it transmits a worked scientific solution. It also prevents later users from silently upgrading a component result into broad OEE.

## 12. Evoloops

**Fact.** Evoloops constructively demonstrated Darwinian evolution of self-reproducing cellular-automaton organisms under local deterministic rules, with inherited variants and differential reproductive success. The 2025 review treats this line as a major historical accomplishment while retaining the challenge of more open-ended evolution. [Sayama & Nehaniv 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00451)

**Interpretation.** Evoloops is a defensible **CANONICAL COMPONENT EXEMPLAR** of Darwinian self-reproduction and evolution in a cellular automaton. Its value does not depend on being an OEE exemplar. The boundary remains:

$$
\boxed{\text{Darwinian self-reproduction/evolution}\not\Rightarrow\text{OEE}.}
$$

No Evoloops mechanism claim is reopened.

## 13. Stringmol

**Fact.** Stringmol supplies the strongest reviewed finite repeated-AFN history: two distinct supported AFN events. Static examination found no qualifying third event, and external independent functional-class validation has not occurred.

**Interpretation.** Its present defensible status is **PARTIAL HALLMARK EXEMPLAR**: the model–method–result package clearly teaches how finite repeated AFN can be reconstructed and how recurrence can stop short of ongoingness. Perfect external validation is not required for scientific usefulness, but the stronger labels “independently validated” and “ongoing-AFN exemplar” are not warranted.

## 20. Methodological exemplars

**Interpretation.** Evolutionary activity/shadow methods, MODES, Tokyo Type 1 analysis, and system-generic → system-specific analysis are defensible established field methods because they have published formulations and uses beyond this repository. The AFN event-history workflow is a conditionally validated repository method; the complexity S/I scheme is a retrospectively validated repository integration. Neither repository method is called a field exemplar.

The most instructive methodological lesson is plural: generic filters support comparison, while system-specific semantics determine what entities, functions, or complexity objects mean. No generic score eliminates this division of labor.

## 21. Strictness-artifact firewall

The adversarial alternative is that the repository failed to find exemplars because its criteria are too strict.

**Fact.** Hintze constructed a deliberately simple system that satisfies weak unbounded-diversity and complexity conditions, showing that permissive definitions can classify growth without the intended organizational or evolutionary richness as OEE. [Hintze 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00289)

**Interpretation — scientifically necessary requirements.** Distinguishing replication, activity, novelty, function, adaptation, complexity, and OEE; declaring the measured bearer; excluding raw padding and unused capacity; preserving within-history continuity; and qualifying finite observations are necessary to prevent known false positives.

**Interpretation — useful but conservative requirements.** Independent functional-class coding for AFN and strong same-object S3/S4 plus I3/I4 for a strong complexity conjunction are conservative at the strongest claim level. They should not erase lower-confidence or finite component accomplishments.

**Interpretation — stricter than some field usage.** Event-level N/F/H/A/C validation is more demanding than labeling positive evolutionary activity as novelty. The strong-S/strong-I requirement is more demanding than calling operational scalability alone complexity OEE.

**Interpretation — not substantially overrestrictive.** The framework permits graded, operational finite evidence, does not require literal infinite observation, does not require all Tokyo types, and accepts component exemplars. It would become accidentally overconstrained only if “broad OEE” were silently equated with the strongest Tokyo Type 1 conjunction or with a universal super-system. That equation is rejected. G5 is therefore not supported.

## 22. Tokyo Type 1 conjunction

Tokyo Type 1 joins ongoing adaptive novelty with ongoing complexity growth. [Channon 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00430)

**Interpretation — result: NOT ESTABLISHED IN THE REVIEWED CASE SET.** Stringmol has finite repeated AFN but no ongoing AFN or strong complexity record. Aevol has S2 complexity but no reviewed ongoing-AFN record or scalability. Geb has I4 scalability but S1 sustainedness and only one accepted AFN class. No reviewed artificial system supports the strong conjunction.

This is a bounded evidence result, not an impossibility claim and not a new Tokyo Type 1 analysis.

## 23. Type 2 / Type 3 firewall

**Fact.** Tokyo Types 2 and 3 remain recognized categories concerning evolution of evolvability and major transitions. Their possible contribution to higher-order complexity does not imply a universal recurrence mechanism.

**Interpretation.** Q024/F3 and Q026/F2 remain closed. This synthesis neither reopens evolvability retention nor recurrent major transitions as mechanism programs. ## 24. Human cultural evolution as calibration

**Fact.** The 2024 special issue presents human cultural evolution as a plausible second real-world OEE instance and emphasizes the distinction between bounded and unbounded cumulative culture. [Borg et al. 2023/2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00406)

**Interpretation.** This is calibration only. The repository's framework can represent the relevant contrast through long event histories, cumulative functional classes, complexity trajectories, changing ceilings, and qualified continuation evidence. It does not demand infinite observation. Human culture is not added to the artificial-system ledger and is not analyzed here.

## 25. Natural genetic evolution as calibration

**Interpretation.** The criteria can in principle express why long-run biological evolution appears more open-ended than current artificial records: it permits graded evidence from extensive lineage histories, repeated adaptive novelty, expanding organization, and multiple transitions without demanding proof of literal infinity. This is a sanity check, not a proof that the biosphere is OEE. A criterion that rejected every finite real-world record by construction would be defective; the present graded framework does not do so.

## 26. Bounded 2025–2026 freshness check

The check was restricted to major Artificial Life reviews/editorials, explicit claims of newly established OEE exemplars, and explicit updates to the exemplar problem. It was not a candidate-system search.

**Fact.** The 2025 review of virtual artificial life reports major component advances but does not present a settled virtual system meeting its full target conception of life. This is compatible with, but does not independently prove, the narrower OEE exemplar boundary. [Stepney 2025](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0298)

It is recorded only as freshness evidence and is not added to the candidate ledger. [de Pinho & Sinapayen 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01701)

**Interpretation.** No explicit 2025–2026 field claim found in this bounded check materially resolves the 2024 exemplar problem. This is not an exhaustive absence claim. The search stops here.

## 31. Relation to Bedau's proposed program

**Interpretation — SUBSTANTIAL PARTIAL ALIGNMENT.** The AFN cross-system matrix, complexity matrix, and retrospective method applications already identify candidate models, pair them with measures, compare evidential behavior across systems, and expose where interpretations fail. This is substantively aligned with the candidate-model/candidate-measure stage of Bedau's program.

The alignment is partial because the repository has not yet built one fixed cross-hallmark exemplar matrix, secured external validation, assessed pedagogy and uptake, or conducted community comparison. A private repository cannot establish community-level canonical exemplars.

## 32. What the repository has and has not done

**Fact.** It has separated two major behavioral hallmarks; built cross-system evidence matrices; reconstructed AFN event histories; structured functional-class identity; separated complexity sustainedness from scalability; applied both methods retrospectively; and preserved negative and partial cases.

**Interpretation.** It has concretely decomposed the known field-level exemplar problem into heterogeneous evidential limits. It has not discovered exemplar scarcity, established a new OEE theory, proved that artificial OEE is absent, evaluated all OEE categories, constructed the global candidate matrix, obtained independent AFN coding, or produced a community consensus.

## 33. Global mechanism readiness

**Interpretation — NOT READY FOR MECHANISM.** A global mechanism program requires a stable positive target and contrasts that discriminate among causal explanations. The reviewed set has finite/partial positives, an AFN validation boundary, a complexity conjunction boundary, and no broad positive exemplar. Generating mechanisms now would explain an unstable mixture of different outcomes.

System-specific causal studies could be valuable in other programs, but the present global evidence does not license **CONDITIONALLY READY FOR SYSTEM-SPECIFIC MECHANISM**, **READY FOR TARGETED EXEMPLAR CONTRAST**, or **READY FOR CROSS-SYSTEM OEE MECHANISM** as the repository's next research-scale state.

## 34. Interpretation of `HYPOTHESES.md`

**Interpretation.** Its continued emptiness now records that no sufficiently stable exemplar/contrast has survived the hypothesis firewall, not a failure to generate ideas. The AFN and complexity programs improved discrimination while showing why causal hypotheses would be premature. The gate should not be loosened, and no hypothesis is added by this synthesis.

## 35. Strongest positive global interpretation

**Interpretation.** Artificial OEE research already possesses several excellent component scientific exemplars. Evoloops constructively demonstrates Darwinian self-reproduction and evolution; Stringmol demonstrates finite repeated AFN; Aevol demonstrates extended realized functional-complexity growth; Geb demonstrates qualified operational scalability; and other systems demonstrate evolutionary activity, continuous emergent evolutionary dynamics, or multiscale replication. These are genuine scientific successes.

The same evidence does not supply a compelling shared multi-hallmark artificial exemplar. The positive conclusion is therefore not “artificial systems fail,” but “component normal science is possible before broad OEE consensus.” This makes exemplar comparison scientifically useful without equating scientific value with OEE completeness.

## 36. Strongest skeptical interpretation

**Interpretation.** AFN and complexity were operationalized with unusually demanding requirements: event-resolved N/F/H/A/C histories, independently auditable class identity, meaningful active complexity objects, fixed-regime sustainedness separated from resource scalability, and same-object conjunction. Many field claims use weaker activity, diversity, novelty, or scalability criteria. Failure under the repository's strongest categories should not be generalized to OEE as a whole.

Moreover, transformational novelty, semantic evolution, Types 2 and 3, and other categories were not studied at equal depth. A system might be a compelling exemplar under one of those defensible categories without satisfying the repository's strongest Tokyo Type 1-like test. The correct global status is therefore **NOT ESTABLISHED in the reviewed set**, not universal nonexistence.

This skeptical case limits the breadth of G1 but does not overturn it: G1 concerns the reviewed broad/multi-hallmark evidence and explicitly preserves component exemplars, graded evidence, and category dependence.
