## 2. Frozen CG3 methodology

CG3A changes none of the following rules:[^2]

1. `X -> M -> E` is an entry gate. The bearer/object, scientific meaning of the metric, and evolutionary grounding are established before S/I interpretation.
2. `C_R(t)` is a declared realized-complexity statistic within one exogenous regime and resource configuration.
3. `K_Π(R)` is an attained, established complexity frontier across resource configurations under a frozen run protocol.
4. S and I are categorical, separate, and never scalarized.
5. A maximum used as a frontier requires a declared route into evolutionary history beyond instantaneous appearance.
6. Finite observation and finite scaling ranges are censored.
7. **OBSERVED**, **MODEL-SUPPORTED EXTRAPOLATION**, and **IN-PRINCIPLE CLAIM** are different epistemic sources.
8. A failed **M** gate cannot be repaired by a long or formally unbounded trend.

## 4. Logical invariants

The following questions remain invariant across all six systems:

- What declared object and metric passed, or failed, `X/M/E`?
- Which temporal statistic supports the S claim?
- Which exogenous regime and observation interval condition it?
- Is an apparent frontier established rather than a single instantaneous outlier?
- Is saturation observed, merely theoretically possible, or unresolved?
- What resource dimensions and scaling path support I?
- Which claim is observed, extrapolated, architectural, or not established?
- Where do right censoring, scaling censoring, and residual external ceilings limit the conclusion?

These invariants compare evidence structure, not metric magnitude. They never assert that an Avida instruction-information unit equals a Geb active gene or a Stringmol reaction step.

## 6. S record format in practice

The frozen S record proved usable with three practical conventions:

1. `Temporal statistic` uses the method's explicit categories—MEAN, MAXIMUM, ESTABLISHED FRONTIER, LINEAGE, QUANTILE/DISTRIBUTION, or OTHER—and states when the publication mixes them.
2. `Saturation evidence` separates a theoretical or external ceiling from evidence that the observed history reached a plateau.
3. When **M** fails, the proxy history is described but no S level is assigned. This is the frozen entry condition, not a new S category.

Record waiting times were **NOT PRACTICALLY AVAILABLE RETROSPECTIVELY** for every system in the fixed corpus. CG3A does not derive them from figures or histories.

## 7. I record format in practice

The frozen I record applies even when no scaling experiment exists: all fields then retain **NOT ESTABLISHED**, and the outcome is I0. Architectural extensibility is recorded separately and cannot substitute for I evidence.

Only Geb supplies a multi-level resource-scaling path. Its record requires a vector because scaling world/population capacity or neural capacity alone exposes the other as a residual bottleneck. The remaining systems require no logical exception; they simply lack an E1-reviewed scaling experiment.

## 9. Avida ceiling/censoring audit

The frozen method keeps two propositions separate:

- **IN-PRINCIPLE / ARCHITECTURAL:** in the focal fixed-length formulation, genomic information is bounded by genome capacity and by the information available from the declared environment.
- **OBSERVED:** complexity rises through the 100,000-update window.

The first does not show that the trajectory reached its ceiling; the second does not show that no ceiling exists. Avida therefore receives **SATURATION UNRESOLVED**, not **OBSERVED SATURATION SUPPORTED** and not S3. Assigning S3 would require the principal bounded alternatives to have been examined under the frozen method; that evidence is not present in the fixed record.

## 21. Scaling-censoring audit

Geb alone has a tested scaling range. ## 29. Strongest skeptical interpretation

The application partly concedes this. CG3A creates no new statistic, fact, or prediction. Its value is constraint, not discovery: the frozen workflow prevents five tempting promotions that loose prose often permits—Avida's fixed-run trend to scalability, Geb's scaling series to one-run ongoingness, Tierra's length to complexity, Hintze's formal proxy to meaningful complexity, and Stringmol's regain to an advancing frontier. It also forces every stronger claim to expose censoring and inference type. That is sufficient to validate an integration method, not to claim a new theory.
