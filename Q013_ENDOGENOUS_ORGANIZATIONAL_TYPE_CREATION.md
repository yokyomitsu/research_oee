# Q013 — Endogenous Organizational-Type Creation

One theoretical research pass, 2026-09-08. Scope: the single highest-information question fixed by [Synthesis 001](SYNTHESIS_001_Q001_Q012.md). This pass searches for a conjunctive demonstration, not for isolated examples of clustering, reproduction, heredity, group selection, or causal necessity.

## 1. Research question

> Under fixed local microdynamics with no predefined higher-level identifier, reproductive cycle, or novelty target, can evolution construct a new heritable/selectable organizational type whose causal reproduction and variation relations disappear when the evolved constructional organization is ablated?

The question contains five jointly necessary clauses:

1. lower-level update rules remain fixed;
2. the dynamics do not consult a designer-supplied higher-level identifier or cycle;
3. selection is not an objective that directly names the sought organizational novelty;
4. a post hoc candidate organization exhibits reproduction, heritable variation, and a selectable or selected collective trait;
5. intervention on the evolved constructional relation removes those collective relations while leaving the relevant lower-level evolutionary process intact.

**Interpretation:** evidence satisfying four clauses is a near case, not a positive answer to the complete question. “No predefined” cannot mean “without substrate affordances” or “without a scientific description”; it means that the evolutionary dynamics and selection procedure do not refer to the later collective label, life cycle, or target phenotype.

## 2. Why this remains unresolved

[Synthesis 001](SYNTHESIS_001_Q001_Q012.md) already removes several tempting but inadequate answers. Universal construction establishes expressibility, not evolutionary discovery. Evolution of evolvability establishes that variation and development maps can change, not that a new hereditary/selectable organization has arisen. Major-transition theory explains how collectives can become individuals, but many models and experiments supply the collective boundary, transfer cycle, or group-selection episode in advance. Causal abstraction can validate a proposed level but cannot uniquely choose it.

The surviving question is therefore not whether fixed microphysics can support changing effective organization; that is **ESTABLISHED**. It is whether a single controlled evolutionary history has demonstrated all of the following without circularity:

\[
\text{level-blind dynamics}
\rightarrow
\text{evolved collective life-cycle closure}
\rightarrow
\text{collective heredity/selection}
\rightarrow
\text{specific ablation and rescue}.
\]

The literature supplies strong fragments of this chain. The fragments occur in different systems, or the same system leaves one of the decisive relations inferred rather than tested. This is not evidence that the conjunction is impossible. It is evidence that the original G1 mechanism gap was too coarsely stated.

## 3. Closest prior art

### Evolutionary transitions in individuality

The closest established family is evolutionary transitions in individuality (ETIs), including multilevel-selection accounts, fitness alignment/export, conflict mediation, and nascent collective life cycles. These theories directly address how entities that formerly reproduced independently can become parts of a higher-level Darwinian individual. They already contain the concepts of collective variation, differential fitness, heredity, and selection; “organizational-type creation” must not be presented as a replacement name for an ETI. [Michod 2007](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0701489104), [Ratcliff et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0420)

Life-cycle closure is the closest formal criterion for the reproductive clause. It treats an ETI as emergence of an embedding collective life cycle, represented by recurrent stage classes and transition probabilities. It can reject arbitrary collections whose putative stages do not form a closed cycle. Its life stages and coarse-graining must nevertheless be specified, so it is a conditional individuation test rather than an observer-free detector. [Doulcier, Takacs & Bourrat 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.162)

Price-equation, contextual, and counterfactual-fitness partitions can quantify or causally analyze between- and within-collective selection after a collective partition and ancestry relation are supplied. They distinguish a change caused by differential success among collectives from a collective phenotype that merely benefits its constituent cells. They do not discover the collective partition or parent–offspring mapping. [Shelton & Michod 2020](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2019.0364)

### Bottom-up and constructive accounts

Bottom-up theories of biological construction argue that reproducible collectives can arise from ecological interactions and co-option of ancestral cell traits before elaborate collective developmental control exists. This already explains why new organization need not be assembled from entirely new components. [van Gestel & Tarnita 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1704631114)

Staps, van Gestel, and Tarnita derive diverse primitive life cycles from an explicitly modelled unicellular ecology after an ancestral trait is co-opted for adhesion. Colizzi, Vroomans, and Merks go further in one respect: selection is defined at the cell level, and multicellular clustering evolves because collective information integration improves cell access to resources, not because the fitness function names multicellularity. The mutable traits are narrow and group-favouring ecological affordances are supplied, so these models establish bottom-up possibility within a delimited construction space, not unrestricted organizational invention. [Staps, van Gestel & Tarnita 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-0940-0), [Colizzi, Vroomans & Merks 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.56349)

The closest single result found is the March 2026 version of Fernandes, Vroomans, and Colizzi. In a spatial model, individual cells with evolving gene-regulatory networks forage, divide, migrate, and express adhesion proteins. The dynamics contain no collective identifiers and do not preselect a reproductive mode. Across resource distributions, unicellular, persistent multicellular, and regulated propagule-forming life cycles evolve. In the propagule case, evolved coupling between cell state and adhesion lets a dividing cell detach, reproduce, and re-form an adhering migratory collective. Transfer experiments show co-option from both unicellular and multicellular ancestors, selective sweeps of propagule-forming lineages, and invasion advantages. [Fernandes, Vroomans & Colizzi 2026, preprint v3](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.681199)

This is strong prior art for **unprescribed reproductive mode**, not yet a complete Q013 case. Lineages are reconstructed by cell genotype, life cycles are classified using an analyst-defined adhesion decision tree, “fitness” is inferred for whole cell populations from resource consumption, and no collective parent–offspring heredity/selection partition or targeted ablation-and-rescue analysis is reported.

### Experimental evolution of nascent multicellularity

Snowflake yeast is the strongest causal near case. Settling selection repeatedly produced clonal clusters whose physical growth and fracture generated multicellular propagules. Cluster reproduction, determinate growth, propagule size, and other collective traits responded to selection; later work measured high heritability of a cluster life-history trait. Loss-of-function changes in `ACE2` caused mother–daughter attachment, and restoring functional `ACE2` to an evolved strain restored unicellularity. This is unusually strong constructional intervention evidence. [Ratcliff et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1115323109), [Ratcliff et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7102)

It does not satisfy the “no novelty target” clause. The transfer protocol explicitly selected rapid settling and subsequently group size; the higher-level trait under selection was anticipated. The `ACE2` rescue demonstrates causal dependence of clustering on cell separation, but by itself does not show that a previously unspecified selectable type was discovered.

Predator–prey experiments supply the complementary near case. Under predation rather than an objective naming clusters, initially unicellular *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii* populations evolved stable, heritable, obligately multicellular forms and varied propagule sizes/life cycles. The multicellular forms protected against predation. However, the studies did not causally reconstruct and then ablate the evolved developmental relation, and they did not partition collective-level reproduction and selection. The ancestor also had a facultative palmelloid toolkit, making co-option—not creation ex nihilo—the leading interpretation. [Herron et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-39558-8), [Bernardes et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-24503-z)

Hammerschmidt and colleagues experimentally evolved fitness decoupling and a developmental switch in *Pseudomonas fluorescens*, but collective persistence was rewarded through an imposed two-phase life cycle. Ecological-scaffolding theory explains how externally supplied collective birth/death structure can later be endogenized and tested by scaffold alteration or removal. These are direct prior art for the transition logic, but the starting collective cycle is deliberately provided. [Hammerschmidt et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13884), [Black, Bourrat & Rainey 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-1086-9), [Doulcier et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-50625-1)

### Artificial chemistries and molecular systems

Stringmol demonstrates that fixed low-level string interactions can host an evolved transition between semantically closed constructor organizations. The evolved copier, expressor, genome, and associated interactions are causally necessary for reproduction. But every run starts with a hand-designed universal-constructor architecture; its component roles, container, mutation operator, and reproductive organization are present at initialization. It is evidence for transformation of an existing constructional organization, not emergence of a new organizational type without a supplied reproductive architecture. [Clark, Hickinbotham & Stepney 2017](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2016.1033)

Long-term RNA evolution produced host–parasite diversification and a cooperative multi-replicator network. This establishes evolution of molecular ecological dependencies. The water-in-oil droplets, serial dilution/fusion regime, encoded replicase, and molecular replication relation are supplied; the interaction network was not shown to become a new reproducing and heritable collective. [Mizuuchi, Furubayashi & Ichihashi 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-29113-x)

Spontaneously self-reproducing chemical or digital structures are another adjacent class, but self-reproduction is not enough. Autocatalytic-set work supplies the decisive counterexample: a system can maintain and reproduce a composition while transmission is too inaccurate for adaptive Darwinian evolution. [Vasas, Szathmáry & Santos 2010](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0912628107)

## 4. What prior art already explains

**Fact — fixed microdynamics are compatible with effective organizational change.** Mutations can alter adhesion, developmental regulation, physical fracture, semantic interpretation, or ecological interaction while the underlying chemical, cellular, or computational rules remain fixed.

**Fact — collective reproduction need not be separately programmed.** In snowflake yeast it follows from growth geometry and mechanical fracture. In bottom-up ecological models it follows from evolved state-dependent adhesion, migration, and division.

**Fact — a group-level fitness objective is not necessary for clustering.** Cell-level ecological selection can favour adhesion when collective behaviour improves resource access; predation can favour heritable clusters without the experiment directly rewarding a named multicellular phenotype.

**Fact — ancestral functions can be co-opted.** Cell separation, adhesion, environmental sensing, and unicellular phases can be integrated into a later collective cycle. A new effective relation need not require a new primitive operation.

**Fact — collective individuality can be tested conditionally.** Given candidate stage classes, ancestry, and traits, life-cycle closure, heritability estimates, and multilevel selection partitions can evaluate whether collective reproduction and selection occur.

**Fact — causal necessity is experimentally accessible.** Genetic complementation, targeted disruption, scaffold removal, and replay can test whether an evolved relation is necessary for the higher-level phenotype.

Together, these results eliminate the broad hypothesis that designer-free higher-level organization requires mutable microphysical laws, an explicit group identifier, a separate group genome, or an intrinsically specified group fitness function.

## 5. What prior art does not explain

No reviewed study demonstrates the full five-clause conjunction in section 1.

The strongest cases divide along a fault line:

| Case | Level/cycle not used by dynamics | Target does not name organization | Collective reproduction/heredity/selection | Constructional ablation or removal | Decisive shortfall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Snowflake yeast | Partial | No | Strong | Strong (`ACE2` rescue) | Settling/size target anticipated the collective trait |
| Predator-evolved *Chlamydomonas* | Yes | Yes | Reproduction and heredity; selection level unresolved | No | No causal disruption/rescue of the evolved developmental relation |
| *Pseudomonas* two-phase life cycle | No | No | Strong | Scaffold dependence/endogenization tests | Collective cycle supplied by protocol |
| Bottom-up ecological models (2019–2020) | Yes | Yes | Emergent grouping/life cycles; level depends on model | Mostly parameter contrasts | Narrow affordance sets; no complete causal individuality assay |
| Fernandes et al. 2026 | Yes | Yes | Evolved reproductive program and lineage competition; collective heredity/selection unpartitioned | No targeted ablation/rescue | Closest case, but the claimed new selectable unit is not independently validated |
| Stringmol UCA | No | Yes | Existing constructor system evolves | Component necessity analysed | Reproductive architecture seeded |
| Evolved RNA network | No | Yes | Molecular lineages and interactions | Compartment necessity | Compartment/cycle supplied; network is not shown to reproduce as a collective |

**Interpretation:** the remaining gap is not “how could collective reproduction arise?” Several established mechanisms answer that. It is the causal identification of a *new evolutionary unit* without using that unit to generate, select, or retrospectively certify itself.

Prior art also does not establish a general mechanism. Adhesion plus ecological heterogeneity works in particular cell-like models; mechanical fracture works for branched clonal clusters; predation works in some lineages and not others. Failure to generalize is not evidence that one universal mechanism is missing. Different organizational transitions can have different mechanisms.

## 6. Counterexamples

### Ablation can be strong yet insufficient

Restoring `ACE2` abolishes the snowflake phenotype. This proves that failed cell separation is causally necessary for that clustering mechanism. It does not by itself prove a new selectable organization: ablation of any necessary trait can destroy a phenotype, including a nonheritable aggregate or a cell-level adaptation. Necessity must be joined to a collective ancestry relation, repeatable cycle, heritable between-collective variation, and selection analysis. Broad ablations that also destroy lower-level viability are especially uninformative.

### Emergence can occur without a new level of selection

Colizzi and colleagues explicitly obtain multicellularity while fitness is assigned only to cells. This destroys the inference

\[
\text{evolved collective phenotype}
\Rightarrow
\text{collective-level selection}.
\]

The result is not a defect: it shows that ecological selection on cells can create a collective causal phenotype. But “collective phenotype” and “new selectable individual” must remain separate until a level-specific analysis is supplied.

### A supplied life cycle can mimic an ETI

Ecological scaffolds and laboratory transfers can create collective birth, death, variation, and differential persistence even when the cells have not evolved a way to rebuild that cycle. Group-level selection under such a protocol is insufficient. Persistence after scaffold alteration is stronger, but still begins from a supplied cycle.

### Heritable clustering can be co-option of latent plasticity

Predator-evolved *Chlamydomonas* is obligately multicellular, yet its ancestor can express palmelloid forms. This does not negate the transition; co-option is an established innovation mechanism. It does negate any requirement that “new organizational type” mean creation from components having no prior collective use. That stronger condition would wrongly exclude ordinary biological innovation and would turn substrate history into an untestable purity test.

### New interaction networks need not be new reproducers

The evolved RNA network has novel cooperative and parasitic dependencies, but the RNAs remain the reproduced hereditary lineages and experimental droplets supply spatial assortment. Network complexity and closure of catalytic dependence do not establish parent–offspring reproduction or heredity of the network as a whole.

### Self-reproduction can lack evolvability

Compositional autocatalytic systems can exhibit homeostatic growth and fission while failing to transmit compositional differences accurately enough for selection to maintain fitter types. Self-production, self-reproduction, heredity, selectable variation, and adaptive evolution therefore cannot be collapsed.

### Absolute non-predefinition is incoherent

Every model fixes laws, components, and possible interactions; every empirical claim uses an analysis ontology. Requiring that no relevant affordance or later scientific category be expressible beforehand would make a positive result impossible by definition. The defensible test is procedural: before the run, the transition protocol must not refer to a higher-level identifier, reproductive episode, or target phenotype; after the run, candidate units may be proposed and independently tested.

## 7. Conceptual distinctions that survive

### Unprescribed outcome versus unprovided affordance

A collective outcome can be unprescribed even though adhesion, division, spatial interaction, and regulation are allowed. The scientific question is whether these generic affordances are sufficient under a level-blind evolutionary process, not whether the outcome was metaphysically absent from the state space.

### Collective phenotype versus collective Darwinian individual

A group can move, forage, persist, or fragment as a causal whole without being a hereditary/selectable unit. A collective Darwinian claim additionally needs a defensible parent–offspring relation, between-collective variation, resemblance attributable to transmission or reconstruction, and differential collective success.

### Reproduction of parts versus reproduction of organization

Cell division can increase the number of cells while a reproducible sequence of aggregation, growth, propagule production, and re-formation increases the number of collectives. The latter is not identified by counting parts alone. Life-cycle closure is the strongest existing formal neighbor.

### Constructional necessity versus organizational novelty

An intervention may show that adhesion or regulation is necessary for a collective cycle. “New type” is a comparative statement: the evolved system must support a collective reproduction/heredity relation absent in the ancestor and in a matched intervention, not merely exhibit a phenotype that depends on a mutated gene.

### Selectability versus realized collective selection

Heritable variance in collective fitness makes collective selection possible. A nonzero between-collective selection component shows it occurred in a specified episode. Neither follows solely from collective benefit or from lineage competition measured at the cell-genotype/population level.

### Endogenous reconstruction versus environmental independence

No reproducer is independent of its environment. Endogenous construction means that evolved organization recurrently rebuilds the relevant collective relation under the declared environment; it does not require reproduction in arbitrary conditions. Scaffold removal should preserve needed resources while removing the externally imposed collective bookkeeping.

## 8. Formalization status

### What can be formalized now

For a specified substrate and recorded history, a conditional assay can combine existing objects:

1. **Pre-run level blindness.** Audit the transition dynamics and survival/reproduction rules for references to a collective label, collective cycle phase, or target collective observable.
2. **Post hoc candidate individuation.** Propose collective stages from local causal interactions, then test a closed, multiplicative stage-transition graph using life-cycle-closure methods.
3. **Collective ancestry and heredity.** Infer parent–offspring mappings from material/causal continuity and estimate resemblance of collective traits across reconstructed generations.
4. **Selection.** Partition evolutionary change into within- and between-candidate components, or use an equivalent causal contextual analysis, under the same ancestry mapping.
5. **Constructional intervention.** Disrupt the evolved coupling while preserving lower-level reproduction and environmental opportunity; test loss of collective cycle closure, heredity, or selection. A rescue or synthetic reconstruction should restore the relation.
6. **Ancestral and recoding controls.** Apply the same detector to ancestral and null histories, and require invariance under invertible relabeling and reasonable changes of spatial/temporal resolution.

No new symbol is needed for “organizational type.” The usable mathematical objects already exist: stage-transition graphs or matrices for life cycles, parent–offspring relations, distributions of collective traits, multilevel selection decompositions, and intervention contrasts.

### What cannot currently be formalized universally

There is no observer-independent algorithm that discovers the unique collective boundary, stage partition, beneficiary, or trait vocabulary in every evolutionary system. Life-cycle closure tests a proposed coarse-graining; the Price equation partitions change after entities and ancestry are identified; causal interventions answer questions relative to chosen variables and intervention families.

Nor is there a universal threshold at which a quantitative increase in group heritability or selection becomes a qualitatively “new type.” The defensible output is a domain-specific causal claim with explicit mappings and failures, not an intrinsic scalar of organizational novelty.

The assay above is therefore **PARTIALLY FORMALIZED**. Its components are established; their integration into a level-blind validation procedure, especially for overlapping and aggregative collectives, remains underdeveloped. This is a formalization and measurement gap, not a new mechanism.

## 9. Observer / representation dependence

“New,” “collective,” “parent,” “offspring,” and “same life-cycle stage” are relative to equivalence relations. The Q013 claim can still be partially objective if the relations are constrained causally.

At minimum, a report must state:

- which micro-events count as continuity, fission, fusion, and death;
- how overlapping or aggregative collectives are assigned ancestry;
- which collective traits are compared for heredity;
- which environmental variables are held fixed during intervention;
- which temporal and spatial coarse-grainings preserve the result;
- whether the selection claim concerns cells, collectives, or both.

The Fernandes model illustrates both sides. Its dynamics are level-blind, but the published analysis classifies life cycles through thresholds on inferred adhesion among migrating and dividing states. That is a legitimate operational description, not an intrinsic discovery of “the” higher level. Robustness across alternative, causally adequate partitions would strengthen the organizational claim.

The right invariance target is not independence from all observers. It is agreement among descriptions that preserve the declared causal reproduction, ancestry, and intervention relations. If two recodings preserve those relations, they should agree about whether the collective cycle exists and whether ablation destroys it. If they ask about different beneficiaries or future traits, incomparability is acceptable.

## 10. Remaining explanatory gap

The narrow surviving gap is:

> No reviewed biological, chemical, or artificial system jointly demonstrates, under a transition protocol that does not reference the later level or phenotype, that an evolved collective has a closed parent–offspring cycle with heritable between-collective variation and a collective selection component, and that specific disruption and rescue of the evolved constructional coupling removes and restores those relations while lower-level reproduction remains viable.

This gap has four types, which must not be conflated:

- **Definition:** domain-specific criteria for collective stages and ancestry are required; no universal type ontology is justified.
- **Measurement:** collective parentage, heritability, and selection must be reconstructed in systems whose groups were not tracked by imposed identifiers.
- **Formalization:** existing life-cycle, multilevel-selection, and causal-intervention tools have not been integrated into a representation-robust level-blind assay.
- **Mechanism:** ecology-driven co-option, adhesion regulation, and emergent mechanics are supported candidate mechanisms in restricted domains; whether any one creates a validated new selectable unit, and where it fails, remains empirically unresolved.

The gap is underexplored but not evidence for an unknown universal force. A positive case would establish existence in its scope. It would not establish OEE, recurrence, monotonic possibility expansion, or a general anti-closure mechanism.

## 11. Classification

### ESTABLISHED

- ETI, Darwinian individuality, biological construction, co-option, life-cycle closure, and multilevel selection are the correct prior-art families; “organizational-type creation” is not a new theory name.
- Fixed lower-level rules can host evolving effective developmental, reproductive, and interaction organization.
- Collective phenotypes and reproductive cycles can emerge without explicit collective identifiers or a fitness objective naming multicellularity.
- Evolved collective organization may reuse ancestral traits and physical consequences rather than invent new primitives.
- A supplied collective cycle, heritable clustering, or causal dependence on adhesion is individually insufficient to establish a new selectable organization.
- Observer-independent individuation is not available; conditional causal objectivity is.

### PARTIALLY EXPLORED

- Snowflake yeast jointly demonstrates emergent collective reproduction, collective-trait response, and reverse-genetic loss of organization, but under a target that anticipates the group trait.
- Predator-evolved algae jointly demonstrate ecological, untargeted selection and stable heritable multicellular life cycles, but not constructional ablation or collective selection partition.
- Bottom-up models, especially Fernandes et al. 2026, evolve unprescribed reproductive modes but do not complete the independent collective heredity/selection and ablation-rescue validation.
- Ecological scaffolding and endogenization test persistence beyond supplied group structure, but start from an imposed collective cycle.

### UNDEREXPLORED

- A single conjunctive, level-blind demonstration with reconstructed collective ancestry and specific disruption/rescue.
- Robustness of such a result across alternative causally adequate coarse-grainings.
- Negative cases in which identical affordances and ecology yield persistent collective phenotypes but no collective hereditary/selection relation.

### POTENTIALLY NOVEL

None. The proposed conjunctive assay combines established tests and closes an evidential gap; it is not itself a new mechanism or scientific hypothesis.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether any published system already satisfies the full conjunction under a defensible alternative analysis not reported by its authors.
- Whether ecology-driven co-option is sufficient to create a new selectable unit outside the restricted cell-adhesion models examined.
- Whether a validated one-off organizational transition improves sustained accessibility or enables recurrent transitions.

## 12. Hypothesis decision

**NO HYPOTHESIS JUSTIFIED**

The strongest mechanism-shaped claim—ecological co-option of ancestral interaction programs can generate collective reproduction—is already explicit prior art and is supported only in delimited models and experiments. Turning it into a new hypothesis would fail the prior-art and distinctness tests. Claiming it as a general mechanism would fail the scope and counterexample tests.

The surviving contribution is an evidential and formal boundary, not a claim about nature that is sufficiently distinct and supported to enter [HYPOTHESES.md](HYPOTHESES.md).

## 13. Highest-information next question

Do the propagule-forming lineages in Fernandes, Vroomans, and Colizzi’s level-blind ecological model satisfy a post hoc collective Darwinian test—closed parent–offspring cycles, heritable between-collective variation, and a collective selection component—and does targeted disruption plus rescue of the evolved cell-state–adhesion coupling specifically remove and restore those relations without eliminating cellular reproduction?
