# Synthesis 002 — Q013–Q021

Research consolidation pass, 2026-09-08. This document reconstructs and compresses the existing repository only. It is not Q022, adds no literature search, experiment, model run, implementation, or hypothesis, and does not continue the Fernandes lineage-analysis branch.

## 1. Why this consolidation was triggered

Q021 reached a decisive boundary. Independent theory now specifies the minimum evidence for collective heredity: independently assigned collective parentage, between-parent variation in one declared collective trait, parent-specific prediction of descendant traits beyond same-environment nonparents, and concatenated reproductive edges in which offspring later reproduce. Reticulate ancestry can retain all measured contributors, and inherited cell programs may reconstruct the collective trait.

The Fernandes publication does not report the event-complete collective birth, split, merger, reconstruction, and later-reproduction histories needed to apply that test. Representative events, periodic snapshots, cell phylogenies, and population-level reconstructions cannot substitute for a collective pedigree. Unless an undocumented complete archive exists, resolving the question requires new lineage recording.

The three kinds of uncertainty are therefore:

| Kind | Meaning | Q021 status |
|---|---|---|
| **Theoretical unknown** | Existing theory does not explain or formalize the phenomenon. | Not primary: heredity theory supplies a defensible criterion. |
| **Empirical validation gap** | A criterion exists, but the available evidence has not tested it. | Present: Fernandes collective heredity is untested. |
| **Instrumentation gap** | The decisive evidence is not recoverable from published outputs and requires new data collection or logging. | **Primary and terminal under current policy.** |

Accordingly, the Fernandes collective-heredity branch is:

\[
\boxed{\text{EMPIRICALLY BLOCKED UNDER CURRENT NO-IMPLEMENTATION POLICY}.}
\]

Questions about differential collective reproduction, reproductive autonomy, or further lineage properties must not be generated from the same missing histories. They are downstream of the blocked heredity test.

## 3. Stable general conclusions

1. **Morphological difference does not establish a new organizational kind.** A new shape or collective phenotype may lack reproduction, heredity, individuality, or a changed identity-bearing relation.
2. **An externally imposed fitness outcome does not specify the evolved organizational solution.** It limits claims about open-ended search only when it supplies the entity boundary, reproductive episode, inheritance channel, or menu of solutions.
3. **Evidence and constitution differ.** Ablation, rescue, closure, causal-emergence measures, and selection partitions test specified claims; none independently defines novelty or individuality.
4. **Construction versus ancestral capacity is conditional.** Reaction norms, standing variation, recombinational capacity, local mutational access, and unrestricted evolutionary reachability answer different questions. No finite assay establishes metaphysical ancestral absence.
5. **Mechanism or implementation change does not determine identity change.** Extensive rewiring can preserve a character or life cycle, while a small change can establish a new reproduction relation.
6. **Organizational identity requires domain theory.** Causal abstraction can test preservation of a declared relation, but it does not select whether homology, parentage, heredity, individuality, or autonomous closure matters.
7. **Collective reproduction, collective heredity, and Darwinian individuality are distinct.** Multiplication and life-cycle closure can occur without pedigree-indexed transmitted variation, reproductive autonomy, or differential collective contribution.
8. **Higher-level heredity need not have a separate higher-level code.** Lower-level inheritance may reconstruct a collective trait, but recurrent reconstruction without between-parent variation and parent-specific prediction is not evidence of collective heredity.
9. **A frozen criterion tests scope as well as targets.** Failure out of system establishes failure of that criterion; it does not by itself establish ontological difference between the systems.
10. **A missing conjunction of evidence is not automatically a missing mechanism.** Once theory supplies a criterion and the remaining test depends on unavailable histories, the branch is an empirical/instrumentation problem and theoretical narrowing must stop.

These statements preserve the required corrections:

\[
\text{new morphology}\not\Rightarrow\text{new organizational type},
\]

\[
\text{external selection target}\not\Rightarrow
\text{externally specified organizational solution},
\]

\[
\text{ablation/rescue}\not\Rightarrow\text{novelty or individuality},
\]

\[
\text{mechanistic rewiring}\not\Rightarrow\text{identity change},
\]

and, instead of a universal invariant,

\[
\boxed{\text{identity-bearing relations are domain-specific}.}
\]

## 4. Case-specific conclusions

### Snowflake yeast

- The `ACE2` transition supports a clonal growth–fragmentation collective reproducer: cellular reproduction becomes embedded in a collective parentage and life cycle.
- Later macroscopic size, toughness, cell elongation, packing relief, and entanglement retain the reported clonal collective cycle. They are major morphological and biomechanical changes but not additional reproducer identities under that domain model.
- The complete three-way intervention-preserving comparison remains unperformed. This is causal validation, not an unknown snowflake-origin mechanism.

### Fernandes

- The model encodes cells, cell division, adhesion, sensing, regulation, and ecology, but not collective IDs, collective generations, a collective fitness variable, or enumerated collective reproductive modes.
- Its aggregate–propagule–aggregate regimes support broad collective multiplication and recurrent life-cycle reconstruction; the single-cell-propagule case supports one local source-cluster → propagule → newborn-cluster edge.
- Cell heredity can reconstruct the collective phenotype, and collective behavior affects cell-lineage success. Published evidence nevertheless does not establish a concatenated collective pedigree, parent-specific collective trait transmission, differential collective reproduction, or Darwinian individuality.
- The blocked result is **not** that Fernandes lacks collective heredity. It is that published outputs cannot decide the question.

### DISHTINY

- DISHTINY remains a contrast case because its configured group identifiers and nesting channels pre-enable later levels. This clarifies designer-enumerated organizational structure but does not establish a universal negative result about artificial evolution.

Case-specific details are evidence and controls. They should no longer dominate the canonical OEE frontier.

## 5. Methodological corrections

All five lessons deserve canonical status, with the stated scope.

1. **Frozen-transfer lesson — KEEP.** Predeclare a criterion before applying it out of system. This tests hidden system dependence and retrospective fitting.
2. **Failed-transfer lesson — KEEP.** A failed transfer identifies a mismatch between criterion and target. Ontological non-equivalence requires independent domain theory; it does not follow from failure alone.
3. **Domain-theory lesson — KEEP.** Formal or causal equivalence preserves specified variables, interventions, and outcomes. Independent science must say why those relations bear identity.
4. **Conjunction lesson — KEEP.** When fragments of an evidential package occur in different systems, first audit whether every clause is necessary and whether the conjunction is sufficient. An absent conjunction is not evidence for an absent mechanism.
5. **Instrumentation lesson — KEEP AS A STOP RULE.** Once a stable criterion exists and decisive evidence requires unrecorded events, mark the question empirically blocked. Do not convert missing histories into progressively narrower theoretical questions.

## 7. Final status of endogenous organizational-type creation

**Classification: MIXED — ABSORBED INTO EXISTING THEORY + QUESTION DECOMPOSED INTO DOMAIN-SPECIFIC PROBLEMS + EMPIRICALLY BLOCKED VALIDATION.**

The original phrase does not survive as an active general mechanism frontier.

- Its morphological, developmental, and individuality readings are covered by evolutionary novelty/homology, developmental-system evolution, evolution of evolvability, collective reproduction, ETI, heredity, and autonomy theories.
- Its proposed universal type boundary failed: Q016 supports only domain-specific identity relations.
- Its useful comparative residue is a formalization/validation task within a declared domain, not one missing OEE mechanism.
- In the selected collective-reproduction domain, the decisive Fernandes heredity validation requires new lineage instrumentation.

Thus the theoretical gap does **not** survive under the original name. Domain-specific formalization gaps survive, and one model-specific validation gap survives, but the branch as pursued is empirically blocked. F1 should be retired from active mechanism competition rather than preserved because it motivated nine passes.

## 9. Theoretical questions still open

Only questions answerable initially through literature and formal reasoning are retained.

### Gap: ecological construction of new selection structure

**Why it matters for OEE:** Organisms may change not only fitness values but which interactions, beneficiaries, dependencies, and traits become selectively consequential, potentially creating new evolutionary opportunities.

**Closest prior art:** niche construction, ecological inheritance, ecosystem engineering, coevolution, frequency-dependent selection, self-generated scaffolding, and adjacent-possible accounts.

**Why it remains open:** The repository establishes that organisms modify environments and that ecological scaffolds can initiate transitions. It has not determined whether “new selective/problem dimensions” adds a causal mechanism beyond those theories or merely redescribes their outcomes.

**Blocked by new data? NO.** A prior-art and causal-boundary audit can first resolve whether a genuine residual exists.

**Expected information gain:** VERY HIGH. It returns directly to anti-closure, escapes the individuality-heavy path, and can absorb or sharpen F2, F3, and F5.

### Gap: sustained accessibility transformation

**Why it matters for OEE:** Local increases in evolvability do not explain how viable access survives repeated organizational change, rising coordination costs, and entrenchment.

**Closest prior art:** facilitated variation, potentiation, modularity, neutral networks, evolution of evolvability, scalable evolvability, scaffolding, and first-passage theory.

**Why it remains open:** These accounts provide target-relative or bounded gains. The repository has no general account of repeated, resource-feasible access to causally distinct novelty as the target ontology changes.

**Blocked by new data? NO.** Its mechanism boundary can be clarified theoretically, although eventual validation would require data.

**Expected information gain:** HIGH, but observer dependence and cross-transition measurement create a substantial risk of returning to Q008–Q010.

### Gap: self-maintaining plasticity across organizational change

**Why it matters for OEE:** Accumulation requires enough identity/heredity for selection, while transformational change requires some generative organization to remain alterable.

**Closest prior art:** robustness–evolvability, facilitated variation, developmental plasticity, canalization, organizational closure, and evolution of evolvability.

**Why it remains open:** The ordinary identity–plasticity tension is established. A stronger question survives only if it concerns alteration of the organization that produces heritable variation while lineage continuity remains scientifically identifiable. The repository has not determined whether that is more than a reformulation of robustness–evolvability.

**Blocked by new data? NO.** Prior-art and formal comparison can decide whether the stronger residual is coherent.

**Expected information gain:** MEDIUM–HIGH. It reconnects autonomy and development, but risks another domain-identity loop.

### Gap: recurrent evolutionary-regime transformation

**Why it matters for OEE:** One or several transitions do not explain why opportunities for further changes in heredity, reproduction, or selection should recur rather than terminate.

**Closest prior art:** ETIs, recursive transitions, facilitating transitions, ecological scaffolding/endogenization, evolved open-endedness, and cumulative cultural evolution.

**Why it remains open:** Repeated transitions and target-specific enabling effects are known, but no general sign or repeatable non-predefined regime-construction mechanism is established.

**Blocked by new data? NO.** The theoretical dependency structure and prior-art boundary can be audited without new output; strong validation would eventually be empirical.

**Expected information gain:** MEDIUM. It is central but currently too easily mimicked by predefined hierarchies and historical prerequisite chains.

### Gap: continuity across changing evolutionary units

**Why it matters for OEE:** Claims of accumulated transformation require a principled account of continuity when hereditary or selectable units themselves change.

**Closest prior art:** ETI continuity, multilevel selection, life-cycle theory, causal abstraction, and partial cross-ontology mappings.

**Why it remains open:** Q010 permits only partial comparison and Q016 denies a universal identity relation. A formal account may clarify claims, but it would not itself explain OEE.

**Blocked by new data? NO.**

**Expected information gain:** MEDIUM–LOW as a mechanism frontier; high only for conceptual hygiene.

## 10. Original project audit

### Evoloops

Evoloops have served their main role as the minimal Darwinian baseline: endogenous self-reproduction, heritable variation, intrinsic selection, and genuine Darwinian evolution occur without establishing OEE. Later work also blocks a simple “size converged, therefore evolution ended” story. No additional Evoloops-specific theory is presently needed for the general mechanism frontier. The unresolved plateau diagnosis is an empirical anchor question, not the next theoretical question.

### Lenia

Lenia still has received little direct attention. Its original prospective contribution was continuous morphology, deformable organization, persistence in a continuous dynamical state space, and possibly the relation between smooth phenotypic variation and maintained organization. Q013–Q021 neither used nor invalidated that role. It becomes relevant only if self-maintaining plasticity or continuous organizational identity is selected later; it should not be forced into the current ecological frontier or retained as a coequal pillar by default.

### Autopoiesis / autonomy

Closure and autonomy did not yield a universal novelty criterion. A narrower role remains theoretically relevant after Q016: they can specify, within a domain, how organizational identity is maintained while components and processes change. This makes autonomy a possible constraint on the identity side of self-maintaining plasticity, not an OEE mechanism or cross-domain invariant.

### OEE

The target remains why evolutionary processes continue to construct access to new adaptive possibilities rather than merely explore a fixed or designer-enumerated repertoire. Q013–Q021 clarify what one proposed transformation would require but do not explain recurrence, maintained accessibility, or ecological creation of new selective structure.

### Deep learning

Deep learning has not materially advanced the theory beyond one useful caution: expressive capacity is not effective accessibility.

## 11. Frontier-drift audit

1. **Did the branch illuminate the central OEE problem?** Yes, negatively and methodologically. It showed that one-off collective organization must not be mistaken for OEE and that a universal “organizational type” is unavailable.
2. **What generalizes beyond multicellularity?** Domain-specific identity, bounded ancestral-capacity tests, evidence/constitution separation, frozen-transfer discipline, reproduction/heredity/selection distinctions, and the instrumentation stop rule.
3. **What is specific to collective individuality?** Clonal versus aggregative formation, propagule size, life-cycle closure for collectives, reticulate collective pedigrees, MLS1/MLS2 bookkeeping, and the Snowflake/Fernandes classifications.
4. **Would continuing this branch likely improve an OEE theory?** Not under the present evidence and policy. It would improve one model's individuality classification, not explain continuing novelty.
5. **Has the project reached diminishing theoretical returns here?** **Yes.** Q017–Q021 repeatedly narrowed a validation target until it depended on missing event histories. Further theoretical subdivision would confuse empirical blockage with theoretical depth.

The branch was worthwhile, but it became excessively concentrated on multicellularity, ETIs, and collective individuality. The canonical frontier must now move back to mechanisms of anti-closure that are not tied to a single biological transition class.

Facilitated variation, potentiation, scaffolding, and evolution of evolvability supply bounded answers, not an established long-depth anti-saturation mechanism. It nevertheless ranks below F5 because comparison across changing target ontologies risks repeating Q008–Q010.

F4's core tension is established:

\[
\text{identity persistence}\quad\text{versus}\quad\text{organizational plasticity}.
\]

As ordinarily stated, it is a robustness–evolvability problem. It becomes potentially stronger only when the identity-bearing organization of reproduction, development, or heredity itself changes while evolutionary continuity is retained. Q016 shows that this stronger statement must be domain-specific, so it is not yet a distinct mechanism and is not promoted to a hypothesis.

The high-value task is to determine whether they do. The repository has not yet asked whether evolved ecological products can change which variables, beneficiaries, or interactions are selectively consequential, rather than only alter fitness values in a fixed role vocabulary.

## 13. Highest-information next theoretical question

> **Do niche construction, ecosystem engineering, ecological inheritance, coevolution, and frequency-dependent selection fully explain how evolved organism–environment interactions create new selectively consequential ecological roles, dependencies, or beneficiaries; if not, what independently manipulable causal process distinguishes creation of a new selection structure from a fitness change within an existing one?**

This is exactly one theoretical prior-art and mechanism-boundary question. It does not presume that “ecological generativity” names a novel mechanism, and it can be addressed initially without implementation or new experimental output.

## 14. Why this question beats the alternatives

It returns directly to the OEE anti-closure problem, breaks the recent overconcentration on collective individuality, and can eliminate a major branch if established ecological theories already contain the whole effect. If a residual survives, it could connect endogenous relevance, accessibility transformation, and recurrent regime construction without assuming a hierarchy of individuals. F3 is comparably central but currently carries heavier cross-ontology measurement debt; F4 risks restating robustness–evolvability; F2 is difficult to separate from historical chains and predefined levels; F1 is no longer a coherent active mechanism family.

## 15. Stop conditions for the next branch

Stop and consolidate rather than recursively narrow if any of the following occurs:

1. the proposed residual is fully absorbed by niche construction, ecosystem engineering, ecological inheritance, coevolution, frequency dependence, or established innovation theory;
2. “new selection structure” cannot be distinguished from a relabeling or finer observer partition using independently justified ecological variables and interventions;
3. the result becomes only a metric, definition, or list of examples rather than an independently manipulable mechanism;
4. decisive comparison requires new simulations, experiments, or unrecorded event histories;
5. one biological or artificial case begins to dominate without establishing a general theoretical boundary;
6. no falsifiable residual remains after prior-art and counterexample attack.

If condition 4 is reached, classify the issue as empirical validation or instrumentation, move it to the blocked frontier with provenance, and do not generate further theoretical questions that depend on the same missing data. No hypothesis should be added unless a distinct mechanism survives these stop conditions.
