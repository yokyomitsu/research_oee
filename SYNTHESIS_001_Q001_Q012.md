The first reconvergence occurs in Q006: the observer/relevance branch and the future-capacity branch meet at the need to distinguish output differences from changes in the process that generates heritable differences. The second occurs in Q009–Q010: map change, universality, and accessibility all require target- and resource-conditioned comparisons. Q011–Q012 then use major transitions as a mechanistic stress test, not as the only possible form of OEE.

The recurrent circularity is now explicit:

```text
future possibility
  -> generative capacity
  -> changed generator
  -> ongoing evolvability
  -> scalable accessibility
  -> transition propensity
  -> future possibility
```

Each arrow introduced a valid distinction, but the loop becomes circular if the last term is offered as the explanation of the first. “The system remains open because it preserves future capacity” is a restatement, not a mechanism. Breaking the loop requires an independently manipulable organization and a causal comparison, not another name for future openness.

## 2. Stable known boundary

The ninety local claims in `KNOWN.md` compress to eight stable boundary statements.

1. **Observed activity is weaker than OEE.** Replication, turnover, Darwinian adaptation, diversity, novelty, complexity growth, and OEE are distinct. Evoloops establish several of the former, not sustained OEE. A plateau of one observable is not proof of closure.

2. **Closure is property- and model-relative.** “Stopped,” “converged,” “stagnant,” recurrent, repertoire-exhausted, and unable to reach a specified target are different claims. OEE definitions also target different properties; no reviewed criterion subsumes them all.

3. **History is weaker than capacity.** Non-observation does not establish inaccessibility. Logical admissibility, path existence, positive probability, feasible first passage, viability, heritability, establishment, persistence, and altered later access must remain separate.

4. **Evolutionary significance can be constrained but not made universally intrinsic.** Selection history, organizational maintenance, regulation, causal interventions, and semantic-information assays can ground particular distinctions. They do not select a unique beneficiary, system boundary, novelty vocabulary, or catalogue of future functions.

5. **More organization is not automatically more generative.** Components, dependencies, genome length, semantic information, functions, characters, reuse, and complexity can increase without increasing future evolutionary capacity. Constructive neutral evolution and entrenchment provide strong negative cases.

6. **Formal capacity is weaker than evolutionary exploitation.** A fixed universal constructor, interpreter, physical law, extensible description language, or indefinitely scalable substrate may support unbounded formal constructions while viable evolutionary paths remain negligible or absent. Conversely, map/operator change is already covered by evolution of evolvability and need not create a new qualitative organizational type.

7. **Accessibility is distributional and conditional.** Path existence, path abundance, hitting probability, waiting-time distribution, establishment, and resource burden differ. Complexity has no established universal sign on accessibility. Comparisons are invariant under honest coordinate transport and under specific coarse-graining conditions, but across genuine organizational transformations they may be only partial.

8. **Evolutionary regimes can transform continuously without forming a ladder.** Multilevel overlap, ecological or reproductive scaffolding, nascent life cycles, conflict mediation, and endogenization explain bounded transitions. Transitions can open and close possibilities, lose lower-level autonomy, reverse, or stagnate. Repeated transitions and self-generated scaffolds are prior art; no general transition-to-transition feedback or indefinitely repeatable mechanism is established.

These statements replace any default equation of OEE with monotonic expansion of a set. The supported abstraction is a changing, directed, resource-conditioned accessibility structure with gains, losses, changing entities, and sometimes incomparable novelty classes.

## 3. Candidate ideas absorbed by prior art

### Expanding possibility spaces

- **Closest established theory:** adjacent possible, door-opening states, expansive/transformational OEE, model/meta-model novelty.
- **What was absorbed:** realization can change what becomes accessible next; a possibility space need not be fixed.
- **What remains unresolved:** whether evolution constructs new causal organizational types and keeps them practically accessible, rather than traversing an already supplied expansion rule.

### Generative constraints and reusable primitives

- **Closest established theory:** combinatorial and generative entrenchment, modularity, facilitated variation, developmental toolkits, co-option, and exaptation.
- **What was absorbed:** constraints and standardized parts can enable recombination or redeployment.
- **What remains unresolved:** whether an evolved organization creates a nontrivial new heritable transformation class, rather than a finite reuse or task-relative gain.

### Evolving genotype–phenotype maps or “new generators”

- **Closest established theory:** evolution of evolvability, evolving genetic operators, developmental-system evolution, modifiers, and major transitions in inheritance.
- **What was absorbed:** the map or variation process can evolve, not merely its outputs.
- **What remains unresolved:** a causal demonstration of endogenous organizational-type creation that is not just another program in a designer-fixed operator language.

### Evolution of evolvability and meta-evolution

- **Closest established theory:** modifier theory, Type 2 OEE, meta-genetic programming, autoconstructive evolution, evolved open-endedness.
- **What was absorbed:** evolvability can evolve and ongoing evolution of evolvability is already an OEE hallmark.
- **What remains unresolved:** what dynamics keep such changes recurrent and resource-feasible; an infinite meta-level regress is neither necessary nor explanatory.

### Recursive self-reference

- **Closest established theory:** self-referential/state-dependent dynamics, meta-genetic programming, recursive and autoconstructive systems.
- **What was absorbed:** finite rules can act on descriptions of rules or reproduction procedures.
- **What remains unresolved:** self-reference can converge and a fixed meta-rule can generate many outputs; it does not explain continued qualitative transformation.

### Universal construction, extensible descriptions, and indefinite scalability

- **Closest established theory:** von Neumann construction, computational/physical universality, constructor theory, Ackley’s indefinite scalability.
- **What was absorbed:** fixed laws can support an unbounded effective repertoire and increasing substrate scale.
- **What remains unresolved:** endogenous discovery, viable intermediate paths, establishment, and repeated exploitation.

### Viable paths and scalable accessibility

- **Closest established theory:** accessible landscapes, holey landscapes, neutral networks, first-passage theory, Taylor’s continuing-path requirement, scalable evolvability.
- **What was absorbed:** existential reachability is insufficient; path probability and discovery cost can scale badly, and composition can preserve accessibility in designed task families.
- **What remains unresolved:** the mechanism maintaining useful access as evolutionary organization and the target ontology change.

### Evolving norms and endogenous relevance

- **Closest established theory:** autopoiesis, adaptivity, organizational function, changing viability sets, evolutionary norms, semantic information.
- **What was absorbed:** organisms can constitute, regulate, and alter conditions relevant to their own continuation.
- **What remains unresolved:** a reproducible distinction between newly enabling organization and new dependency, without treating every dependency change as novelty.

### Self-generated scaffolding

- **Closest established theory:** niche construction, ecological inheritance, social niche construction, constructionist ecological scaffolding.
- **What was absorbed:** evolved activity can construct conditions that later alter selection or initiate collective organization.
- **What remains unresolved:** controlled generate–initiate–endogenize sequences, especially beyond one transition.

### Ongoing or cumulative major transitions

- **Closest established theory:** ETIs, recursive transitions, ongoing Type 3 OEE, facilitating evolutionary transitions, cumulative cultural evolution.
- **What was absorbed:** repeated changes in individuality and inheritance are explicit OEE topics and historical prerequisite chains are known.
- **What remains unresolved:** a general causal sign or recurrence mechanism. One transition, or one prerequisite relation, does not establish increased branching access to later regimes.

### “Transition propensity”

- **Closest established theory:** target-specific evolvability, conditional transition rates, key innovations, facilitating transitions, and directional-bias analysis.
- **What was absorbed:** a prior transition can condition access to a specified later transition.
- **What remains unresolved:** no scalar or general tendency is established; positive and negative effects are both possible.

### Causally intrinsic novelty classes

- **Closest established theory:** causal states, probabilistic bisimulation, causal abstraction/emergence, organizational and selected-effect functions.
- **What was absorbed:** dynamics and interventions can constrain equivalence classes and validate specified comparisons.
- **What remains unresolved:** observables, intervention sets, beneficiary, and future distinctions are not uniquely selected by those formalisms.

## 4. Definition gaps

### D1 — Evolutionary novelty across changing beneficiaries

- **Gap:** There is no unique general rule for deciding when a causal difference is a new evolutionary kind rather than a new value, dependence, use, or description—especially when the reproducing beneficiary changes.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** existing accounts ground different scientific questions; demanding one observer-free partition would erase legitimate pluralism.
- **Closest prior art:** model-relative OEE novelty, character identity, selected-effect and organizational function, causal equivalence.
- **What would close it:** either a justified domain-specific equivalence for a stated claim, or a formal impossibility/underdetermination result showing why no stronger invariant is available.
- **Result type:** definition result, possibly formal; not an OEE mechanism.

This is the only surviving definition gap. “OEE” itself should remain plural unless the project explicitly chooses one target property.

## 5. Measurement gaps

### M1 — Diagnosing the Evoloop plateau

- **Gap:** Existing observations do not determine whether original Evoloops ceased producing a specified functional/ecological novelty, produced it too rarely, or failed to establish it.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** size and coarse species counts underresolve genotype and function; finite absence is not exclusion.
- **Closest prior art:** Evoloop ancestry/accessibility studies, MODES, activity statistics, first-passage distinctions.
- **What would close it:** reanalysis or replay under a declared functional ontology, with arrival, establishment, and exclusion evidence separated.
- **Result type:** measurement result about the anchor system.

### M2 — Partial accessibility comparison across organizational change

- **Gap:** A practical procedure is missing for validating target, clock, and causal correspondences through a transition while leaving genuinely new classes unmatched.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** first-passage, lumpability, bisimulation, and causal abstraction preserve specified objects; they do not supply their evolutionary matching.
- **Closest prior art:** Q009–Q010’s first-passage and coarse-graining mathematics, MODES, multilevel bookkeeping.
- **What would close it:** a worked transition case with validated partial mappings, distributional comparisons, and explicit undefined entries.
- **Result type:** measurement/formal result, not a mechanism.

### M3 — Prospective capacity without a future-task catalogue

- **Gap:** Current assays measure access to specified targets or realized descendants; cryptic potential is lost if only current effects are retained, while microscopic noise is retained if every difference counts.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** causal equivalence preserves selected consequences, and target-free “all future differences” is not scientifically discriminating.
- **Closest prior art:** neutral networks, predictive states, behavioral equivalence, intervention-based significance.
- **What would close it:** a domain-bounded prospective assay shown to predict multiple later outcomes better than current-phenotype or genotype counts without defining those outcomes individually in advance.
- **Result type:** measurement result.

## 6. Formalization gaps

### F1 — Relations among non-equivalent OEE criteria

- **Gap:** The implication and incompatibility relations among adaptive activity, exploratory novelty, expansive/transformational novelty, counterfactual dynamical innovation, and complexity-growth definitions remain unmapped under common assumptions.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** the frameworks choose different entities, baselines, and targets; disagreement is partly substantive rather than notational.
- **Closest prior art:** the individual definitions catalogued in Q002.
- **What would close it:** explicit countermodels and implication theorems after entities, histories, and accessibility assumptions are aligned.
- **Result type:** formal result.

### F2 — Effective organizational-type change under fixed microdynamics

- **Gap:** The project lacks a precise relation separating a new effective hereditary/reproductive organization from a new output or program under a fixed expressive substrate.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** operator languages, causal abstractions, and ETI criteria each presuppose some ontology; no universal representation-independent test is established.
- **Closest prior art:** evolution of evolvability, autoconstructive evolution, causal abstraction, ETI theory.
- **What would close it:** a causal equivalence and ablation criterion for a specified substrate that identifies a newly constructed reproduction/heredity relation and is invariant to mere recoding.
- **Result type:** formal result supporting a mechanistic test.

These formal gaps should not be redescribed as absent biological mechanisms.

## 7. Mechanism gaps

### G1 — Endogenous organizational-type creation

- **Gap:** It is not established what dynamics let evolution construct a new heritable/selectable organization when the higher-level identifier, life cycle, reproduction relation, and novelty target are not already supplied by the designer.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** universal construction proves capacity; evolution of evolvability changes maps; ETI mechanisms bridge supplied or naturally available structures; DISHTINY exposes later levels in advance. None is a general controlled demonstration of new type construction under a minimally prejudged substrate.
- **Closest prior art:** Stringmol’s endogenous constructional change, autoconstructive evolution, nascent-life-cycle ETIs, ecological scaffolding, niche construction.
- **What would close it:** replicated evolution of a causally individuated hereditary/reproductive organization, with a matched counterfactual or ablation showing that the evolved construction—not a latent level identifier or imposed cycle—creates access to the new regime.
- **Result type:** mechanistic theory or falsifiable scientific result.

### G2 — Sustained accessibility transformation

- **Gap:** Known mechanisms can improve later access locally, but it is not known what prevents their benefits from saturating, becoming entrenched, or being overwhelmed by rising coordination and search costs as organization changes.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** scalable-evolvability models solve designed families; neutral networks, modularity, duplication, and learning provide conditional gains; none supplies a general indefinite tendency.
- **Closest prior art:** scalable evolvability, facilitated variation, cumulative construction, evolution of evolvability, first-passage scaling.
- **What would close it:** long-depth evidence that evolved structures repeatedly preserve or improve access to causally distinct novelty under matched resource budgets, together with interventions identifying the structures responsible.
- **Result type:** mechanistic theory or falsifiable scientific hypothesis if a specific structure–effect relation emerges.

### G3 — Recurrent regime construction

- **Gap:** No controlled sequence shows that completed regimes repeatedly construct the causal channels for several later regimes rather than exploit a predefined hierarchy or one specific prerequisite.
- **Why existing theory does not close it:** repeated transitions are documented, and their bridge mechanisms are heterogeneous; fragmentation alone is not the gap. The deficit is causal evidence for recurrence generated by prior evolved organization.
- **Closest prior art:** recursive ETIs, facilitating transitions, evolved open-endedness, cumulative culture, constructionist scaffolding.
- **What would close it:** replay/ablation across more than one subsequent regime showing that products of earlier transitions create the later reproduction, heredity, or individuality relations under resource-controlled conditions.
- **Result type:** mechanistic theory or falsifiable scientific result.

G3 is stronger than G1 and depends on it. G2 crosses organizational transitions but does not require every novelty to be a new regime. Treating all three as “future generativity” would conceal these dependencies.

## 8. Original framing audit

### Evoloops

Evoloops remain useful as the empirical anchor and negative control. They establish intrinsic selection, constructive reproduction, heritable change, and adaptation beneath an apparent size plateau. Their unresolved contribution is narrow: what functional/ecological novelty actually stopped, and whether the cause was arrival, establishment, or structural exclusion. They do not currently identify the general OEE mechanism.

### Lenia

Lenia has not been substantively investigated in Q001–Q012. The repository therefore has no stable conclusion about continuous morphodynamics, persistence, heredity, or evolvability in Lenia. Retaining Lenia as a coequal pillar would overstate the evidence. It is a coverage gap and possible future test domain, not currently part of the argument.

### Autopoiesis

Autopoiesis survived as a constraint on what “endogenous relevance” and identity can mean: self-production, closure of constraints, regulation, and developmental reconstruction can ground beneficiaries and functions. It did not survive as an OEE explanation. Organizational closure can coexist with a fixed repertoire, and adaptivity does not imply heritable novelty or changing evolutionary organization.

### OEE

OEE remains the central target, but only in a deliberately plural form. The most important unresolved mechanistic issue is not a missing universal scalar; it is whether evolutionary organization can construct new hereditary/selectable organization rather than merely explore designer-predefined types, and whether such construction can recur without accessibility collapse.

### Deep learning

Deep learning has contributed one durable caution only: expressive capacity and effective trainability/accessibility are different. Evolutionary theory already supplied the operative concepts—GP maps, evolvability, search complexity, and first passage. No deep-learning mechanism has yet generated an independent biological hypothesis.

### Framing decision

The useful core is now **Evoloops as anchor + autonomy as an identity constraint + OEE as the target**. Lenia and deep learning are distractions if kept as mandatory ingredients; they become useful only when tied to a precise frontier test. The project should not seek an Evoloops–Lenia–autopoiesis synthesis merely to preserve its starting slogan.

## 9. Blind-spot audit

The trajectory is path-dependent. Q003–Q010 devoted extensive effort to definitions, observer dependence, evolvability, accessibility, and measurement; Q011–Q012 then concentrated the mechanistic branch on individuality transitions. This was useful but risks equating transformational OEE with hierarchical ETIs.

| Domain | Coverage in Q001–Q012 | Audit conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Artificial chemistries | One substantial Stringmol case; otherwise light | Major blind spot for endogenous construction, heredity, and operator emergence |
| Origin-of-life theory | Mostly background to autopoiesis and transitions | Underexamined; relevant to the first appearance of evolutionary organization, but may differ from ongoing OEE |
| Autocatalytic sets | Touched only to reject “transitions catalyze transitions” | Underexamined as constructive organization; molecular autocatalysis must not be equated with OEE recursion |
| Constructive dynamics | Universal constructors and Stringmol considered | Important but underdeveloped between formal construction and evolving developmental realization |
| Developmental systems | Strong conceptual coverage through GP maps and facilitated variation | Mechanisms surveyed, but continuous constructive development and identity preservation were not a dedicated frontier |
| Ecological network evolution | Niche construction and scaffolding covered | Major blind spot: ecology was mostly an enabling environment, not an evolving generator of new interaction/problem classes |
| Evolutionary innovation theory | Substantial conceptual coverage | Not a major blind spot; should be used rather than renamed |
| Information inheritance | Covered through major transitions and codes | Historical examples considered; causal comparison of distinct inheritance channels remains limited |
| Multiscale dynamical systems | Coarse-graining and multilevel selection covered | Formal measurement covered more than endogenous scale creation |
| Learning and plasticity | Mentioned as evidence only | Significant blind spot; its relevance to OEE has not been established |
| Cultural evolution | Used as a stress test | Light coverage; strong for evolved open-endedness, weak evidence for recurrent new evolutionary regimes |
| Indefinite scalability | Directly examined | Not a blind spot; established as infrastructure, not mechanism |

The largest combined blind spot is **constructive organizational emergence outside predefined individuality hierarchies**, especially in artificial chemistries, developmental systems, and evolving ecological networks. This is an audit conclusion about repository coverage, not a claim that those literatures lack answers.

## 10. Terminology compression

Use the following containment and separation map.

```text
formal possibility / constructive capacity
    contains what a substrate can realize under some supplied description
    != evolutionary accessibility

evolutionary accessibility
    = target- and process-relative reachability plus probabilities/resources
    contains viable-path existence as its weakest form
    is one component/sense of evolvability

evolvability
    includes distributions of heritable variation and responses to selection
    may include changes to variation/development/inheritance maps
    != OEE and != guaranteed long-run accessibility

scalable evolvability
    = preservation of specified evolvability properties across problem scale
    is prior art
    != universal cross-ontology scalability

adjacent-possible expansion
    ~= expansion of accessible outcomes under a specified adjacency rule
    != change in the rule/map and != endogenous organizational-type creation

generator/map change
    is covered by evolution of evolvability, GP-map/operator evolution,
    or an ETI when heredity/individuality changes
    != recursive continuation

generativity / future capacity
    is an ambiguous umbrella, not a metric:
      outcome production | map change | reusable construction | later access
    replace it with the intended term above

organizational closure / autopoiesis
    = maintenance/self-production relation
    != evolutionary closure

scaffolding
    = temporarily supplied enabling organization, external or self-produced
endogenization
    = evolved reconstruction/persistence beyond the initiating scaffold
transition bridge
    adds no content and should not be used

transition propensity
    = at most a family of target-specific conditional enabling/suppressing effects
    not a universal scalar

open ontology
    should mean a change in effective hereditary/selectable organization only
    after the causal ontology is specified
    != a claim that physical state space literally expands
```

## 11. Strongest surviving unknowns

Ranked by expected information gain, not chronology:

1. **G1 — Endogenous organizational-type creation (F1 family).** Highest importance and falsifiability; directly tests the designer-predefinition problem; moderate prior-art risk; largely independent of a universal novelty metric because reproduction, heredity, and ablation can be causally specified.
2. **G2 — Sustained accessibility transformation (F3 family).** Closest to the anti-closure core, but currently depends on difficult target/resource comparison and risks returning to the Q009–Q010 measurement loop.
3. **G3 — Recurrent regime construction (F2 family).** Strong OEE relevance, but logically downstream of demonstrating one non-predefined organizational-type creation; otherwise predefined hierarchies can mimic success.
4. **Ecological generativity (F5 family).** Potentially breaks the individuality-heavy path by asking whether evolved interactions create new problem and selection structures. High importance, but current repository coverage is too thin to call it an established explanatory gap.
5. **Self-maintaining plasticity (F4 family).** Could join autonomous identity with changing degrees of freedom and bring Lenia/plasticity back under a precise question. It remains an unaudited frontier family, not yet a supported gap.
6. **F1/M2 — Formal and measurement invariance.** Scientifically necessary for comparisons, but unlikely by itself to explain why OEE occurs.
7. **M1 — The Evoloop plateau.** Empirically useful and capable of correcting the anchor story, but narrower than the general mechanism frontier.

The ranking rejects automatic continuation from Q012. A branching second-transition experiment is valuable only after the first regime is shown not to be a supplied level in disguise.

## 12. Highest-information next question

**Under fixed local microdynamics with no predefined higher-level identifier, reproductive cycle, or novelty target, can evolution construct a new heritable/selectable organizational type whose causal reproduction and variation relations disappear when the evolved constructional organization is ablated?**

## 13. Why this question beats the alternatives

This question targets a mechanism rather than a universal definition or scalar. It tests the common unresolved residue of Q006–Q012: the difference between exploiting an expressive substrate and constructing a new effective evolutionary organization. A positive controlled case would supply the missing foundation needed before asking whether such construction recurs or preserves accessibility; a negative result would redirect the project toward substrate requirements or show that supposed transformations were predefined. It also exposes the largest coverage blind spot—constructive chemistry/development/ecology—without assuming that major transitions form a ladder. Unlike the current Q012 frontier, it does not require cross-transition branching evidence before establishing that even the first later transition channel was genuinely evolved.
