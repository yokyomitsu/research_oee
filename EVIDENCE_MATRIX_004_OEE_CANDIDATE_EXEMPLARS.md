## 3. Exemplar terminology

| Term | Final use in EX1 |
| --- | --- |
| **COMPONENT EXEMPLAR** | Strong reusable example of one OEE-relevant phenomenon, method, or failure boundary. |
| **HALLMARK CANDIDATE** | Evidence approaches one recognized behavioral hallmark, but an important link remains unresolved. |
| **HALLMARK EXEMPLAR** | Strong evidence for one recognized OEE behavioral hallmark. |
| **BROAD-OEE CANDIDATE** | Evidence spans several substantial OEE dimensions but does not warrant broad exemplar status. |
| **BROAD-OEE EXEMPLAR** | Compelling evidence for substantial artificial OEE under a declared category. |
| **NOT EXEMPLAR-READY** | The target, evidence, or interpretation is too incomplete even for a reusable component example. |

**Interpretation.** These terms are not a strict ladder. A component exemplar may have greater teaching value than a broad candidate for a particular problem.

## 4. Why model + measure + result is the unit

The weakest sufficient exemplar unit is:

$$
\boxed{\text{model}+\text{measure or observational method}+\text{qualified interpreted result}}.
$$

**Interpretation.** A model name permits incompatible claims to travel together. A measure without its object can reward a confound. A result without its method cannot be audited. The package states what was observed and blocks platform-wide upgrade: for example, Geb plus active-individual complexity plus joint resource scaling supports a specific scalability result, not “Geb is open-ended.”

Several packages are inherently multi-method. Generic evolutionary activity can locate persistent candidates, but system-specific reconstruction supplies semantics. A system-specific complexity metric identifies the object, while the S/I framework separates temporal growth from resource scalability.

## 5. Evaluation dimensions

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **P — phenomenon clarity** | Is the claimed evolutionary phenomenon clearly identifiable? |
| **M — measurement/interpretation quality** | Does the method distinguish the phenomenon from its main trivial alternatives? |
| **H — historical/longitudinal evidence** | Is an evolutionary history, ancestry, or temporal record available? |
| **N — nontriviality** | Does the result survive the principal substrate-appropriate triviality controls? |
| **R — reproducibility/auditability** | Can an independent analyst reconstruct the claim from the available methods and evidence? |
| **G — general methodological usefulness** | Does the package teach a reusable lesson beyond its substrate? |

Values are **HIGH**, **MODERATE**, **LOW**, **NOT ESTABLISHED**, or **NOT APPLICABLE**. They are categorical evidence profiles, not quantities. R conservatively distinguishes method description, retrospective reconstruction, and independent validation. Hallmark coverage and limitation clarity are recorded separately.

## 10. Evoloops

**Fact.** Evoloops establishes self-reproduction, inherited variation, intrinsic differential reproductive success, and subsequent adaptive/evolutionary behavior in a cellular automaton. [Sayama 1999](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/ALIFE5-4-343.pdf); [Sayama & Nehaniv 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00451)

**Interpretation.** EVO-D is a **COMPONENT EXEMPLAR**, and its historical importance is matched by clear problem-solution value rather than used as a proxy for OEE strength.

> This package is a useful exemplar for studying the transition from self-reproduction to Darwinian evolution because genotype-resolved inherited variation and intrinsic spatial competition make the evolutionary step explicit.

Its limitation strengthens its teaching value:

$$
\boxed{\text{Darwinian evolution}\not\Rightarrow\text{ongoing open-ended evolution}.}
$$

## 11. Stringmol

**Fact.** STR-AFN preserves the frozen count of two distinct events in run 2: nonreciprocal exploitation followed by partner discrimination. Static review of all eight processed histories established no third distinct post-discrimination event; later parasite/defense changes are same-class activity, recurrent earlier roles, or unresolved. [Validation 001](VALIDATION_001_STRINGMOL_ADAPTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_NOVELTY.md); [Analysis 001](ANALYSIS_001_STRINGMOL_STATIC_HISTORY_REANALYSIS.md)

**Interpretation.** Stringmol is a **HALLMARK CANDIDATE**, not an independently validated ongoing-AFN exemplar. Its strongest exemplar value is the conjunction of the finite result with the generic-to-specific method: activity locates changes, while reaction reconstruction, heredity/adaptation evidence, and pairwise causal-role comparison determine what counts.

> This package is a useful exemplar for studying finite versus ongoing adaptive novelty because it supports two explicit class-distinct events while preserving a well-audited no-third-event and external-validation boundary.

The methodological package is at least as reusable as the count, but the count is what anchors it to a real positive phenomenon.

## 12. Avida

**Fact.** AVI-C and AVI-EQU are distinct packages. The former records fixed-length genomic environmental-information increase; the latter reconstructs a complex logic feature along an exact lineage. Other Avida experiments on task switching, cooperation, parasitism, or division of labor remain disconnected. [Adami, Ofria & Collier 2000](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.97.9.4463); [Lenski et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568)

**Interpretation.** AVI-C is a **HALLMARK CANDIDATE** for bounded realized complexity growth. AVI-EQU is a **COMPONENT EXEMPLAR** of rigorous complex-feature evolution within a declared task ecology. Avida is also a frequently used experimental platform, but platform versatility is not within-history OEE.

> These packages are useful exemplars for studying measured finite complexity and complex-feature origins because their objects, rewards, lineages, and bounds are unusually explicit without requiring unrelated experiments to be merged.

$$
\boxed{\text{many phenomena across separate experiments}\neq
\text{one open-ended evolutionary history}.}
$$

## 13. Aevol

**Fact.** AEV-C tracks active functional-proteome organization across replicated 250,000-generation lineages, separates active functions from genome size and duplicate counts, and finds rare return to the simple functional class. The authors report an apparent bound; no same-object scalability record is present. Its frozen profile is `(S2,I0)`. [Liard et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00312)

**Interpretation.** AEV-C is the strongest reviewed component package for **REALIZED COMPLEXITY ACCUMULATION** and a **HALLMARK CANDIDATE**, not a full complexity hallmark exemplar.

> This package is a useful exemplar for studying complexity accumulation and retention because replicated long histories measure active functional organization while demonstrating that a ratchet can coexist with a finite frontier.

$$
\boxed{\text{complexity retention/ratchet}\neq\text{indefinite scalability}.}
$$

## 14. Geb

**Fact.** GEB-I measures maximum individual complexity as distinct active genes used in neural development. Scaling either world/population capacity or maximum neurons alone approaches an asymptote; joint scaling raises attained maxima approximately logarithmically in the lower limiting resource over the tested range. The frozen profile is `(S1,I4)`. [Channon 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00285)

**Interpretation.** GEB-I is a **HALLMARK CANDIDATE** and the strongest reviewed component exemplar for **COMPLEXITY SCALABILITY**. Its broad-OEE candidacy is not supported because strong fixed-regime growth and repeated AFN are absent.

> This package is a useful exemplar for studying realized growth versus a resource-scalable frontier because it makes co-limiting external ceilings and the difference between S and I empirically visible.

The result is qualified operational scalability, not empirical proof that (C\to\infty).

## 15. Genelife

**Fact.** GEN-A reports positive new evolutionary activity in multiple configurations and rich genetic/spatial evolutionary dynamics. The established record does not ground a new adaptive function or a complexity-growth object. [Packard & McCaskill 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00426)

**Interpretation.** GEN-A is a **COMPONENT EXEMPLAR** of persistent detected evolutionary activity, not a hallmark exemplar. “OEE-relevant scientific exemplar of persistent evolutionary activity” is defensible when the target is stated this narrowly.

> This package is a useful exemplar for studying the reach and limit of evolutionary-activity measures because activity can remain positive while stronger functional-novelty claims explicitly fail.

$$
\boxed{\text{positive evolutionary activity}\not\Rightarrow\text{broad OEE}.}
$$

## 16. Flow-Lenia

**Fact.** FL-E demonstrates intrinsic evolutionary dynamics in a continuous mass-conservative cellular automaton with locally embedded parameters and deformable morphologies. The frozen repository complexity status is `(S1,I0)`; visual morphology is not its complexity evidence. [Plantec et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00471)

**Interpretation.** FL-E is a **COMPONENT EXEMPLAR** of an evolutionary process in a continuous, deformable morphological substrate. Its exemplar value reconnects the workspace's Lenia interest to a measured substrate problem without implying OEE.

> This package is a useful exemplar for studying evolutionary dynamics in a continuous deformable substrate because inheritable local parameters, mass competition, and turnover are realized while entity/function measurement remains visibly difficult.

## 17. Outlier

**Fact.** OUT-HR uses causal/hierarchical replication analysis to establish spontaneous multicomponent self-replication across spatial and temporal scales. It does not establish heritable adaptive variation, AFN, or longitudinal complexity growth. [Yang 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00449); [Hintze & Bohm 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-026-00074-2)

**Interpretation.** OUT-HR is a **COMPONENT EXEMPLAR** of **EMERGENT MULTISCALE REPLICATION**.

> This package is a useful exemplar for studying emergent hierarchical replication because causal ancestry distinguishes reproduction from mere recurring pattern while leaving the missing evolutionary links explicit.

$$
\text{hierarchical self-replication}\not\Rightarrow\text{AFN},
\qquad
\text{hierarchical self-replication}\not\Rightarrow\text{complexity OEE}.
$$

## 22. Component-boundary value

EX1 treats a component package as exemplar-quality when it combines a clear phenomenon, a defensible observation or measure, a canonical interpretable result, a well-understood limitation, and pedagogical or comparative value. A known limitation can strengthen the package by making the relevant inference boundary reusable.

| Package | Boundary it makes reusable |

| EVO-D | Self-reproduction plus Darwinian evolution is below OEE. |
| STR-AFN | Finite repeated functional novelty is below ongoing AFN. |
| AVI-C | Realized information-complexity increase is below scalability or unboundedness. |
| AVI-EQU | A complex feature inside an enumerated ontology is below continuing class expansion. |
| AEV-C | Accumulation and retention are compatible with an apparent bound. |
| GEB-I | A resource-scalable frontier is distinct from strong within-regime temporal growth. |
| GEN-A | Persistent activity is below causal function and broad OEE. |
| FL-E | Rich continuous morphology and turnover are below validated complexity or AFN. |
| OUT-HR | Hierarchical replication is below adaptive evolution and OEE. |

**Interpretation.** Limitation clarity is not a consolation category. These packages are model problems because each locates a specific inference boundary that future work must cross.

## 28. Redundancy audit

**Interpretation.** AEV-C and GEB-I are retained together because their contrast is the scientific lesson: strong realized accumulation and strong operational scalability occupy different packages. AVI-C is omitted from the five-package teaching set because AEV-C covers the realized-growth problem with stronger long-history evidence; AVI-EQU is omitted because STR-AFN more directly teaches the novelty/ongoingness boundary. GEN-A and FL-E remain useful matrix controls but add less than OUT-HR to the small complementary set.

No omission downgrades the omitted system's established result.

## 29. Scientific exemplar versus Kuhnian field exemplar

**Interpretation.** EX1 can establish that a package is an excellent repository-level scientific component exemplar. It cannot establish that the package functions as a Kuhnian field exemplar, which requires shared uptake, training use, and community recognition.

Evoloops is historically canonical as a self-reproducing/evolving CA line, and Avida is frequently used as a digital-evolution platform. Those facts do not establish either as a canonical broad-OEE exemplar. For every package, broad OEE consensus remains unestablished.

## 30. Bedau-program alignment

**Decision: STRONG STRUCTURAL ALIGNMENT.**

**Interpretation.** EX1 does the repository-scale analogue of comparing candidate models, measures, interpreted results, confounds, and limitations. It demonstrates why exemplary units are packages and identifies a complementary teaching set. It does not perform community adjudication, broad independent replication, training studies, or consensus formation and therefore does not complete Bedau's program.

## 33. Strictness audit

**Decision: CONSERVATIVE BUT DEFENSIBLE.**

**Fact.** Hintze's deliberately simple system shows that weak unbounded-diversity or complexity definitions can label formally expanding but scientifically uninformative behavior as OEE. [Hintze 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00289)

**Interpretation.** The repository is stricter than some field usage in requiring event-resolved N/F/H/A/C for AFN, independent semantic validation for the strongest reproducibility claim, an active nontrivial complexity object, and separate S/I records. These requirements have direct scientific reasons: they exclude neutral persistence, bloat, implementation novelty, finite increase, external ceiling release, and visual richness.

The criteria are conservative rather than merely “appropriate” because external class coding and the strongest same-object conjunction set a high bar. They are not substantially overrestrictive: finite positives, component exemplars, operational scaling, and category-specific evidence are retained, and no literal infinite observation or all-types super-system is required.

## 34. Strongest positive interpretation

**Interpretation.** Artificial OEE research already has a useful normal science of components and boundaries. The fixed set provides worked examples of Darwinian self-reproduction, finite adaptive novelty, bounded realized complexity, operational scalability, persistent activity, continuous-substrate evolution, and hierarchical replication. Explicit limitations make these packages reusable for training and comparison rather than failed OEE systems.

The repository's AFN integration

$$
\text{detection}\rightarrow\text{historical reconstruction}\rightarrow N/F/H/A/C
$$

and complexity integration

$$
\text{valid metric}\rightarrow(S,I)
$$

improve comparison by making unlike systems answer the same logical questions without pretending their measures are numerically commensurable. This is an integration of prior methods, not a claimed methodological discovery.

## 35. Strongest skeptical interpretation

**Interpretation.** The matrix compares only eight systems selected through prior AFN and complexity programs; it does not represent every OEE category. It privileges evidence compatible with event-resolved AFN and S/I complexity analysis, while transformational novelty, semantic evolution, Types 2/3, and other categories remain unequally examined. Several R and provenance judgments depend on what the repository retained rather than a full replication audit.

Therefore “no broad exemplar” means no defensible candidate under the declared fixed comparison, not that every plausible artificial-OEE claim fails. The matrix's strongest inference is the component/broad distinction, not a field-wide prevalence estimate or sociology of consensus.

## 36. Central decision

**A — COMPONENT EXEMPLAR SET ESTABLISHED, BROAD EXEMPLAR ABSENT.**

Several model–measure–result packages are strong reusable component exemplars, but no broad artificial-OEE exemplar is defensible in the fixed set.

- **B is not selected:** no package reaches a full recognized OEE hallmark; the leading cases remain hallmark candidates.
- **C is not selected:** no candidate spans multiple strong OEE dimensions.
- **D is not selected:** validation dominates Stringmol's strongest AFN claim but not the other packages.
- **E is not selected:** evidence is not yet strong enough for consensus to be the sole or dominant missing element.
