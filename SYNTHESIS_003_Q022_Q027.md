# Synthesis 003 — Q022–Q027 Frontier-Family Consolidation

Date: 2026-09-08

Scope: exactly one consolidation pass over Q022–Q027 and the preceding repository state. This is not Q028. It introduces no hypothesis, implementation, simulation, or experiment.

## 1. Why consolidation is required

Q022–Q027 did not produce another surviving general OEE mechanism. They successively absorbed or dissolved every active mechanism family inherited from [Synthesis 002](SYNTHESIS_002_Q013_Q021.md), and Q027 also closed the conceptual continuity question generated by Q026. Continuing to subdivide the same families would now risk manufacturing novelty by terminology.

Three kinds of exhaustion must be separated.

- **Field exhaustion — REJECTED.** OEE is not explained merely because this repository eliminated several candidate explanations. The 2024 OEE special-issue editorial reports relatively strong agreement about behavioral hallmarks and distinctly weaker agreement about requirements or mechanisms that generate them. Recent artificial systems likewise do not supply a settled positive account of ongoing adaptive novelty. [Channon et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_e_00445)
- **Frontier-family exhaustion — ESTABLISHED FOR THE CURRENT MAP.** F1–F5 no longer contain a defensible unresolved *general mechanism* under their present formulations. Their remaining residues are domain theory, formalization, validation, or unavailable data.
- **Search-representation exhaustion — STRONGLY SUPPORTED.** Q011–Q027 concentrated on regime transitions, evolutionary units, identity, heredity, and continuity. That work removed real ambiguities, but it explored one conceptual neighborhood much more deeply than the field-level outcome that originally motivated the project: continued production of adaptively consequential new ways of living.

The correct diagnosis is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{frontier-family exhaustion}
+
\text{search-representation exhaustion}
\neq
\text{OEE-field exhaustion}
}
\]

The eliminations reinforce one another. Q022 prevents endogenous ecological change from serving as a residual catch-all. Q027 then shows that such a history remains scientifically continuous without a conserved hereditary or selectable bearer. Nothing in that chain explains OEE; it establishes what *cannot* carry the explanatory burden by itself.

## 4. What remains unsolved at field level

The field-level unknown remains substantial. Behavioral hallmarks include ongoing adaptive novelty, complexity-related growth, new evolutionary entities, major transitions, evolving evolvability, and semantic evolution; the 2024 editorial explicitly says agreement about generating mechanisms is weaker than agreement about these hallmarks. [Channon et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_e_00445) The MODES framework likewise separates novelty, change, ecology/diversity, and complexity instead of treating them as one outcome. [Dolson et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00280)

What is missing from the repository's map is not obviously another transition mechanism. It is a target-conditioned account of why artificial evolutionary systems so rarely continue producing **new adaptive functional relationships**, rather than merely new configurations, genotypes, morphologies, lineages, or levels. The repository has grounded function and relevance locally, distinguished arrival from establishment, and studied access to future adaptations. It has not directly organized the evidence for repeated origin and establishment of genuinely new adaptive functions, nor determined whether one causal explanation spans those cases.

This statement does not assert a mechanism gap. It identifies a field hallmark that the transition-heavy decomposition did not analyze directly enough.

The explanations for why the repository's candidate mechanisms collapsed while the field problem persists rank as follows:

1. **E — OEE is heterogeneous.** The literature treats it as several hallmarks and types; no evidence shows that they form one mechanistic natural kind.
2. **C — much of the unresolved problem is formalization and measurement.** “Novel,” “adaptive,” “functional,” and “ongoing” must be made comparable without letting observer labels decide the result.
3. **B — natural OEE-like behavior may arise from coupled established processes.** This is plausible, but “many mechanisms interact” has no explanatory content until a target effect and coupling structure are specified.
4. **D — artificial systems provide weak discriminating contrasts.** Strong systems establish different subsets of replication, heredity, activity, morphology, and novelty; few establish long-run adaptive functional innovation.
5. **A — a wholly omitted mechanism class exists.** This remains possible but currently has the least positive support.

## 5. Explanatory inventory

The repository already absorbs the following into established theory:

- ordinary Darwinian variation, heredity, differential reproduction, adaptation, and population/ecological dynamics;
- evolution of evolvability, modifier theory, robustness, modularity, facilitated variation, developmental bias, and changing genotype–phenotype maps;
- potentiation, cryptic variation, exaptation, key innovations, adjacent-possible expansion, and compositional reuse;
- niche construction, ecological inheritance, ecological opportunity, ecological fitting, ecosystem engineering, coevolution, and changing interaction/dependency networks;
- ecological scaffolding, multilevel selection, conflict mediation, endogenization, evolutionary hysteresis, life-cycle closure, and transitions in individuality;
- changes in inheritance and information-transmission systems, including non-hierarchical major transitions;
- exploratory, expansive, and transformational novelty as distinct descriptions, plus evolved open-endedness as an explanatory perspective;
- conditional evolutionary generalization and its no-free-lunch scope boundary;
- historical contingency, reticulate ancestry, and relational continuity through changes of hereditary, reproductive, or selectable units.

After these are acknowledged, the remaining degree of freedom is not “can evolution change its map, ecology, units, or mechanisms?” Each can change. The unorganized question is which combinations and histories yield a sustained stream of *adaptively consequential new functions*, and how that outcome should be distinguished from activity, exploration, and organizational turnover.

## 6. Hidden framing assumptions

### Transition focus

The project implicitly gave major transformations explanatory priority. That priority is not warranted as a general OEE thesis. Taylor et al. place major transitions among several OEE-relevant phenomena, not as the definition of all OEE. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210) Continuing adaptive novelty can in principle occur within a persistent organization, while a major transition can occur once and then close. Q026 established the latter point directly.

### Individuality focus

Questions about what reproduces, what is inherited, and which level is selected were necessary conceptual cleanup for collective and transition claims. Their durable OEE contribution is negative: no single unit, identity relation, or handoff model can define the whole phenomenon. They did not explain why new adaptive functions continue to arise. The unit-of-evolution problem became a local attractor after Q013.

### Mechanism singularity

The repeated question “what mechanism generates OEE?” assumed too readily that one privileged causal class should explain all hallmarks. A conjunction of known mechanisms is not a new mechanism, but neither must every complex historical outcome have one master cause. The repository should now condition causal questions on one declared hallmark before deciding whether singular, plural, or coupled explanation is appropriate.

## 7. Adaptive novelty audit

The repository has **not** answered:

> Why do artificial evolutionary systems so rarely continue generating genuinely new adaptive functional relationships?

It has instead established several prerequisites and firewalls:

- a novel configuration is not a novel function;
- persistence, complexity, semantic information, or a new dependency does not alone establish adaptive novelty;
- function must be grounded through selected effects, independently supported causal contribution to reproduction or persistence, ecological interaction, or an explicitly external task when that is the stated target;
- arrival, heritable transmission, adaptive consequence, population establishment, recurrence, and saturation are different observations;
- finite non-observation cannot by itself establish permanent closure.

The 2024 Tokyo Type 1 procedure is directly relevant because it combines adaptive novelty, accumulation of adaptive success, diversity, and complexity, but it remains a procedure for testing a declared OEE type rather than a sufficient causal mechanism. [Channon 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00430) Genelife is especially diagnostic: it reports open-ended genetic and spatial innovation while explicitly stating that the innovation falls short of functional biological innovation. [Packard & McCaskill 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00426)

The audit therefore classifies ongoing adaptive functional novelty as a **genuine field-level target that requires decomposition before mechanism search**. It is not already explained, not yet a new mechanism frontier, not reducible to Q002/Q003 state-space closure, and not known to be empirically blocked at the literature-audit stage.

## 8. Substrate audit

Substrate properties have been underrepresented relative to evolutionary transitions, but no standalone substrate mechanism survives this pass. Spatial locality, conservation, composability, persistent flows, multiscale organization, endogenous boundaries, and reusable interaction laws can change what organizations are physically stable or constructible. Q008 remains binding:

\[
\text{indefinite substrate capacity}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{evolutionary exploitation}.
\]

Flow-Lenia makes the point sharply. Mass conservation, local parameter fields, continuous morphology, and multispecies dynamics enable intrinsic evolutionary activity, but the reported activity components are parameter-defined species and the paper notes limits in identifying phenotypes and individuals. This is evidence that substrate design changes evolutionary affordances and measurement, not evidence of ongoing adaptive functional novelty or a missing substrate law. [Plantec et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00471)

Continuous self-organizing morphology therefore exposes a **coupled substrate–evolution design dimension**, not a distinct general OEE mechanism. It should be revisited only after the target adaptive function and evolutionary uptake criterion are declared; otherwise it collapses into scalability, accessibility, or generic self-organization.

## 9. Coupled-mechanism audit

Established processes plainly interact: eco-evolutionary feedback changes selection; development changes available variants; niche construction changes environments; multilevel selection changes conflict; evolving heredity changes transmission. Evolutionary systems theory, eco-evolutionary dynamics, evo-devo, niche construction, and major-transition theory already study many such couplings.

The residual cannot therefore be “all mechanisms interact.” A potentially useful coupled-system representation would track organismal states, environments, developmental mappings, hereditary relations, and interaction structure together. Yet “joint endogenous nonstationarity” is only a state description unless a particular coupling is shown to alter a declared OEE hallmark.

The same caution applies to timescales. Behavior, development, ecology, heredity, and regime change can occur on interacting timescales, but their ordering is not universal and cross-timescale coupling is already part of neighboring theories. No new mechanism is supplied by listing the timescales.

A coupled-dynamics view is most useful for distinguishing:

\[
\text{novelty generation}
\quad\text{from}\quad
\text{avoidance of effective closure}.
\]

Neutral wandering can avoid a fixed point without generating adaptive novelty; a long adaptive sequence can still enter an absorbing, recurrent, metastable, or stationary functional repertoire. These closure categories become scientific only after the relevant entities, functions, histories, and observation scale are fixed. Consequently, coupled-dynamics formalization is premature as the next move: it first needs a sharper hallmark-specific target.

## 10. OEE heterogeneity audit

The strongest current conclusion is methodological:

\[
\boxed{
\text{OEE is presently best treated as a family of dynamical hallmarks,}
\text{ not as one demonstrated mechanistic natural kind.}
}
\]

Taylor et al. explicitly defend plural perspectives; Taylor's later taxonomy separates exploratory, expansive, and transformational novelty; the Tokyo classification distinguishes adaptive-novelty/complexity, evolving evolvability, major transitions, and semantic evolution; and the 2024 editorial again describes multiple hallmark categories. [Taylor et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), [Taylor 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00290), [Channon et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_e_00445)

These categories can overlap, but their implications do not follow automatically. Complexity can rise without adaptive novelty; evolvability can change without a major transition; semantic change requires more than syntactic novelty; a major transition can end in closure. Searching for a sufficient mechanism common to all of them is therefore currently underjustified.

Semantic evolution remains a distinct named branch but is not selected here. Q004 already absorbed the general claim that semantics or relevance must be grounded in viability, intervention, organization, or selection history. Any remaining Type 4 question requires its own meaning/sign relation and evidence base; reopening it now would risk relabeling endogenous relevance. Origin-of-life transitions are likewise separate: the production of the first Darwinian process is not automatically an explanation of why an established Darwinian process remains open-ended.

## 11. Original ingredients audit

- **Evoloops — RETAIN AS BASELINE.** They establish self-reproduction, heritable variation, and intrinsic selection while showing that Darwinian evolution is weaker than OEE. Their unresolved plateau diagnosis is empirical. The 25-year review still identifies eventual dominance and the lack of generalizable principles preventing pseudo-equilibria as open problems. [Sayama & Nehaniv 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00451)
- **Lenia — SUPPORTING SUBSTRATE CONTRAST.** Lenia and Flow-Lenia show how continuous deformable morphology, mass flow, and locally embedded parameters alter the availability and observability of evolutionary entities. They do not reveal a dimension independent of substrate affordance, heredity, accessibility, and measurement.
- **Autopoiesis — SUPPORTING DOMAIN THEORY.** Organizational closure and autonomy can ground organism-relative maintenance and functional contribution. Q025 showed that they do not supply a distinct OEE mechanism. Autopoiesis should remain available for case-specific organization/function claims but leave the active frontier.
- **Deep learning — CURRENTLY EXHAUSTED.** Generalization, representation, compositionality, overparameterization, and continual adaptation have been absorbed respectively by conditional evolutionary generalization, genotype–phenotype/developmental maps, modular reuse, regulatory evolvability, and plasticity/evolving environments. Q024's no-free-lunch boundary blocks a generic transfer principle. No new ML-architecture search is warranted.

## 12. Recent model evidence audit

| System | Strongest demonstrated ingredient | What is not thereby demonstrated | Theoretical use here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evoloops | Constructive self-reproduction, heredity, intrinsic selection, within-size adaptation | Ongoing adaptive functional innovation or a proven general closure mechanism | Minimal Darwinian baseline |
| Genelife | Open-ended genetic and spatial innovation by activity statistics under reported conditions | Functional biological innovation; the authors explicitly mark this shortfall | Direct separation of pattern/genetic novelty from functional novelty [Packard & McCaskill 2024](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00426) |
| Flow-Lenia | Evolutionary activity in a mass-conservative continuous substrate with local inheritable parameters and multiple species | Independently grounded new adaptive functions, clear organism/phenotype identity, or OEE | Substrate and measurement contrast [Plantec et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00471) |
| Outlier CA | Spontaneous distributed self-replication, hierarchical structures, and causal branching ancestry | Heritable adaptive variation, selection among functions, or ongoing adaptive innovation | Shows how far replication and multiscale self-organization can remain below the target [Yang 2025](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00449), [Hintze & Bohm 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-026-00074-2) |
| Stringmol | Coevolution of genomic material, copier, and constructor; transitions between semantically closed organizations | Indefinitely repeated functional innovation or nonclosure | Strong fixed-substrate organizational-change case |
| DISHTINY | Evolved multilevel traits and several nested individuality transitions | Non-predefined hierarchy generation or ongoing adaptive functional novelty | Negative control for designer-supplied levels |

Across the systems, the varying ingredients include fixed versus locally evolvable parameters, discrete versus continuous morphology, conservation and resource flow, explicit versus emergent individuals, constructive versus rule-mediated reproduction, and supplied versus ecological selection. None of those differences alone identifies the missing causal dimension. The strongest shared gap is evidential: self-reproduction, novelty, and multiscale organization are not yet the same as a continuing series of newly established adaptive functions.

## 13. Candidate new frontiers

Only four candidates emerge from the global audit. “Candidate” here means a possible next target, not an active hypothesis or a surviving mechanism claim.

### Candidate 1

```text
Candidate: Ongoing adaptive functional novelty
Core question: Under what evidence can an artificial evolutionary history be said to keep originating and establishing new heritable causal contributions to persistence or reproduction, rather than only new configurations or refinements of existing functions?
Why not already F1–F5: The target is adaptive functional consequence, whether or not units, regimes, ecology, or evolvability change.
Closest prior art: selected-effect and organizational function; evolutionary innovation and exaptation; Bedau/MODES adaptive activity; Tokyo Type 1 testing; Genelife's explicit functional-innovation shortfall.
Strongest reason it may collapse: After functions and histories are fixed, the problem may decompose into established domain-specific innovation mechanisms plus validation, leaving no general causal residue.
Problem class: FORMALIZATION
Blocked by new data: NO for the first comparative audit; possibly YES for causal discrimination afterward.
Expected information gain: HIGH
```

### Candidate 2

```text
Candidate: Effective closure of an adaptive functional repertoire
Core question: How can entry into a fixed, recurrent, metastable, or stationary repertoire of adaptive functions be distinguished from long waiting times and neutral nonclosure in a coupled evolutionary system?
Why not already F1–F5: It classifies the long-run target dynamics rather than proposing ecological feedback, evolvability, plasticity, regime recurrence, or a new unit.
Closest prior art: activity statistics and MODES; counterfactual dynamical innovation; reachability and first-passage analysis; critiques of finite-run OEE claims.
Strongest reason it may collapse: Without a defensible functional ontology it restates Q002/Q003; with one, it may be only a model-specific measurement problem.
Problem class: FORMALIZATION
Blocked by new data: NO for theory; YES for diagnosing particular systems.
Expected information gain: MEDIUM-HIGH after Candidate 1
```

### Candidate 3

```text
Candidate: Substrate–evolution coupling for functional innovation
Core question: Which substrate constraints change the production and establishment of new adaptive functions after constructive capacity and ordinary Darwinian evolution are held fixed?
Why not already F1–F5: It concerns the causal coupling between physical/dynamical affordances and evolutionary uptake, not substrate size, general evolvability, or ecological feedback alone.
Closest prior art: universal construction and accessibility; artificial chemistries; spatially distributed self-reproduction; Flow-Lenia's conservation and local-parameter dynamics.
Strongest reason it may collapse: Capacity without uptake returns to Q008/F3, while model-specific substrate choices become implementation or comparative validation rather than a general mechanism.
Problem class: THEORETICAL
Blocked by new data: YES for discriminating causal substrate properties across current systems.
Expected information gain: MEDIUM
```

### Candidate 4

```text
Candidate: Coupling topology among established mechanisms
Core question: Which specific feedback or timescale couplings among development, heredity, ecology, and selection alter a declared OEE hallmark beyond the effects of their components?
Why not already F1–F5: The putative cause is an interaction effect among mechanism families, not another member of one family.
Closest prior art: eco-evolutionary dynamics, evo-devo, niche construction, multilevel selection, evolutionary systems theory, and evolved open-endedness.
Strongest reason it may collapse: Without a specified interaction effect it is the forbidden conjunction claim; with one, neighboring theory may already explain it.
Problem class: THEORETICAL
Blocked by new data: NO for targeted prior-art/formal work, but likely YES for general causal discrimination.
Expected information gain: MEDIUM-LOW until one hallmark is fixed
```

No separate candidates are retained for generic interaction novelty, nonstationarity, emergence, complexity, semantics, or origin of life. Each either fails the novelty firewall or requires a separate target decomposition.

## 14. Candidate-elimination test

| Candidate | Firewall result | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptive functional novelty | Does not reduce to F1–F5, because it asks about the target outcome rather than assuming transition, evolvability, plasticity, or ecology as its cause. | **SURVIVES ONLY AS A HALLMARK-DECOMPOSITION PROGRAM**, not yet as a mechanism frontier. |
| Effective functional closure | Distinct from F2 recurrence and F3 access only after adaptive function is fixed. | **DEFER UNTIL THE HALLMARK IS OPERATIONALIZED.** |
| Substrate–evolution coupling | Currently inherits Q008's capacity/accessibility divide and lacks matched causal evidence. | **NOT ACTIVE; THEORETICAL PROBE WITH EMPIRICAL BLOCK.** |
| Coupling topology | “Mechanisms interact” adds no content; a specified interaction may be ordinary eco-evo, evo-devo, NCT, or multilevel theory. | **NOT ACTIVE; REQUIRES A TARGET-SPECIFIC EFFECT FIRST.** |

Interaction novelty does not survive separately: molecular, ecological, behavioral, symbiotic, communicative, and regulatory relations are already treated by network evolution, exaptation, niche construction, ecological opportunity, and compositionality. Q022 covers the narrower case in which an interaction changes selection. Generic nonstationarity likewise fails: simultaneous change in \((GP,E,H,S)\) is a conjunction, not a cause. Complexity growth remains too metric-dependent and can occur without adaptation. Semantic evolution is not safely separable from Q004 without a declared sign/meaning relation.

Thus no materially new general mechanism question is ready for Q028.

## 15. Why `HYPOTHESES.md` remains empty

The empty file is evidence that the novelty firewall is doing useful work. The strongest explanations, in order, are:

1. proposed mechanisms repeatedly proved to be established prior art or conjunctions of it;
2. the target “OEE” remained too heterogeneous for one falsifiable dynamical claim;
3. several residuals were formalization or validation problems rather than causal gaps;
4. decisive contrasts for Fernandes, snowflake equivalence, Evoloop closure, and cross-system substrate effects require unavailable or unperformed empirical work;
5. some searches remained too high-level, especially with respect to adaptive functional novelty.

The correct response is to narrow the target, not loosen the hypothesis standard. No hypothesis is created or modified in this synthesis.

## 16. Implementation-policy impact

\[
\boxed{\textbf{SOMEWHAT}}
\]

Substantial literature and conceptual work remains: adaptive function can be scoped, existing evidence can be compared, and candidate systems can be separated by what they actually demonstrate. Stronger causal discrimination will eventually benefit from interventions, lineage data, or matched measurements, especially for functional closure and substrate effects. The current no-implementation policy therefore limits later validation but does not yet exhaust useful theoretical work. The policy is not changed here.

## 17. Research-scale decision

\[
\boxed{\textbf{B — HALLMARK DECOMPOSITION}}
\]

No general mechanism frontier survives the present audit. The next research program should evaluate **ongoing adaptive functional novelty** first.

This target is preferred because it is central to the 2024 field framing, closest to the original Evoloops dissatisfaction, stronger than neutral novelty, and more causally interpretable than generic complexity. It is also the exact dimension on which Genelife's authors mark a shortfall and which the other recent model systems do not independently establish. It is not selected because adaptive novelty is “the essence” of every OEE definition; it is selected because hallmark pluralism makes one target necessary, and this target offers the highest discriminating value.

The alternatives are rejected for scale reasons:

- **A** is premature: no genuinely new mechanism class survived.
- **C** is premature: a coupled state vector without a fixed target would repackage generic nonstationarity.
- **D** is too strong: the first comparative and definitional pass remains possible without new experiments.
- **E** is unnecessary: the main hallmark families are already sufficiently mapped to choose a target; a new global survey would diffuse the question again.
- **F** is false: the current map is exhausted, not the OEE research program.

This decision does not perform Q028.

## 18. Highest-information next research operation

> **Highest-information next research operation:** Construct a cross-system evidence matrix for ongoing adaptive functional novelty, using only independently grounded selected-effect, causal persistence/reproduction, ecological-interaction, or explicitly specified task-function claims, and record separately the origin, heritable transmission, adaptive consequence, ecological establishment, recurrence, and eventual saturation of each claimed function.

This is a change in research scale and representation, not Q028 and not a hypothesis. It is specified here but not conducted.

## 19. Stop condition for the next phase

Consolidate or change scale again as soon as **one** of the following occurs:

1. the evidence matrix yields at least two defensible positive/negative cross-system contrasts and a narrower causal question not absorbed by established innovation, ecology, evolvability, or measurement theory;
2. no function criterion survives cross-system use without observer-dependent relabeling, in which case classify the residue as definition/formalization rather than mechanism;
3. the decisive distinctions require new lineage, intervention, or long-run data, in which case mark the program empirical/instrumentationally blocked;
4. all claimed cases decompose into established domain-specific innovation mechanisms with no common causal remainder.

Do not continue by renaming adaptive novelty, nonstationarity, coupling, substrate richness, or anti-closure. Do not create a hypothesis until a falsifiable dynamical relation survives these tests.
