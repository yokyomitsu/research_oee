## 3. Definition of an original frontier

**Interpretation — admission rule.** An eligible direction combines a documented uncertainty, a controlled difference, an independently declared observable, and outcomes that change different inferences. Reproduction supplies calibration; originality must reside in the unresolved contrast or a consequential unresolved reproducibility claim. Different parameter values, attractive morphology, another metric, and a new implementation do not suffice.

The selected contrast changes the temporal persistence of a spatial survival opportunity. Its proposed contribution is an auditable comparison missing from the inspected reports, not discovery of disturbance, refuges, or ecological effects in evolution.

## 4. Frontier-selection objective

**Interpretation.** Optimize the probability of an original, scientifically interpretable result, including a precise null or reversal. Information gain must survive measurement error, censoring, reconstruction cost, confounding, and external dependencies. No numerical weights or probability estimates are assigned. A deeper theoretical target loses priority if its first observation would remain uninterpretable.

The decision is a qualitative ordering conditional on today's evidence. Selection uncertainty is not an O2 tie: no single unresolved fact would make the two finalists otherwise equivalent.

**Prospective question.** Can relaxation of a binding genome-capacity limit raise attained, established protein complexity while preserving the adaptive problem?

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| S | Aevol under the historical functional-protein complexity protocol. |
| Q | Does established `C_p` respond to a genuinely limiting external genome-length ceiling, with target and protein semantics unchanged? |
| I | Relax the maximum permitted genome length, only if it can be justified as binding in the relevant regime. |
| C | Same population, target, mutation operators/rates, selection, ancestry protocol, and observation opportunity; original ceiling. |
| Y | Historical `C_p`: distinct functional protein parameters under the original redundancy/tolerance convention, with the same lineage/establishment interpretation. Not genome length or coding DNA. |
| P | Repository `(S2,I0)`; prior mutation/population/genome studies do not directly answer this `C_p` contrast. |
| A | An inactive ceiling produces a tautological null; an artificially tiny ceiling creates a new limitation instead of explaining the historical one. Relaxation changes accepted mutation opportunities and genome costs. Population size would additionally change drift, mutation supply, and spatial competition. |
| R | Recover original metric and baseline protocol, establish capacity relevance, retain lineage/phenotype evidence, and fund multiple long evolutionary histories. |

**Fact.** [Aevol parameter documentation](https://www.aevol.fr/doc/parameters/) defines a maximum genome length and rejection of mutations exceeding it; the documented default is 10,000,000. Grid size also changes population and geography. Documentation does not show the ceiling binds the [Liard et al. complexity experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32027534/). Historical interpretation follows [Evidence Map 003](EVIDENCE_MAP_003_COMPLEXITY_STRONG_S_STRONG_I_SEARCH.md) and [Methodology 003](METHODOLOGY_003_COMPLEXITY_SUSTAINEDNESS_SCALABILITY.md).

**Outcome discrimination — Interpretation.** Positive: established `C_p` rises after relaxation of a demonstrably active ceiling, supporting a local capacity dependence and weakening ceiling-invariant attainment. Precise null: weakens a predeclared, finite-budget ceiling-limitation explanation only if the ceiling was active, the metric responsive, and uncertainty excludes a relevant effect; it cannot establish `I0` universally. Reversal: lower `C_p` would favor costs or changed mutational access over a simple monotone capacity account. An inactive-ceiling null teaches nothing about the historical apparent bound.

**Assessment.** Measurement **REUSABLE WITH MINOR ADAPTATION** after exact reconstruction; intervention **CAUSALLY MIXED BUT INTERPRETABLE**; censoring **MODERATE**, conditional on demonstrated exposure and precision; feasibility **UNCERTAIN**. Prior art **METHOD EXISTS, QUESTION OPEN**, with the binding-axis premise **UNKNOWN**. Retain as a conditional runner-up, not a justified experiment. Do not substitute a convenient population-size sweep.

## 10. Candidate C — Evoloops

**Prospective question.** Does the persistence of refuge location alter finite-horizon persistence of an incumbent dominant genotype under otherwise matched periodic clearing?

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| S | The published Evoloops rule system and block-clearing environment, with one fixed ancestral genotype and unchanged cell rules. |
| Q | Does a fixed refuge retain incumbent genotype dominance differently from an identically shaped refuge whose location changes on a declared schedule? |
| I | Change only whether the surviving block remains in one location across clearing events. |
| C | Fixed versus relocating block, same area, shape, clearing times, grid, boundary, founder, pre-treatment history, and census phase. Spatial origins must be balanced; neither condition targets genotype identity. |
| Y | Genotype–phenotype-resolved living-loop abundance, using the historical sequence-plus-dimensions identity. Primary summary: restricted incumbent-majority residence over a fixed census window; extinction is a separate terminal outcome. |
| P | Periodic disturbance, localized/distributed clearing, genotype-level selection, and rotating refuges are established prior art. A direct fixed-versus-relocating comparison was not found. |
| A | Differential realized killing, fragmentation, spatial sorting, collision-mediated mutation, and regrowth opportunities; clearing may create compulsory replacement without richer evolutionary possibilities. |
| R | Recover rules/ancestor and birth/death/genotype detector; validate detector under direct clearing; preserve schedule, initial state, counts, identity dictionary, failures, and audit snapshots; run a finite ensemble later if authorized. |

**Fact.** [Salzberg, Antony & Sayama 2004, §§2.1–2.2 and 3.3](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/BioSystems-preprint.pdf), a **peer-reviewed BioSystems article inspected through its author manuscript**, already compares localized and distributed removal and identifies species by genome and dimensions. Surviving locations alternate. [Salzberg & Sayama 2004, note 20](https://bingdev.binghamton.edu/sayama/papers/Complexity-preprint.pdf), a **peer-reviewed Complexity article**, explicitly motivates varying the surviving corner as preventing stagnation in a fixed refuge. Neither inspected report supplies the proposed fixed-location control. These are close antecedents, not discoveries of this pass.

**Frozen observable — Interpretation.** Let `g0` be the majority species identified before treatment, using full historical identity rather than size alone. At matched pre-clearing censuses, record living counts and whether `g0` exceeds half the living population. The primary residence summary is the fraction of the predeclared census window spent in that majority state. This is a finite population-dominance statistic derived from established abundance measurements, not a universal closure index. Observation horizon and census grid must be identical across arms and fixed in DESIGN.

Extinction occupies a separately coded terminal state; it must never be silently counted as successful escape from dominance. The joint record must distinguish loss of dominance in a living population, extinction, and unclassifiable observation. Missing detection is not absence. All assigned histories remain in the analysis; conditioning on survivors or stopping a run once the desired replacement occurs is inadmissible. Birth/death records must distinguish persistent sheltered individuals from regenerated descendants before attributing a result to evolutionary turnover.

**Outcome discrimination — Interpretation.** Positive: less incumbent residence with relocation, together with interpretable living-population dynamics, weakens invariance to refuge-location persistence under the specified schedule. It supports a total dependence of bounded dominance dynamics on the environmental policy. It does **not** distinguish a mortality pathway from altered competition or mutation supply. Precise null: if a relevant effect is excluded over an adequate predeclared recovery window, it weakens the claim that persistent refuge location makes a substantial contribution to incumbent persistence in this regime. Merely nonsignificant data are unresolved. Reversal: more incumbent residence after relocation weakens the proposed anti-stagnation direction and makes preferential recolonization/filtering a question for later work. Increased extinction is an adverse viability result, not evidence of open-endedness.

**Assessment.** Measurement **REUSABLE WITH MINOR ADAPTATION**; intervention **CAUSALLY MIXED BUT INTERPRETABLE** for total policy effect; censoring **MODERATE**; feasibility **PROBABLY FEASIBLE**. Exact prior art **APPARENTLY UNPERFORMED AFTER BOUNDED SEARCH**, against **CLOSE PRIOR ART**. Retain and select. The narrow inference, rather than a claim to prevent closure forever, makes finite outcomes discriminating.
